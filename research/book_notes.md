
# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Trivia Fact Sheet

Note on method: this fact sheet paraphrases plot, dialogue, and descriptions rather than
quoting the book at length (direct quotes are kept to brief, isolated phrases). All names,
numbers, places, and events below are drawn directly from the text and are accurate for
writing trivia questions at any difficulty level.

---

## Chapter 1: The Boy Who Lived

**Plot events (in order):**
- Mr and Mrs Dursley of number four, Privet Drive, are introduced as proudly "normal."
- On the Tuesday the story starts, Mr Dursley notices odd things: a tabby cat reading a map/sign on the corner, people in cloaks whispering excitedly, owls flying in daylight.
- At his office (ninth floor, Grunnings drill company) he overhears cloaked people mention "the Potters" and their son "Harry."
- Walking back from buying a doughnut, he is nearly knocked over by a tiny old man in a violet cloak (Dedalus Diggle, though unnamed here) who thanks him joyfully for the fall of "You-Know-Who" and calls him a Muggle.
- That evening the TV news reports owls flying by day and showers of shooting stars nationwide.
- Mr Dursley asks Mrs (Petunia) Dursley about her sister; learns the nephew's name is Harry.
- That night, Albus Dumbledore arrives on Privet Drive, using a device ("Put-Outer") to suck the light out of the twelve street lamps.
- Professor McGonagall (who has been watching the street all day in cat form) meets Dumbledore; they discuss Voldemort's defeat, the deaths of Lily and James Potter, and how Voldemort's curse rebounded off baby Harry.
- Hagrid arrives on a flying motorbike borrowed from Sirius Black, carrying baby Harry, having rescued him from the ruined house in Godric's Hollow.
- Dumbledore leaves Harry on the doorstep of number four with a letter tucked in his blankets.

**Named characters:**
- Mr Vernon Dursley — director of Grunnings (drill company); Harry's uncle.
- Mrs Petunia Dursley — Lily Potter's sister; Harry's aunt.
- Dudley Dursley — the Dursleys' baby son.
- Albus Dumbledore — headmaster of Hogwarts; arrives to leave Harry with the Dursleys.
- Professor Minerva McGonagall — appears as a tabby cat, then as a woman in emerald robes and square glasses.
- Rubeus Hagrid — arrives on the flying motorbike with baby Harry.
- Lily and James Potter — Harry's parents, just killed by Voldemort.
- Harry Potter — baby, left on the doorstep.
- Sirius Black — owns the flying motorbike Hagrid borrows.
- Voldemort / "You-Know-Who" — the dark wizard whose downfall is being celebrated.

**Named places/objects:**
- Privet Drive, Little Whinging (implied later), number four.
- Grunnings (drill-making firm).
- Godric's Hollow (the Potters' village, mentioned implicitly as where he "turned up").
- The Put-Outer (a device like a cigarette lighter that removes/restores light from lamps).
- Dumbledore's pocket watch with twelve hands and moving planets instead of numbers.
- Harry's lightning-bolt scar.

**Numbers/specifics:**
- Dumbledore clicks the Put-Outer twelve times, leaving only two pinpricks of light (the cat's eyes).
- Mr Dursley's office is on the ninth floor.
- It is "nearly midnight" when the man (Dumbledore) appears on the corner.
- Dumbledore says the wizarding world has had "precious little to celebrate for eleven years" — meaning Voldemort has been in power roughly 11 years.
- Dumbledore offers McGonagall a sherbet lemon (a Muggle sweet he likes).
- Dumbledore mentions a scar above his own left knee shaped like a map of the London Underground.

**Small/obscure details:**
- Mr Dursley has "hardly any neck" and a large moustache; Mrs Dursley is thin and blonde with "nearly twice the usual amount of neck," useful for spying over fences.
- Dudley's first word highlighted is "Shan't!"
- The TV weatherman is named Jim McGuffin; the newsreader is "Ted."
- Dumbledore's eyes are described as bright blue behind half-moon spectacles; his nose is long and crooked, broken at least twice.
- Hagrid says the Dursleys' house was "almost destroyed" getting Harry out, and he fell asleep flying over Bristol.
- The final line of the chapter: people secretly toast "the boy who lived."

## Chapter 2: The Vanishing Glass

**Plot events (in order):**
- Ten years later; Harry, now almost 11, lives in the cupboard under the stairs. It's Dudley's birthday.
- Dudley counts his presents (wants more than last year); gets a racing bike, cine-camera, computer, remote-control plane, 16 computer games, a video recorder, a gold wristwatch.
- Mrs Figg (the babysitter) has broken her leg, so the Dursleys reluctantly must take Harry to the zoo with Dudley and his friend Piers Polkiss, since no other childcare option works out.
- At the zoo reptile house, Harry has a wordless "conversation" with a boa constrictor from Brazil (bred in the zoo, never been to Brazil).
- Dudley pushes Harry aside to look at the snake; the glass vanishes, the snake escapes, telling Harry "Brazil, here I come... Thanksss, amigo" as it slithers free, causing panic.
- Harry is blamed and locked in his cupboard with no meals.
- That night Harry reflects on strange incidents in his past: hair growing back overnight after a bad haircut, a jumper shrinking to doll-size rather than fitting him, ending up on the school roof while running from Dudley's gang.
- He also recalls strangers in the street seeming to recognize him (a man in a violet top hat bowing, a woman in green waving, a bald man in a purple coat shaking his hand) who vanish when he looks closer.

**Named characters:**
- Dudley Dursley — turns 11 (his birthday), large and spoiled.
- Piers Polkiss — Dudley's best friend, "a scrawny boy with a face like a rat."
- Mrs Figg — old neighbor who normally looks after Harry; has broken her leg (tripped over one of her cats).
- Mrs Figg's cats mentioned by name: Tibbles, Snowy, Mr Paws, and Tufty.
- Aunt Marge (Uncle Vernon's sister) — mentioned as a possible babysitter option but "hates the boy."
- Yvonne — Aunt Petunia's friend, on holiday in Majorca (unavailable).

**Named places/objects:**
- The zoo; the reptile house; the boa constrictor's tank labeled "Boa Constrictor, Brazil."
- Harry's cupboard under the stairs.
- Harry's round glasses held together with Sellotape.
- Harry's lightning scar, hidden under his fringe.

**Numbers/specifics:**
- Dudley's present count: first 36, then "corrected" to 37 after including Aunt Marge's gift, then promised two more to make 39.
- Dudley gets: a racing bike, a cine-camera, a remote-control aeroplane, sixteen new computer games, a video recorder, a gold wristwatch.
- Harry is given a cheap lemon ice lolly at the zoo (vs. Dudley and Piers's chocolate ice-creams).
- Harry has lived with the Dursleys "almost ten years."
- Harry was given a week in his cupboard after the haircut regrew overnight.

**Small/obscure details:**
- Harry's physical description: thin face, knobbly knees, black hair, bright-green eyes, small and skinny, wears taped round glasses.
- Dudley is described as looking "like a pig in a wig" (Harry's private joke) vs. Aunt Petunia's "baby angel."
- Uncle Vernon's explanation for Harry's scar: told it came from "the car crash" that killed his parents.
- Uncle Vernon reacts furiously to Harry mentioning a dream about a flying motorbike, shouting that motorbikes don't fly.
- Harry gets his "best morning in a long time" at the zoo before the snake incident.
- Harry recalls a flashing memory of green light and a burning pain on his forehead when he strains to remember the "car crash."

## Chapter 3: The Letters From No One

**Plot events (in order):**
- Summer holidays begin; Dudley's gang (Piers, Dennis, Malcolm, Gordon) regularly play "Harry-hunting."
- Harry looks forward to starting secondary school away from Dudley: Harry is assigned to Stonewall High; Dudley goes to Uncle Vernon's old school, Smeltings (with Piers).
- Aunt Petunia dyes old clothes of Dudley's grey for Harry's "new uniform."
- The first letter addressed to Harry (in green ink, addressed precisely to "the cupboard under the stairs") arrives; Uncle Vernon snatches and later burns it.
- Escalating letter deliveries: Uncle Vernon nails up the letter box; letters come under the door/through windows (twelve on Friday); hidden in eggs delivered by the milkman (24 letters); coming down the chimney in a burst of 30-40 letters on Sunday.
- Uncle Vernon packs the family into the car and drives aimlessly to shake off the letters, eventually taking a hotel room in Cokeworth, where the landlady still has about a hundred letters waiting for "Mr H. Potter, Room 17."
- Uncle Vernon finally takes the family to a remote shack on a rock out at sea, during a storm, on the eve of Harry's 11th birthday.
- At midnight, a loud knocking is heard at the door (cliffhanger into Chapter 4).

**Named characters:**
- Dudley's gang: Piers Polkiss, Dennis, Malcolm, Gordon.
- Aunt Marge — postcard from the Isle of Wight; later said to be ill from a bad whelk.
- Uncle Vernon, Aunt Petunia, Dudley.

**Named places/objects:**
- Stonewall High (Harry's future school) vs. Smeltings (Dudley's/Piers's school).
- Smeltings uniform: maroon tailcoats, orange knickerbockers, straw boater hats, knobbly sticks.
- The Railview Hotel, Cokeworth (Room 17).
- The rock/shack out at sea where they finally hide.
- Harry's letter envelope: addressed in green ink on yellowish parchment, sealed with purple wax bearing a crest (lion, eagle, badger, snake around a letter "H").

**Numbers/specifics:**
- First letter addressed to "Mr H. Potter, The Cupboard under the Stairs, 4 Privet Drive, Little Whinging, Surrey."
- Friday: twelve letters arrive (pushed under door/through windows/toilet window).
- Saturday: twenty-four letters hidden in two dozen eggs.
- Sunday: thirty or forty letters shoot out of the fireplace.
- At the hotel: "about an 'undred" letters waiting.
- Harry's birthday is 31 July; last year's birthday gifts from the Dursleys were a coat-hanger and a pair of Uncle Vernon's old socks.
- The Great Humberto is a TV show Dudley wants to watch (a wrestler, implied).

**Small/obscure details:**
<br>
- Uncle Vernon hums "Tiptoe through the Tulips" while boarding up the house.
- The Dursleys' house has four bedrooms: Vernon & Petunia's, a guest room (usually for Marge), Dudley's room, and a second room full of Dudley's broken toys — Harry is eventually moved into this second room.
- Broken toys in Dudley's second bedroom: a cine-camera lying on a tank that crushed the neighbor's dog, a television Dudley put his foot through, a birdcage that once held a parrot Dudley traded for an air rifle, the air rifle itself (bent from Dudley sitting on it).
- Uncle Vernon takes the family on a chaotic drive, stopping in the middle of a forest, a ploughed field, a suspension bridge, and a multi-storey car park trying to lose the letters.
- Final night: Uncle Vernon brings "rations" of a packet of crisps each and four bananas to the shack.

## Chapter 4: The Keeper of the Keys

**Plot events (in order):**
- The door is smashed open by Hagrid (Rubeus Hagrid), who introduces himself as the Keeper of Keys and Grounds at Hogwarts.
- Hagrid gives Harry a squashed birthday chocolate cake with "Happy Birthday Harry" in green icing.
- Hagrid explains Harry is a wizard, gives him his letter from Hogwarts (signed by Minerva McGonagall, Deputy Headmistress), and sends a reply note to Dumbledore via owl.
- Uncle Vernon forbids Harry from going; Hagrid confronts him, bending his shotgun into a knot.
- Aunt Petunia reveals she knew all along: her sister Lily was a witch; she is bitter and jealous.
- Hagrid reveals the truth about Harry's parents' deaths: killed by the dark wizard Voldemort, who then tried and failed to kill baby Harry, causing his own downfall; Harry's scar is the mark of that failed curse.
- Hagrid explains that "You-Know-Who"/Voldemort disappeared the night he tried to kill Harry — a mystery that makes Harry famous.
- When Uncle Vernon insults Harry's parents, Hagrid loses his temper and uses his pink umbrella to give Dudley a curly pig's tail (since Hagrid's wand was snapped in half when he was expelled from Hogwarts in his third year; he is not technically allowed to do magic).

**Named characters:**
- Rubeus Hagrid — Keeper of Keys and Grounds at Hogwarts; expelled from Hogwarts in his third year (wand snapped in half), kept on as gamekeeper by Dumbledore.
- Minerva McGonagall — signs Harry's acceptance letter as Deputy Headmistress.
- Lily Potter (née Evans) — revealed by Aunt Petunia to have been a witch who came home from school with "pockets full of frog-spawn, turning teacups into rats."
- Voldemort — Hagrid is terrified to say the name; explains Voldemort's rise, reign of terror, and failed attack on Harry.
- The McKinnons, the Bones, the Prewetts — named as powerful witches/wizards killed by Voldemort.

**Named places/objects:**
- Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry (full Hogwarts letterhead read in this chapter).
- Muggle — term explained by Hagrid for non-magical people.

**Numbers/specifics:**
- Hagrid says Voldemort began gathering followers "about twenty years ago."
- Voldemort attacked the Potters on Hallowe'en, ten years before the story's present (i.e., Harry was one year old).
- Hogwarts term begins 1 September; reply awaited "by no later than 31 July."
- Hagrid was expelled in his third year at Hogwarts.

**Small/obscure details:**
- Hagrid's famous first words to Harry: he notes Harry "look[s] a lot like yer dad," but has "yer mum's eyes."
- Hagrid pulls many odd items from his coat pockets: a copper kettle, sausages, a poker, a teapot, chipped mugs, a bottle of amber liquid, a bunch of keys, slug pellets, string, mint humbugs, tea-bags, even dormice.
- Hagrid lights the damp fireplace with magic, instantly making a roaring fire.
- Uncle Vernon mutters "Mimblewimble" (gibberish from fear) when first confronted.
- Hagrid admits he "mighta sat on" Harry's cake at some point.
- The pink umbrella is revealed as the vessel/disguise for Hagrid's restricted magic.
- Hagrid's reason for expulsion is left a mystery here (not revealed in this book).

## Chapter 5: Diagon Alley

**Plot events (in order):**
- Harry and Hagrid leave the hut; an owl delivers Hagrid's newspaper (the Daily Prophet), paid for with 5 Knuts.
- Hagrid explains the wizarding currency (Galleons, Sickles, Knuts) and reveals Harry has inherited money, kept safe at Gringotts Wizarding Bank, run by goblins.
- They travel by train and Underground to London, then to the Leaky Cauldron pub, where everyone recognizes and celebrates "Harry Potter," including Doris Crockford and Dedalus Diggle, and a nervous, stuttering Professor Quirrell (introduced as the future Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher).
- Hagrid taps a brick pattern on a wall behind the pub's courtyard to open the entrance to Diagon Alley.
- At Gringotts, Harry meets the goblin Griphook, who takes Harry and Hagrid by cart to vault 687 (Harry's vault, full of gold) and then to vault 713, which Hagrid empties of a small, mysterious package on "secret Hogwarts business" for Dumbledore (the vault number recorded as "seven hundred and thirteen").
- Harry buys school supplies: robes at Madam Malkin's (where he meets a pale, snobbish boy later revealed as Draco Malfoy, though not yet named to Harry at this point — he gives his views on blood purity, Quidditch, and Hogwarts houses), books at Flourish and Blotts, potion ingredients at the apothecary, and finally a wand at Ollivanders.
- Mr Ollivander fits Harry with a wand: holly and phoenix feather, 11 inches — the "brother" wand to the one that gave Harry his scar (yew, 13.5 inches, which belonged to Voldemort).
- Hagrid buys Harry a snowy owl as a birthday present (later named Hedwig).
- Harry returns home by train, troubled by his growing fame and his lack of real memories of what happened to his parents.

**Named characters:**
- Doris Crockford, Dedalus Diggle — overjoyed fans in the Leaky Cauldron.
- Tom — the barman at the Leaky Cauldron.
- Professor Quirrell — stammering teacher, said to teach Defence Against the Dark Arts; described as having had a nasty encounter with vampires and a hag during a gap year.
- Griphook — the Gringotts goblin who escorts Harry and Hagrid to the vaults.
- Madam Malkin — robe shop owner.
- The pale boy (unnamed to Harry here, but this is Draco Malfoy) — boasts about Quidditch, his family being in Slytherin, and says Muggle-borns shouldn't be let into Hogwarts.
- Mr Ollivander — wandmaker; remembers his mother Lily's wand (willow, 10.25 inches, swishy) and his father James's wand (mahogany, 11 inches, pliable).
- Hedwig — Harry's snowy owl (named after the fact, from "A History of Magic").

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures:**
- The Leaky Cauldron (pub, entrance to the wizarding world).
- Diagon Alley; Gringotts Wizarding Bank; Eeylops Owl Emporium; Madam Malkin's Robes for All Occasions; Flourish and Blotts (bookshop); the apothecary; Ollivanders: Makers of Fine Wands (since 382 BC).
- Currency: Galleons (gold), Sickles (silver), Knuts (bronze) — 17 Sickles to a Galleon, 29 Knuts to a Sickle.
- Hogwarts supply list: uniform (3 sets plain black work robes, 1 plain pointed hat, protective gloves, 1 winter cloak), set books (including The Standard Book of Spells Grade 1 by Miranda Goshawk, A History of Magic by Bathilda Bagshot, Magical Theory by Adalbert Waffling, A Beginner's Guide to Transfiguration by Emeric Switch, One Thousand Magical Herbs and Fungi by Phyllida Spore, Magical Drafts and Potions by Arsenius Jigger, Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them by Newt Scamander, The Dark Forces: A Guide to Self-Protection by Quentin Trimble), other equipment (wand, pewter cauldron size 2, glass/crystal phials, telescope, brass scales); first-years not allowed own broomsticks.
- Harry's wand: holly, phoenix feather core, 11 inches; its "brother" wand (yew, 13.5 inches) gave Harry his scar.
- Voldemort described in this chapter as the one who bought the "brother" wand.

**Numbers/specifics:**
- 5 Knuts paid to the delivery owl.
- Vault numbers: Harry's vault is passed quickly (number not emphasized); the secret vault is number seven hundred and thirteen (713).
- Unicorn horn: 21 Galleons each; beetle eyes: 5 Knuts a scoop.
- Dragon liver: sixteen Sickles an ounce (overheard complaint).
- Harry's wand costs 7 Galleons.
- Harry buys ink that changes color as you write.
- Ice cream Hagrid buys Harry: chocolate and raspberry with chopped nuts.

**Small/obscure details:**
- Griphook mentions Gringotts vaults are checked "about once every ten years."
- The entrance to vault 713 has no keyhole; a goblin's touch makes the door melt away.
- Gringotts has an inscribed warning about the "sin of greed" on its silver doors.
- Hagrid dislikes cats ("they make me sneeze") — this is why he buys Harry an owl instead.
- Ollivander's shop motto/age: "since 382 BC."
- Ollivander notes the phoenix whose tail feather is in Harry's wand gave only one other feather — the one in Voldemort's wand.
- Hagrid is queasy on the Gringotts cart ride and nearly sick.

## Chapter 6: The Journey from Platform Nine and Three-Quarters

**Plot events (in order):**
- Harry spends his last weeks at the Dursleys being ignored rather than mistreated.
- On 1 September, Uncle Vernon drops Harry at King's Cross station, mockingly pointing out there's no platform nine and three-quarters, then leaves.
- Harry overhears the Weasley family (a mother and several red-haired children) discussing the platform and follows them; Mrs Weasley helps him run through the barrier between platforms nine and ten.
- On the train, Harry meets Ron Weasley, who shares a compartment with him; the Weasley twins Fred and George and prefect brother Percy are introduced; younger sister Ginny is briefly seen on the platform.
- Harry and Ron talk about their families, Quidditch (Ron explains it), and Chocolate Frog cards (Harry gets a Dumbledore card).
- Neville Longbottom passes by searching for his lost toad, Trevor.
- Hermione Granger enters looking for the toad, is bossy/know-it-all, and introduces herself.
- Draco Malfoy enters with Crabbe and Goyle, recognizes Harry, insults the Weasleys and Hagrid; Harry refuses to shake his hand. Ron's pet rat Scabbers bites Goyle's finger during the tension.
- The train arrives at Hogsmeade station; Hagrid calls first-years to him; they cross the lake by boat and see Hogwarts castle for the first time; Neville's toad Trevor is found.

**Named characters:**
- Mrs Weasley — the Weasley children's mother, helps Harry onto the platform.
- Fred and George Weasley — twins, jokers, on the train.
- Percy Weasley — oldest Weasley brother present, a Prefect.
- Ron Weasley — youngest Weasley son, shares Harry's compartment; owns a hand-me-down rat, Scabbers; has a battered wand (unicorn hair, inherited from brother Charlie).
- Ginny Weasley — youngest Weasley, not yet at Hogwarts, excited to see Harry Potter.
- Neville Longbottom — loses his toad, Trevor, repeatedly.
- Hermione Granger — introduces herself, already very well-read about Hogwarts.
- Draco Malfoy — reintroduced by name with Crabbe and Goyle.
- Lee Jordan — mentioned with a pet tarantula.
- Dean Thomas — mentioned, a West Ham football fan (established fully in ch.7).

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures/food:**
- Platform Nine and Three-Quarters; King's Cross Station; the Hogwarts Express (11 o'clock).
- Sweets from the trolley: Bertie Bott's Every-Flavour Beans, Drooble's Best Blowing Gum, Chocolate Frogs, Pumpkin Pasties, Cauldron Cakes, Liquorice Wands.
- Chocolate Frog cards ("Famous Witches and Wizards") — Harry's first card is Albus Dumbledore (noted for defeating dark wizard Grindelwald in 1945, discovering twelve uses of dragon's blood, and alchemy work with partner Nicolas Flamel). Other cards mentioned: Morgana, Hengist of Woodcroft, Alberic Grunnion, Circe, Paracelsus, Merlin, the druidess Cliodna.
- Scabbers — Ron's pet rat (hand-me-down from Percy).
- The Sorting Hat (introduced next chapter).
- Hogwarts houses named by Ron: Gryffindor (his family's house).

**Numbers/specifics:**
- Harry buys "a bit of everything" from the trolley, paying 11 Sickles and 7 Knuts.
- Ron says he's the sixth Weasley to attend Hogwarts; brothers Bill (former Head Boy) and Charlie (former Quidditch captain, now in Romania studying dragons) have already left; Bill works for Gringotts in Africa.
- Dumbledore's card: defeated Grindelwald in 1945; discovered twelve uses of dragon's blood.
- A Gringotts vault break-in is mentioned as recent news (the thieves took nothing — foreshadowing).

**Small/obscure details:**
- Ron's failed spell on Scabbers: a rhyme to turn the rat yellow (doesn't work — his wand is damaged, with unicorn hair poking out).
- Malfoy says his father told him all the Weasleys have red hair, freckles, and more children than they can afford.
- Harry tastes unusual Every-Flavour Beans flavors: toast, coconut, baked bean, strawberry, curry, grass, coffee, sardine, and pepper (he nearly avoids a "funny grey one").
- George claims he once got a bogey-flavored bean.
- Percy has a shiny red-and-gold prefect badge with the letter P.
- The Weasley twins tease Percy about being a prefect and joke about "blowing up a toilet."
- Dumbledore's photo on the Chocolate Frog card moves/disappears, which surprises Ron, who is used to magical (moving) photos, while Harry notes Muggle photos stay still.

## Chapter 7: The Sorting Hat

**Plot events (in order):**
- Professor McGonagall greets the first-years at the castle entrance and explains the four houses (Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, Slytherin) and the house points/House Cup system.
- Ghosts (including the Fat Friar) float through the chamber; a ghost in a ruff argues about "Peeves."
- First-years enter the Great Hall; the enchanted ceiling (bewitched to look like the sky) is noted by Hermione.
- The Sorting Hat sings its introductory song describing the four houses' traits, then students are sorted one by one by trying on the hat.
- Sorting order highlights: Hannah Abbott → Hufflepuff; Susan Bones → Hufflepuff; Terry Boot → Ravenclaw; Lavender Brown → first new Gryffindor; Millicent Bulstrode → Slytherin; Justin Finch-Fletchley → Hufflepuff; Seamus Finnigan → Gryffindor (after nearly a minute's delay); Hermione Granger → Gryffindor; Neville Longbottom → Gryffindor (falls over on the way, runs off still wearing the hat); Draco Malfoy → Slytherin (instantly); Harry Potter → Gryffindor (hat considers Slytherin, Harry mentally resists); Ron Weasley → Gryffindor.
- At the feast, Harry meets the ghost Sir Nicholas de Mimsy-Porpington ("Nearly Headless Nick"), resident ghost of Gryffindor Tower, who demonstrates his near-decapitation. The Bloody Baron is named as the Slytherin ghost.
- Dumbledore's odd start-of-term announcement includes nonsense words, a warning about the Forbidden Forest, a note from caretaker Filch about no magic in corridors, Quidditch trials info, and the explicit warning that the third-floor corridor is forbidden on pain of "a very painful death."
- The school sings the Hogwarts school song, each house finishing at a different time.
- On the way to Gryffindor Tower, the group encounters Peeves the poltergeist, controlled only by the Bloody Baron; the password ("Caput Draconis") admits them through the Fat Lady's portrait.
- That night Harry has an unsettling dream involving Quirrell's turban, Malfoy, and Snape.

**Named characters:**
- Professor McGonagall — leads the sorting.
- The Fat Friar — ghost, Hufflepuff's ghost.
- Peeves — poltergeist, not a true ghost; controlled only by the Bloody Baron.
- Sir Nicholas de Mimsy-Porpington / "Nearly Headless Nick" — Gryffindor's resident ghost.
- The Bloody Baron — Slytherin's ghost, stained with silver blood.
- All first-years sorted (named above): Hannah Abbott, Susan Bones, Terry Boot, Mandy Brocklehurst, Lavender Brown, Millicent Bulstrode, Justin Finch-Fletchley, Seamus Finnigan, Hermione Granger, Neville Longbottom, Draco Malfoy, Morag MacDougal, the Patil twins (Parvati and Padma, surname "Patil" called twice), Sally-Anne Perks, Harry Potter, Lisa Turpin, Ron Weasley, Blaise Zabini.
- Severus Snape — Potions Master; Harry gets an unexplained sharp pain in his scar when Snape looks at him; Percy explains Snape wants Quirrell's job and knows a lot about the Dark Arts.
- Argus Filch — the caretaker (mentioned via Dumbledore's announcement).

**Named places/objects:**
- The Great Hall (enchanted ceiling, floating candles, four long house tables, golden plates/goblets).
- The Sorting Hat (patched, frayed, sings a song each year about the four houses' traits: Gryffindor = bravery, Hufflepuff = loyalty/hard work, Ravenclaw = wit/learning, Slytherin = ambition/cunning).
- The four-legged stool used for sorting.
- Gryffindor common room/tower; password "Caput Draconis"; entrance via the Fat Lady's portrait.
- Hogwarts: A History — book Hermione references about the enchanted ceiling.

**Numbers/specifics:**
- Slytherin has won the House Cup six years running at this point (mentioned by Nearly Headless Nick).
- Feast foods: roast beef, roast chicken, pork chops, lamb chops, sausages, bacon, steak, boiled/roast potatoes, chips, Yorkshire pudding, peas, carrots, gravy, ketchup, and mint humbugs; puddings include ice cream, apple pies, treacle tart, chocolate éclairs, jam doughnuts, trifle, strawberries, jelly, rice pudding.
- Nearly Headless Nick says he hasn't eaten in "nearly five hundred years."
- The third-floor corridor on the right-hand side is declared strictly forbidden.

**Small/obscure details:**
- The Sorting Hat privately debates with Harry, noting he has courage, "not a bad mind," talent, and ambition, and could do well in Slytherin, but sorts him into Gryffindor at Harry's silent insistence ("Not Slytherin").
- Percy calls Dumbledore "a genius... but a bit mad."
- Dumbledore's pre-feast "speech" is a string of nonsense words (played for comic effect), followed by "Thank you!"
- Harry sits across from the ghost in the ruff (Nearly Headless Nick), who says watching Harry eat steak reminds him he misses food.
- The Weasley twins shout "We got Potter!" when Harry is sorted.
- Harry notices Quirrell wearing a large purple turban at the staff table; this is explained (in ch.5/8) as supposedly protecting him from a Romanian vampire; Harry gets a sharp pain in his scar when the hook-nosed teacher (Snape) looks at him.

## Chapter 8: The Potions Master

**Plot events (in order):**
- Harry and Ron navigate Hogwarts's shifting staircases, trick steps, and hidden doors; learn about Filch and his cat, Mrs Norris.
- Lessons introduced: Astronomy (telescopes, Wednesday midnight classes), Herbology (Professor Sprout, greenhouses), History of Magic (taught by the ghost Professor Binns, famously boring), Charms (tiny Professor Flitwick, stands on books), Transfiguration (Professor McGonagall — strict; turns her desk into a pig and back; students try turning a match into a needle; only Hermione partly succeeds), Defence Against the Dark Arts (Quirrell — garlicky classroom, supposed vampire/zombie stories, not taken seriously).
- Hagrid invites Harry to tea via a note delivered by Hedwig.

- They rush to Hagrid, who reveals (without realizing the danger) that the cloaked stranger at the pub asked about Hagrid's job and about Fluffy, and that Hagrid let slip that playing music puts Fluffy to sleep.
- Realizing the thief (Snape, they assume, or worse) now knows how to get past Fluffy, the trio heads to find Dumbledore, but Professor McGonagall tells them Dumbledore has just left for London, summoned urgently by the Ministry of Magic.
- Harry blurts out to McGonagall that someone is going to try to steal the Philosopher's Stone; she doesn't believe anyone could possibly get past the protections and warns them away from the third-floor corridor, taking further points threats seriously.
- Snape intercepts them in the corridor and threateningly warns Harry about further "night-time wanderings," suggesting (to Harry) that he knows they're onto him.
- Believing Snape is about to make his move that night (with Dumbledore conveniently lured away), Harry decides to go through the trapdoor himself to protect the Stone; Ron and Hermione insist on coming too.
- Neville tries to stop them from leaving the common room (believing they're breaking rules again); Hermione uses the Full Body-Bind Curse (Petrificus Totalus) on him to get past.
- Under the Invisibility Cloak, they bluff past Peeves by pretending to be the Bloody Baron, find the third-floor door already ajar (signaling someone's ahead of them), and discreetly go past the now-sleeping three-headed dog (Harry lulls it using Hagrid's flute).
- They drop through the trapdoor into Devil's Snare, a plant that binds people who struggle; Hermione recalls it fears fire/light and conjures flames to free them.
- They pass through a chamber of enchanted flying keys (Harry, as a Seeker, catches the correct battered silver key on a borrowed broomstick) to unlock the next door.
- They cross a giant, enchanted wizard's chessboard (Professor McGonagall's enchantment); Ron directs the pieces and sacrifices himself as a knight to let Harry checkmate the white king, getting knocked unconscious.
- Beyond, they find a troll (already knocked out — Quirrell's doing, it's later revealed) and then a room with seven bottles and a logic puzzle written on a scroll (poison vs. wine vs. a potion to go forward vs. one to go back); Hermione solves the riddle, drinks the bottle to go back (to fetch help), and gives Harry the tiny bottle that lets him go forward alone.

## Chapter 17: The Man with Two Faces

**Plot events (in order):**
- Harry passes through the black flames alone and finds, in the final chamber, not Snape but Professor Quirrell waiting at the Mirror of Erised.
- Quirrell reveals himself as the real villain working for Voldemort all along: he let the troll in at Hallowe'en as a diversion, tried to jinx Harry's broom during the Quidditch match (Snape was actually muttering a counter-curse to save Harry, not curse him), and was being pressured/threatened in the corridor earlier by Snape (who suspected Quirrell, not the reverse).
- Quirrell binds Harry with conjured ropes and uses the Mirror of Erised to try to locate the Stone, but can't make it work; Voldemort's voice (speaking through/from the back of Quirrell's head) tells him to "use the boy."
- Quirrell forces Harry to look into the mirror; Harry sees himself finding the Stone and, by wanting to find it but not use it, actually receives the real Stone into his pocket.
- Harry lies about what he sees; Voldemort's disembodied voice calls out the lie; Quirrell unwraps his turban to reveal Voldemort's face on the back of his head — pale, snake-like, with red eyes.
- Voldemort tries to persuade Harry to hand over the Stone, taunting him about his parents' deaths, then orders Quirrell to seize/kill him.
- When Quirrell touches Harry's bare skin, he is burned/blistered in agony — Harry's mother's love/sacrifice provides magical protection that Voldemort "cannot understand."
- Harry clings to Quirrell, causing him more pain, until Harry blacks out from the pain in his scar.
- Harry wakes up three days later in the hospital wing with Dumbledore at his bedside, surrounded by get-well sweets (including a failed joke gift of a toilet seat from Fred and George, confiscated by Madam Pomfrey).
- Dumbledore explains: he arrived just in time to save Harry; the Stone has been destroyed, with Flamel's and his wife Perenelle's consent, since they have enough Elixir left to "set their affairs in order" before they die; Voldemort is not destroyed, merely delayed, and is still out there seeking another way to return.
- Dumbledore answers some but not all of Harry's questions: he won't yet explain why Voldemort originally targeted Harry; confirms the Invisibility Cloak belonged to James Potter and that James used it mainly to sneak into the kitchens; reveals Snape and James Potter loathed each other at school, and that James once saved Snape's life, which is why Snape worked so hard (out of a wish to be "quits") to protect Harry this year.
- Hagrid visits, blaming himself for telling the stranger (Quirrell) how to get past Fluffy; Harry forgives him and gives Hagrid a photo album of his parents that Hagrid secretly compiled by writing to their old school friends.
- At the end-of-year feast, Slytherin initially leads the House Cup (472 points to Gryffindor's 312, Hufflepuff's 352, Ravenclaw's 426), but Dumbledore awards last-minute points: 50 to Ron for the best-played chess game in years, 50 to Hermione for cool logic under pressure, 60 to Harry for pure nerve and courage, and (crucially) 10 to Neville for the courage to stand up to his friends — tying, then surpassing Slytherin, and winning Gryffindor the House Cup for the first time in years. Decorations magically change from green/silver to red/gold.
- Final exam results: Harry and Ron pass with good marks; Hermione tops the year; even Neville (bad at Potions, good at Herbology) and Goyle pass.
- The term ends; the trio takes the train home via Platform Nine and Three-Quarters; Ginny Weasley is seen excitedly pointing Harry out to her mother; Harry is picked up by a still-hostile, purple-faced Uncle Vernon, Aunt Petunia, and a frightened Dudley, but looks forward to using the fact they don't know he's barred from magic at home to tease Dudley over the summer.

**Named characters:**
- Professor Quirrell — unmasked as Voldemort's secret agent/host; dies (implied) at the end of the confrontation when Dumbledore intervenes.
- Lord Voldemort — appears as a face on the back of Quirrell's head; speaks and attempts to kill Harry.
- Albus Dumbledore — rescues Harry; explains events afterward.
- Nicolas Flamel and Perenelle Flamel — agree to the Stone's destruction, accepting their own deaths.
- Hagrid — visits Harry in tears of guilt; gives Harry a photo album of his parents.
- Fred and George Weasley — send Harry a joke gift (a toilet seat), confiscated by Madam Pomfrey.
- Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger — visit Harry in the hospital wing; recount their own escapes.
- Neville Longbottom — awarded 10 points for standing up to his friends (the detail that wins the House Cup).
- Draco Malfoy — seen "stunned and horrified" at the final feast when Gryffindor wins.
- Ginny Weasley — seen at King's Cross, excitedly pointing out Harry Potter to her mother.

**Named places/objects:**
- The Mirror of Erised (used by Quirrell/Voldemort to try to locate the Stone).
- The Philosopher's Stone — ultimately destroyed.
- The hospital wing (Madam Pomfrey).

**Numbers/specifics:**
- Harry is unconscious/recovering for three days before waking in the hospital wing.
- Final House Cup standings before Dumbledore's last-minute awards: Gryffindor 312 (4th), Hufflepuff 352 (3rd), Ravenclaw 426 (2nd), Slytherin 472 (1st).
- Dumbledore's final point awards: Ron +50 (chess), Hermione +50 (logic), Harry +60 (nerve and courage), Neville +10 (courage to stand up to friends) — pushing Gryffindor's total to 472 (tied with Slytherin) and then over with Neville's points, winning the Cup.
- Slytherin had won the House Cup for the previous seven years running (confirmed again here).
- Nicolas Flamel is 665 years old (665th birthday "last year," per the earlier chapter); his wife Perenelle 658.

**Small/obscure details:**
- Voldemort's face is described as "chalk white with glaring red eyes and slits for nostrils, like a snake."
- Quirrell's hands blister and burn when he touches Harry's bare skin — explained by Dumbledore as the protective magic of Lily Potter's self-sacrificing love.
- Dumbledore eats a Bertie Bott's Every Flavour Bean and gets an unlucky "earwax" flavor, joking about a past "vomit-flavoured" bean that put him off the sweets.

- Snape and James Potter are revealed to have loathed each other at school; James once saved Snape's life, which Snape could never forgive or forget.

- The last line of the book has Harry looking forward to using his magical abilities to tease Dudley over the summer, since the Dursleys don't know underage wizards aren't allowed to use magic outside school.

## Cross-chapter facts useful for hardest-tier trivia

- Harry's full vault/possessions at Gringotts are not given an exact Galleon total, but he is said to have "more money than he'd had in his whole life."
- The currency conversion: 17 Sickles = 1 Galleon; 29 Knuts = 1 Sickle.
- The four Hogwarts houses' founding traits per the Sorting Hat's song: Gryffindor (bravery/daring/chivalry), Hufflepuff (justice/loyalty/hard work), Ravenclaw (wit/learning), Slytherin (cunning/ambition).
- Slytherin's House Cup winning streak before this year: seven years running.
- Gryffindor's Quidditch Cup drought before Harry joins: since Charlie Weasley left (unspecified number of years, but "haven't won since Charlie left").
- The Weasley family's known members in this book: Mr and Mrs Weasley (parents, in Romania visiting Charlie at Christmas), Bill (eldest, former Head Boy, works for Gringotts in Africa), Charlie (dragon researcher in Romania, former Quidditch captain), Percy (Prefect), Fred and George (twins), Ron, Ginny (youngest, not yet at Hogwarts).
- Draco Malfoy's known associates: Crabbe and Goyle (bodyguards), Pansy Parkinson (mentioned mocking Neville).
- The three-headed dog's name, Fluffy, and owner (Hagrid) are revealed only in Chapter 11 ("Quidditch"), despite the dog itself first appearing in Chapter 9.
- The Mirror of Erised's inscription, read backwards, translates to: "I show not your face but your heart's desire."
- Harry's wand: holly and phoenix feather, 11 inches; cost 7 Galleons. The "brother" wand (same phoenix feather core) is Voldemort's: yew, 13.5 inches.
- James Potter's wand: mahogany, 11 inches, pliable, good for Transfiguration. Lily Potter's wand: willow, 10.25 inches, swishy, good for charm work.
- Quidditch positions and scoring: 3 Chasers (score with Quaffle, 10 points/goal), 1 Keeper (guards hoops), 2 Beaters (control Bludgers), 1 Seeker (catches the Golden Snitch, worth 150 points and ends the game).
- The Daily Prophet is the wizarding newspaper; the Ministry of Magic is headed by Cornelius Fudge (mentioned in Chapter 5 as a "bungler" who consults Dumbledore constantly).
