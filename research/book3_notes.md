## Chapter 1: Owl Post

- Ron had called the Dursleys on the telephone earlier in the summer, causing a row; Harry hasn't heard from Ron or Hermione in five weeks.
- At one minute past midnight Harry turns 13 (his birthday, July 31, though not stated in text directly, implied).
- Three owls arrive: Errol (Weasley family owl, collapses from exhaustion), Hedwig (Harry's owl, returning from delivering Ron's note), and an unknown tawny owl carrying the Hogwarts letter.
- Harry receives: a Daily Prophet clipping about Mr. Weasley winning a prize; a letter and birthday card from Ron; a Pocket Sneakoscope from Ron; a letter and gift from Hermione (a Broomstick Servicing Kit); a gift from Hagrid (The Monster Book of Monsters, which tries to bite him); his Hogwarts letter with the Hogsmeade permission form.
- Harry worries about getting the Hogsmeade form signed by the Dursleys.

**Specific numbers/quantities:**
- Wendelin the Weird allowed herself to be caught "no less than forty-seven times."
- Harry turns 13 years old at 1:00 a.m.

## Chapter 2: Aunt Marge's Big Mistake

**Plot events (in order):**
- Breakfast at the Dursleys'; TV news reports on escaped convict (Sirius Black, unnamed on Muggle news) warning he's armed and dangerous.
- Uncle Vernon reveals Aunt Marge is visiting for a week; Harry must pretend to attend "St. Brutus's Secure Center for Incurably Criminal Boys."
- Harry blackmails Uncle Vernon into agreeing to sign the Hogsmeade form if Harry behaves during Marge's visit.
- Harry sends Hedwig and Errol away to the Weasleys for the week.
- Aunt Marge arrives with her bulldog Ripper; subjects Harry to days of insults, suggesting his parents (especially his father) were worthless and that "bad blood" explains Harry.
- A wineglass explodes in Marge's hand after she insults Harry's parents (accidental magic).
- On the final evening, after much wine and brandy, Marge insults James Potter as a "no-account, good-for-nothing, lazy scrounger," says his parents "died in a car crash, drunk" — Harry loses control; Marge inflates like a balloon and floats to the ceiling.
- Harry flees the house with his trunk and Hedwig's cage, threatening Uncle Vernon with his wand, declaring he's leaving.

**Named characters:** Harry, Uncle Vernon, Aunt Petunia, Dudley, Aunt Marge (Marjorie Dursley, Vernon's sister), Ripper (Marge's bulldog), Colonel Fubster (Marge's neighbor who looks after her dogs), Sirius Black (unnamed on TV, described as escaped convict), Minister of Agriculture and Fisheries (mentioned by newsreader).

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures/food:** St. Brutus's Secure Center for Incurably Criminal Boys (fake school name), Privet Drive, fruitcake, lemon meringue pie, Grunnings (Vernon's drill-making company), brandy, wine.

**Specific numbers/quantities:**
- Marge staying "for a week."
- Dudley's fifth birthday party mentioned (Marge hit Harry with her walking stick).
- Dudley given a crisp twenty-pound note by Marge.
- Marge drinks "wine," then "a bit more brandy," then more — gets progressively drunk.
- Ripper bit Uncle Vernon's leg.
- One of the panes of glass in the front door breaks when Vernon slams it.

**Small/obscure details:**
- Aunt Petunia is Lily Potter's sister (not blood-related to Marge).
- Marge brought a computerized robot for Dudley and a box of dog biscuits for Harry one Christmas.
- Harry trod on Ripper's tail once; was chased up a tree until past midnight.
- Harry has been given "an official warning" last summer for a house-elf's pudding incident (Dobby, referenced from Chamber of Secrets).
- Harry practices "A Charm to Cure Reluctant Reversers" from his Broomcare handbook to stay calm (page twelve).
- New Ministry of Magic hotline for Black sightings.
- Vernon's new company car mentioned.
- Harry uses underage magic (the explosion) — risks expulsion.

## Chapter 3: The Knight Bus

**Plot events (in order):**
- Harry sits on a wall in Magnolia Crescent, panicking about being expelled and having nowhere to go.
- He senses something watching him in a nearby alley; lights his wand ("Lumos") and sees a large shape with gleaming eyes.
- He falls, and the Knight Bus appears with a BANG. Conductor Stan Shunpike greets him; Harry gives the false name "Neville Longbottom."
- Ernie Prang is the driver. The bus bounces around Britain picking up/dropping off passengers.
- Stan shows Harry a Daily Prophet with Sirius Black's photo; recounts Black's history — murdering 13 people with one curse 12 years ago, famous Azkaban escapee.
- Bus drops Harry at the Leaky Cauldron in London; he runs into Cornelius Fudge, Minister of Magic.
- Fudge takes Harry to a private parlor, reveals the Ministry "fixed" the Marge incident (modified her memory), says Harry won't be punished or expelled, arranges a room at the Leaky Cauldron for the rest of the holiday, insists Harry stay only in Diagon Alley and be back before dark. Fudge refuses to sign the Hogsmeade form (not Harry's guardian).
- Harry goes to his room; Hedwig is already there.

**Named characters:** Harry (as "Neville Longbottom"), Stan Shunpike (conductor), Ernie Prang (driver), Madam Marsh (a passenger, dropped off at Abergavenny), a tiny wizard in a nightcap "pickling slugs," Sirius Black, Cornelius Fudge (Minister of Magic), Tom (toothless landlord of the Leaky Cauldron), real Neville Longbottom (referenced).

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures:** Magnolia Crescent, Knight Bus (triple-decker, purple), Wales, Abergavenny, Anglesey, Aberdeen, Diagon Alley, Leaky Cauldron, Charing Cross Road, Azkaban fortress, Decree for the Restriction of Underage Wizardry, Invisibility Cloak (Harry's father's), Daily Prophet, Galleons/Sickles/Knuts (currency mentioned), room number eleven (Harry's room at Leaky Cauldron).

**Specific numbers/quantities:**
- Fare to London: Eleven Sickles (basic), thirteen Sickles for hot chocolate, fifteen Sickles for hot water bottle and toothbrush.
- Stan Shunpike is "eighteen or nineteen at most."
- Sirius Black murdered "thirteen people with a single curse," "twelve years ago" (massacre).
- Never been a breakout from Azkaban before Black's escape.
- Hagrid spent two months in Azkaban the previous year (Chamber of Secrets reference).

**Small/obscure details:**
- Knight Bus: brass bedsteads instead of seats, candles in brackets, wood-paneled walls.
- The bus can jump "a hundred miles at a time"; Muggles don't notice it because they "don't look properly."
- Stan pours hot chocolate on Harry's pillow by accident.
- Fudge wears a bottle-green suit under a pinstriped cloak.
- Tom serves tea and crumpets.
- Fudge mentions the Ministry sent "two members of the Accidental Magic Reversal Squad" to fix Marge, and that she was "punctured" and memory-modified.
- Fudge was criticized by the International Federation of Warlocks for informing the Muggle Prime Minister about Black.

## Chapter 7: The Boggart in the Wardrobe
