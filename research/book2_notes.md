# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Trivia Fact Sheet

Source: full text of "Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets" (pdftotext -layout extraction), read sequentially cover to cover. 18 chapters. Compiled for pub-quiz question writing across four difficulty tiers (easy → very obscure).

---

## Chapter 1: The Worst Birthday

**Plot events (in order):**
- Breakfast argument at number four, Privet Drive: Uncle Vernon rages at Harry about Hedwig hooting (third time that week).
- It is revealed this is Harry's 12th birthday, which the Dursleys ignore completely (no cards, no cake).
- Uncle Vernon rehearses the family's plan for a dinner party that evening with the Masons, a builder and his wife, hoping to land a large drill order.
- Harry is assigned to hide in his room "making no noise and pretending I'm not there" during the dinner.
- Harry is made to do all the garden chores (windows, car, lawn, flowerbeds, roses, repainting the bench) while Dudley eats ice cream and watches.
- Dudley taunts Harry about having no friends/cards; Harry pretends to threaten to curse the hedge ("Jiggery pokery... Hocus pocus... squiggly wiggly") to scare him; Aunt Petunia hits Harry with a soapy frying pan.
- That night, Dobby the house-elf is discovered sitting on Harry's bed, having watched him from the garden hedge earlier.
- Dobby warns Harry not to return to Hogwarts this year, hinting at a "plot" to make "terrible things" happen, but won't say who or what.
- Dobby reveals he has been intercepting and hiding letters from Ron, Hermione, and Hagrid all summer.
- When Harry refuses to promise not to return to school, Dobby levitates Aunt Petunia's pudding (a mound of whipped cream and sugared violets) and drops it, then vanishes.
- A Ministry of Magic warning letter arrives by owl mid-dinner-party (delivered in front of the Masons), ruining Uncle Vernon's business deal.
- Furious, Uncle Vernon locks Harry in his room, bars the window, and only allows him out via a cat-flap fitted in the door for food three times a day.
- Three days later, starving, Harry is awakened at night by Ron Weasley's face at the barred window.

**Named characters:**
- Harry Potter — protagonist, wizard, 12 years old as of this chapter.
- Vernon Dursley — Harry's uncle, works in a company that makes drills; attended Smeltings school.
- Petunia Dursley — Harry's aunt.
- Dudley Dursley — Harry's cousin, large, attends Smeltings.
- Dobby — a house-elf who appears for the first time, serves an unnamed wizarding family.
- Hedwig — Harry's owl, padlocked in her cage by Uncle Vernon.
- Mr and Mrs Mason — dinner guests; Mr Mason is a builder Uncle Vernon wants a contract from.
- Ron Weasley — mentioned as Harry's best friend (hasn't written all summer, actually because Dobby intercepted letters); appears at window at chapter's end.
- Hermione Granger — mentioned, Harry's other best friend.
- Lord Voldemort — referenced as "He Who Must Not Be Named," the dark sorcerer Harry survived as a baby and met again at the end of year one.
- Draco Malfoy — mentioned as Harry's "arch-enemy."
- Mafalda Hopkirk — signs the Ministry warning letter (Improper Use of Magic Office).

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures/food:**
- Privet Drive, number four — Dursley home.
- Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.
- Hover Charm — the spell the Ministry letter says was detected.
- Decree for the Reasonable Restriction of Underage Sorcery, 1875, Paragraph C — cited law.
- International Confederation of Warlocks' Statute of Secrecy, section 13 — cited law.
- Ministry of Magic, Improper Use of Magic Office.
- Nimbus Two Thousand — Harry's broomstick, locked in the cupboard.
- Quidditch — described: six goalposts, four flying balls, fourteen players on broomsticks.
- Smeltings — Dudley and Vernon's school.
- Cupboard under the stairs — where Harry's school things are locked.

**Specific numbers/quantities:**
- Harry turns 12 years old.
- "Third time this week" the owl hooted.
- Hover Charm used "at twelve minutes past nine" in the evening.
- Harry spent ten years with the Dursleys before Hogwarts letter arrived (now "exactly a year" since).
- Quidditch: six goalposts, four balls, fourteen players.
- Uncle Vernon's company makes drills; talking about the deal "for a fortnight."
- Dinner party schedule: 8:00 arrival, 8:15 dinner announced.
- Harry locked up "three days" before Ron arrives.
- Food allowed "three times a day" via cat-flap.

**Obscure/small details:**
- Aunt Petunia's pudding: "a huge mound of whipped cream and sugared violets."
- Uncle Vernon's joke being ruined was "Japanese-golfer joke."
- Dudley's line to rehearse: "May I take your coats, Mr and Mrs Mason?"
- The dessert destroyed became the turning point that enraged Uncle Vernon alongside the Ministry letter.
- Dobby wears a filthy pillowcase with holes for arms/legs — the mark of house-elf enslavement.
- Dobby self-punishes by banging his head, ironing his ears, etc.
- Harry dreams of being in a zoo with a sign "Underage Wizard."
- The Dursleys' planned reward for landing the Mason deal: a holiday home in Majorca.

## Chapter 2: Dobby's Warning

(Continues same night as Chapter 1; included above for narrative flow — see Chapter 1 for Dobby's visit, which this chapter is named for.)

**Plot events (in order):**
- Dobby introduces himself properly as a house-elf bound to serve one family forever; says a house-elf can only be freed if given clothes by the master.
- Dobby refuses to name who is plotting trouble at Hogwarts, banging his head whenever close to telling, implying it is forbidden.
- Harry guesses several names (Voldemort, a brother of Voldemort, Dumbledore) — Dobby denies all but hints heavily.
- Dobby reveals he has hidden letters from Ron, Hermione and Hagrid.
- When Harry refuses to promise not to return, Dobby destroys Aunt Petunia's pudding and vanishes.
- Uncle Vernon initially manages to smooth over the pudding incident with the Masons.
- A Ministry owl drops the official warning letter onto Mrs Mason's head at the dinner table; Mrs Mason (terrified of birds) flees the house; the business deal collapses.
- Uncle Vernon locks Harry up, has bars fitted on his window, installs a cat-flap in the bedroom door.
- Three days pass; Harry is fed cold tinned soup through the cat-flap.
- Harry falls asleep and dreams of being in a zoo cage; wakes to see Ron's face at the window.

**Named characters:** Dobby, Harry, Uncle Vernon, Aunt Petunia, Dudley, Mr and Mrs Mason, Mafalda Hopkirk (letter signer), Ron Weasley (arrives), Hedwig.

**Named places/objects:** Ministry of Magic, Improper Use of Magic Office; the Dursleys' dining room/kitchen; Hedwig's cage.

**Numbers:** Letter cites spell used "this evening at twelve minutes past nine"; bars fitted "the following morning"; three days pass before Ron's arrival; food given three times daily.

**Obscure details:**
- Dobby's family is "careful not to pass Dobby even a sock" so he can never be freed.
- Dobby gets "death threats... five times a day at home."
- The tinned soup Harry is given is stone cold; he shares soggy vegetables from it with Hedwig.

## Chapter 3: The Burrow

**Plot events (in order):**
- Ron, with twins Fred and George, arrives in Mr Weasley's flying turquoise Ford Anglia to rescue Harry.
- They tie a rope to the window bars and pull them out with the car.
- Fred and George pick the lock on the cupboard under the stairs with a hairpin (a "Muggle trick") to retrieve Harry's trunk, wand, and broomstick.
- Uncle Vernon wakes and grabs Harry's ankle as he's being pulled out the window; the Weasleys pull him free.
- They fly back to The Burrow, the Weasleys' home near the village of Ottery St Catchpole.
- During the flight, Ron explains Mr Weasley works in the Ministry's Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office; recounts the story of an enchanted Muggle teapot that squirted boiling tea and clamped sugar tongs to a man's nose.
- They land at The Burrow; Mrs Weasley is furious about the stolen car and unauthorized rescue, but welcomes Harry warmly.
- Breakfast: Harry meets the Weasley kitchen (self-stirring dishes, enchanted clock with no numbers, "Witching Hour" radio show with singer Celestina Warbeck).
- Ginny Weasley appears briefly, flustered, and flees.
- Fred and George are sent to de-gnome the garden by Mrs Weasley (as punishment); Harry helps; gnomes are swung by the ankles and flung over the hedge.
- Mr Weasley arrives home from a Ministry raid; mentions Mundungus Fletcher and confiscated shrinking door-keys, a biting kettle, and Mortlake under investigation for odd ferrets.
- Mrs Weasley confronts Mr Weasley for enchanting the Ford Anglia despite the "loophole" he wrote into the law himself.
- Ron shows Harry his room: orange decor, Chudley Cannons posters, Scabbers the rat, frogspawn tank, Self-Shuffling playing cards.

**Named characters:**
- Fred and George Weasley — Ron's twin elder brothers, rescue Harry.
- Mrs Molly Weasley — Ron's mother.
- Mr Arthur Weasley — Ron's father, works at the Ministry's Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office.
- Ginny Weasley — Ron's younger sister, starting Hogwarts this year.
- Percy Weasley — Ron's older brother, a prefect (mentioned, seen later).
- Bill Weasley — eldest Weasley brother, works for Gringotts in Egypt (mentioned).
- Charlie Weasley — second-eldest, studying dragons in Romania (mentioned).
- Mundungus Fletcher — tried to hex Mr Weasley during a raid.
- Mortlake — wizard taken for questioning re: odd ferrets.
- Perkins — old warlock who works with Mr Weasley.
- Scabbers — Ron's pet rat.
- Errol — the Weasleys' ancient owl (mentioned).
- Hermes — Percy's owl, a gift for becoming prefect (mentioned).
- Lucius Malfoy — discussed by Fred/George as a former supporter of Voldemort who claimed he "never meant any of it."
- Draco Malfoy — discussed as Harry's enemy; Fred/George speculate the Malfoys own Dobby.
- Celestina Warbeck — "popular singing sorceress" on the radio.
- Gilderoy Lockhart — his book Guide to Household Pests is quoted/shown (Mrs Weasley is a fan).

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures/food:**
- The Burrow — Weasleys' home, described as like a crooked stone pigsty with extra rooms added, held up "by magic."
- Ottery St Catchpole — village near The Burrow.
- Ford Anglia — Mr Weasley's enchanted flying turquoise car.
- Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office — Ministry department.
- Gnomes — garden pests; "de-gnoming" method described.
- Chudley Cannons — Ron's favourite Quidditch team (orange robes, two black Cs and cannonball on bedspread); "ninth in the league."
- Martin Miggs, the Mad Muggle — comic book character Ron reads.
- Self-Shuffling playing cards.
- Gilderoy Lockhart's Guide to Household Pests — book Mrs Weasley owns.
- The ghoul in the attic.

**Specific numbers/quantities:**
- Ron had asked Harry to stay "about twelve times" by letter.
- Mr Weasley's raid: "Nine raids. Nine!"
- Mrs Weasley gives Harry "eight or nine sausages" and "three fried eggs" for breakfast.
- Chudley Cannons: ninth in the league.
- A gnome Ron throws flies "twenty feet"; Harry's goes "fifty feet."
- Fred bets he can beat that.
- "Five minutes later" they're marching up the hill (next chapter reference).

**Obscure/small details:**
- The enchanted clock's hands show phrases like "Time to make tea," "Time to feed the chickens," "You're late" instead of numbers.
- Books on the Weasleys' mantelpiece: Charm Your Own Cheese, Enchantment in Baking, One Minute Feasts — It's Magic!
- The radio show is "Witching Hour," featuring singer Celestina Warbeck.
- A Muggle woman's enchanted teapot (bought at an antiques shop after an old witch died) squirted boiling tea and clamped sugar tongs to a man's nose; required Memory Charms to cover up.
- Mr Weasley's shed is full of Muggle stuff he takes apart, enchants, and reassembles.
- Percy got twelve O.W.L.s; so did Bill. George jokes about "another Head Boy in the family."
- Fred/George consider becoming the next Head Boy "the shame."

## Chapter 4: At Flourish and Blotts

**Plot events (in order):**
- Life at The Burrow described: talking mirror ("Tuck your shirt in, scruffy!"), ghoul banging pipes.
- Hogwarts letters arrive; book list is entirely seven Lockhart books plus Standard Book of Spells Grade 2.
- Fred/George tease that the new Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher must be a Lockhart fan.
- Family travels to Diagon Alley via Floo powder for the first time for Harry; he mispronounces the destination and ends up in Knockturn Alley, at Borgin and Burkes.
- Harry hides in a cabinet and overhears Lucius Malfoy selling Dark artefacts to Mr Borgin (ahead of Ministry raids) and badmouthing Arthur Weasley's Muggle Protection Act; Draco wants the Hand of Glory; Lucius criticizes Draco for being beaten by Hermione Granger in exams.
- Hagrid finds and rescues Harry from Knockturn Alley (was there buying Flesh-Eating Slug Repellent for school cabbages).
- Harry reunites with Hermione and the Weasleys outside Gringotts; meets Hermione's Muggle parents (Mr and Mrs Granger).
- Visit to Gringotts vaults: Weasley vault has very little (a few Sickles and one Galleon); Harry's vault (untouched, inherited) by contrast is full.
- Group splits to shop; Harry treats Ron and Hermione to ice cream (strawberry and peanut butter).
- At Flourish and Blotts, Gilderoy Lockhart is signing his autobiography Magical Me; he recognizes Harry, poses for photos, gives Harry his whole set of books free, and announces he will be the new Hogwarts Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher.
- Draco Malfoy taunts Harry and Ginny (first time Ginny speaks in front of Harry, defending him).
- Lucius Malfoy and Arthur Weasley confront each other; a physical fight breaks out, ended by Hagrid; Lucius returns Ginny's old Transfiguration book to her spitefully (actually planting the diary inside it, revealed later).

**Named characters:**
- Lucius Malfoy — Draco's father, seen for the first time; sells Dark items to Borgin, baits Arthur Weasley.
- Mr Borgin — proprietor of Borgin and Burkes.
- Draco Malfoy — wants a racing broom and the Hand of Glory; mocked for being beaten by Hermione academically.
- Hagrid — rescues Harry from Knockturn Alley, was buying Flesh-Eating Slug Repellent.
- Hermione Granger, Mr and Mrs Granger (her Muggle parents) — introduced properly.
- Gilderoy Lockhart — introduced in person; announced as new DADA teacher.
- Lee Jordan — Fred and George's friend.
- Draco, Crabbe, Goyle — not yet named as a trio here but Draco present.
- Ginny Weasley — first speaks in Harry's presence, defending him against Malfoy.
- The unnamed Flourish and Blotts photographer (Daily Prophet).

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures/food:**
- Diagon Alley; Knockturn Alley; Borgin and Burkes (shop); Gringotts Wizarding Bank; Flourish and Blotts (bookshop); the Leaky Cauldron; Gambol and Japes Wizarding Joke Shop; Quality Quidditch Supplies.
- Floo powder — method of travel, used for first time by Harry.

## Chapter 5: The Whomping Willow

**Plot events (in order):**
- Last night at The Burrow: Mrs Weasley's feast, Fred and George's Filibuster fireworks display.
- Chaotic departure morning; trip to King's Cross in the (secretly magically enlarged) Ford Anglia.
- At the platform 9¾ barrier, Harry and Ron fail to get through — the barrier has been sealed.
- They decide to fly the (recovered, repaired) Ford Anglia to Hogwarts instead, invoking the "genuine emergency" exception for underage magic.
- The flight: Invisibility Booster malfunctions, car becomes visible intermittently over London; they follow the Hogwarts Express by sight; hours-long journey; they run low on supplies (toffees in the glove compartment).
- Nearing Hogwarts at dusk, the engine dies; the car crash-lands into the Whomping Willow, which attacks the car; the car ejects Harry, Ron, and their luggage and drives off into the Forbidden Forest on its own.
- They arrive to watch the Sorting Ceremony through a window; Ginny is sorted into Gryffindor; they notice Snape's seat is empty at the staff table.
- Snape intercepts them, reveals the Daily Prophet headline ("FLYING FORD ANGLIA MYSTIFIES MUGGLES") naming multiple Muggle witnesses, and threatens expulsion; brings in Professor McGonagall and then Dumbledore.
- Dumbledore does not expel them but warns of expulsion if it happens again; McGonagall, after Harry's point about term not yet starting, does not deduct House points but gives detention to both boys.
- They return to Gryffindor Tower to a triumphant welcome from housemates (new password: "wattlebird"); meet roommates Seamus Finnigan, Dean Thomas, Neville Longbottom.

**Named characters:**
- Professor Severus Snape — confronts the boys, reveals the newspaper clipping, is Head of Slytherin, Potions master.
- Professor Minerva McGonagall — Head of Gryffindor, Transfiguration teacher; decides punishment (detention, no points lost).
- Professor Albus Dumbledore — Headmaster; declines to expel them but warns against repeat offenses; mentions wanting a custard tart.
- Gilderoy Lockhart — seen at the staff table in aquamarine robes.
- Hagrid — seen at the staff table drinking from his goblet.
- Hermione Granger — greets them at the Fat Lady's portrait; gives new password.
- Seamus Finnigan, Dean Thomas, Neville Longbottom — roommates introduced by name as second-year Gryffindor boys.
- Lee Jordan — praises the dramatic entrance.
- The Fat Lady — portrait guarding Gryffindor common room.
- Mrs Hetty Bayliss (Norfolk), Mr Angus Fleet (Peebles) — named Muggle witnesses quoted in the Evening Prophet article about the flying car.

**Named places/objects/spells/creatures/food:**
- King's Cross Station, Platform 9¾.
- The Whomping Willow — violent magical tree.
- Ford Anglia (crashes, then drives off into the Forbidden Forest).
- Evening Prophet — newspaper with the "FLYING FORD ANGLIA MYSTIFIES MUGGLES" headline.
- Sorting Hat — places Ginny in Gryffindor.
- The four Hogwarts houses: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, Slytherin.
- Password "wattlebird" for Gryffindor common room.

**Specific numbers/quantities:**
- They reach King's Cross "at a quarter to eleven," with "only five minutes" to get through the barrier.
- Underage magic emergency exception: "section nineteen or something of the Restriction of Thingy" (Ron's vague citation).
- Gryffindor won the House Championship last year, beating Slytherin "for the first time in seven years."
- Harry and Ron's journey by car lasts "several hours."
- Six or seven Muggles reportedly saw the flying car, per Snape.

**Obscure/small details:**
- Mr Weasley's Invisibility Booster was installed secretly in the car ("Not a word to Molly").
- Snape suspects the Whomping Willow (described as "a very valuable Whomping Willow") was damaged; several of its branches are later seen in slings.
- Harry notes "There's an empty chair at the staff table... Where's Snape?" before Snape turns out to be right behind them.
- Punishment: detention for both; McGonagall says she will not deduct Gryffindor points because term had not technically started.
- Ron's wand snaps nearly in two in the crash — a running problem for the rest of the term.

## Chapter 6: Gilderoy Lockhart

- Lockhart corners Harry outside the greenhouse, mistakenly believing Harry flew the car for publicity, lectures him about fame.
- Transfiguration class: turning beetles into buttons; Ron's broken wand smokes and misfires.
- Colin Creevey introduced — a star-struck first-year Gryffindor Muggle-born (father is a milkman) with a camera, asks for Harry's photo.
- Draco Malfoy mocks the "signed photos" rumor in the courtyard, flanked by Crabbe and Goyle.
- First Defence Against the Dark Arts lesson: Lockhart gives a 54-question quiz about himself; Hermione gets full marks; Lockhart releases a cage of Cornish pixies, which wreck the classroom; he proves unable to control them and flees, leaving Harry, Ron and Hermione to clean up.

## Chapter 8: The Deathday Party

**Plot events (in order):**
- October arrives; Madam Pomfrey treats a cold epidemic with Pepper-Up Potion (causes ears to smoke).
- Harry encounters Nearly Headless Nick, upset about rejection from the Headless Hunt (his neck is not fully severed enough — held by "half an inch of skin and sinew").
- Filch catches Harry tracking mud into the castle; in Filch's office, Harry discovers a "Kwikspell" correspondence course pamphlet (a remedial magic course) — implying Filch may be a Squib.
- Peeves (with Nearly Headless Nick's encouragement) distracts Filch by smashing a vanishing cabinet, letting Harry escape detention.
- Nick invites Harry (plus Ron and Hermione) to his 500th Deathday Party.
- At the Hallowe'en feast, Harry/Ron/Hermione attend the Deathday Party instead (ghostly guests, rotten food, Moaning Myrtle appears and is mocked by Peeves, the Headless Hunt arrives led by Sir Patrick Delaney-Podmore).

## Chapter 16: The Chamber of Secrets

**Plot events (in order):**
- Breakfast discussion: regret at not asking Myrtle sooner.
- McGonagall announces exams will proceed, starting 1 June — a week from the announcement — despite the crisis, on Dumbledore's prior instruction to keep school "running as normally as possible."

- Lockhart grabs Ron's semi-broken wand and tries to obliviate them to take credit for a fake rescue, but the wand backfires catastrophically, collapsing the tunnel ceiling and separating Harry from Ron and Lockhart (the backfired spell also wipes Lockhart's own memory, though this isn't confirmed to Harry yet).

## Chapter 17: The Heir of Slytherin

**Plot events (in order):**
- Harry enters the vast Chamber; finds Ginny motionless (alive but barely) at the foot of a giant stone statue of Salazar Slytherin's face.
- Tom Riddle appears as a memory/ghost-like figure from the diary, solid enough to interact, and reveals the full truth: he has been manipulating Ginny through the diary all year, draining her life force and feeding her his own memories/will, causing her to open the Chamber, kill the roosters, and write the threatening messages — all while she blacked out and suspected herself.
- Riddle reveals he orchestrated Hagrid's original expulsion as a frame job to gain Headmaster Dippet's and the school's trust.
- Riddle takes Harry's wand, confirming Harry cannot easily escape; reveals he is, in fact, Lord Voldemort — rearranging the letters of "TOM MARVOLO RIDDLE" into "I AM LORD VOLDEMORT."
- Riddle explains his hatred of his Muggle father's name and pride in his descent (through his mother) from Salazar Slytherin.
- Harry taunts Riddle, saying Dumbledore is the greatest wizard and still frightens him.
- Fawkes the phoenix arrives, carrying the Sorting Hat, dropped at Harry's feet; Riddle mocks the "weapons" Dumbledore has sent.
- Riddle commands the statue of Slytherin to open its mouth; the Basilisk emerges; Riddle orders it to kill Harry.
- Harry flees blind (eyes shut) to avoid the Basilisk's lethal gaze; Fawkes blinds the Basilisk by pecking out its eyes.
- The Sorting Hat produces a silver sword (Godric Gryffindor's sword) when Harry, in desperation, calls for help while wearing it.
- Harry kills the Basilisk by driving the sword through the roof of its mouth, but is wounded by a Basilisk fang that breaks off in his arm, poisoning him.
- As Harry is dying, Riddle gloats; Fawkes cries healing phoenix tears onto the wound, curing the venom.
- Riddle shoots Fawkes away and gloats again, but Fawkes drops the diary into Harry's lap; Harry stabs the diary with a Basilisk fang, destroying Riddle/the horcrux-like memory for good (ink "bleeds" out, Riddle screams and vanishes).
- Ginny wakes, confesses tearfully that she was responsible for everything but didn't mean to; fears expulsion.
- Fawkes guides them back through the tunnel; they reunite with Ron (who has been digging through the rockfall) and reveal Ginny is safe.
- Fawkes flies all four of them (plus the memory-wiped Lockhart) up through the pipe using his tail feathers.
- They emerge in Myrtle's bathroom; Myrtle flirts awkwardly with Harry ("you'd have been welcome to share my toilet").
- They head toward McGonagall's office, following Fawkes's glow.

**Named characters:**
- Tom Riddle / Lord Voldemort — fully revealed as the same person; his backstory elaborated (half-blood, Muggle father who abandoned his witch mother, orphanage upbringing, descent from Slytherin via his mother).

**Obscure/small details:**

## Cross-chapter facts (franchise-relevant running details for this book)

**Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher this year:** Gilderoy Lockhart. Revealed at the end to be a fraud who used Memory Charms to steal other people's real magical feats and claim them as his own in his books (Break with a Banshee, Gadding with Ghouls, Holidays with Hags, Travels with Trolls, Voyages with Vampires, Wanderings with Werewolves, Year with the Yeti, and his autobiography Magical Me). His own Memory Charm backfires on him in the Chamber (cast via Ron's broken wand), leaving him permanently amnesiac; Dumbledore announces at the end-of-year feast that Lockhart cannot return next year as he needs to recover his memory. Several teachers visibly cheer this news. Dumbledore notes they'll need yet another new DADA teacher.

**Quidditch / House Cup outcomes and point totals:**
- Gryffindor had lost the Quidditch Cup the previous year (Book 1) after their "worst defeat in three hundred years" when Harry was unconscious for the final match.
- First match this year: Gryffindor vs Slytherin. A rogue Bludger (secretly bewitched by Dobby) targets only Harry; score at one point is "Slytherin lead, sixty points to zero," but Harry catches the Snitch anyway (worth 150 points) despite a broken arm, winning for Gryffindor. Lockhart then botches mending Harry's arm, removing all the bones instead ("Skele-Gro" needed to regrow them, 33 bones).
- Slytherin's entire team received brand-new Nimbus Two Thousand and One broomsticks, bought by Lucius Malfoy, so Draco Malfoy could be made Seeker.
- The Gryffindor-Hufflepuff match is later cancelled entirely due to the double attack on Hermione and the Ravenclaw prefect.
- Percy confirms early in the book that the Quidditch win earned Gryffindor 50 points, putting them ahead for the House Cup.
- At the final feast, Harry and Ron are each awarded 200 points (400 total) for solving the mystery, securing Gryffindor the House Cup "for the second year running." Exams are cancelled as a treat.

**New Weasley-family details:**
- Bill Weasley (eldest) works as a curse-breaker for Gringotts in Egypt (confirmed fully in the book 3 preview chapter at the very end of this file, but referenced here as working for Gringotts "in Egypt").
- Charlie Weasley (second-eldest) studies dragons in Romania.
- Percy Weasley is a Prefect this year, obsessively ambitious (wants to be Minister of Magic per Ron), was given an owl (Hermes) for making prefect; secretly dating Ravenclaw Prefect Penelope Clearwater all year (revealed on the final train ride home).
- Fred and George Weasley are both Beaters on the Gryffindor Quidditch team; prone to pranks (Filibuster Fireworks, bewitching a Salamander, bewitching Percy's badge to read "Pinhead").
- Ginny Weasley starts her first year at Hogwarts this book; is secretly the one possessed/manipulated via Riddle's diary into opening the Chamber of Secrets; sorted into Gryffindor.
- Mr Arthur Weasley works in the Ministry's Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office; is fined 50 Galleons for bewitching the family's Ford Anglia; loses his temper and fights Lucius Malfoy in Flourish and Blotts; his Muggle Protection Act is a recurring point of contention with the Malfoys.
- Mrs Molly Weasley is overprotective and short-tempered but kind; sends a Howler to Ron over the flying car incident.
- The Weasleys' home, The Burrow, is nicknamed/described in detail; near the village of Ottery St Catchpole.

**Prefects / Head Boy or Girl mentioned:**
- Percy Weasley — Gryffindor Prefect this year (becomes Head Boy the following year, per the Book 3 preview chapter included at the end of the source file).
- Penelope Clearwater — Ravenclaw Prefect, Percy's secret girlfriend; one of the Basilisk's victims (Petrified).
- Tom Riddle was Head Boy and a Prefect fifty years prior, at Hogwarts, as revealed via trophy-room research and his diary/memory.
- Bill Weasley got twelve O.W.L.s (mentioned alongside Percy, also twelve O.W.L.s), fueling jokes about "another Head Boy in the family."

**Named spells introduced in this book (non-exhaustive list of incantations spoken on-page):**
- Expelliarmus (Disarming Charm) — first shown by Snape on Lockhart; later used twice by Harry (against Malfoy for the diary, and against Lockhart in the tunnel).
- Rictusempra (Tickling Charm) — Harry vs. Malfoy in Duelling Club.
- Tarantallegra (dancing-leg jinx) — Malfoy vs. Harry in Duelling Club.
- Finite Incantatem (general counter-spell) — Snape, ending the duel chaos.
- Serpensortia (conjures a snake) — Malfoy's spell.
- Peskipiksi Pesternomi — Lockhart's (ineffective) pixie-control spell.
- Aparecium — revealing/invisible-ink charm (Hermione, on the diary; fails).
- Lumos — wandlight charm (Harry, multiple times).
- Obliviate — Memory Charm (Lockhart, backfires on himself).
- Parseltongue commands — "Open up" / "Open" (not translated as a "spell" per se, but functionally opens the Chamber entrance and the Basilisk's passage; also "Leave him!" to stop the Duelling Club snake).

**Deaths in this book:** No on-page character deaths occur in the present-day storyline. (The historical death of Moaning Myrtle, 50 years prior — 1943 implied by "fifty years ago" from a 1992/93-ish present — is recounted, and it's revealed she herself was the "Mudbloods" victim killed by the Basilisk the first time the Chamber was opened.) Mrs Norris, Colin Creevey, Justin Finch-Fletchley, Nearly Headless Nick (already a ghost), Hermione Granger, and Penelope Clearwater are all "Petrified," not killed, and all are revived via Mandrake Restorative Draught by the end of the book.

**Central antagonist / plot device of this book:**
- The central antagonist is Lord Voldemort (as his teenage self, Tom Marvolo Riddle), acting fifty years after his own time at Hogwarts through an enchanted diary (a preserved fragment/memory of his sixteen-year-old self) that he left behind as a long-term trap. The diary possesses/manipulates whoever writes in it — in this book, Ginny Weasley — compelling her to unknowingly reopen the Chamber of Secrets, release the Basilisk (the "monster of Slytherin"), and attack Muggle-born students and a Squib's cat. Lucius Malfoy is the book's secondary antagonist, having planted the diary on Ginny deliberately (slipping it into her secondhand Transfiguration book at Flourish and Blotts) to discredit Arthur Weasley and reopen the Chamber by proxy; he is also revealed to have orchestrated (via the twelve governors, through blackmail) Dumbledore's temporary suspension as Headmaster. The diary itself — later revealed in the series as one of Voldemort's Horcruxes, though that term is never used in this book — is destroyed by Harry with a Basilisk fang, ending Riddle's hold over Ginny and killing that fragment of Voldemort.
- This is also the first appearance/explicit use of the Parseltongue ability as a major plot element, and establishes Voldemort/Riddle's full backstory (half-blood, son of a Muggle father who abandoned his witch mother, orphanage childhood, descent from Salazar Slytherin through his mother Merope's line — her name is not given in this book).
- Dobby the house-elf, who serves the Malfoy family, is the book's secondary magical device/character: his warnings and sabotage (sealing the platform barrier, bewitching a Bludger) are well-intentioned attempts to keep Harry away from danger. He is freed from servitude at the end when Harry tricks Lucius Malfoy into giving him a sock.
