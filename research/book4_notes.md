# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire — Trivia Fact Sheet
(37 chapters; source: full novel text)

## Chapter 1: The Riddle House
- Plot: In Little Hangleton, the derelict Riddle House is the site of an unsolved 50-year-old triple murder (Tom Riddle Sr. and his parents, found dead with expressions of terror, no visible cause). Gardener Frank Bryce was accused at the time but released (no evidence). Fifty years later (book's "present"), elderly Frank notices lights in the house one August night, investigates, and overhears Voldemort (unnamed, "cold voice") and Wormtail (Peter Pettigrew) discussing a plan involving Harry Potter, timed to occur after the Quidditch World Cup. Voldemort reveals he killed Bertha Jorkins after extracting information from her. A giant snake (Nagini) is in the room. Frank is discovered listening (Nagini senses him) and is killed by Voldemort with the Killing Curse (green light) after Voldemort is revealed sitting in an armchair (face not described to reader yet). Chapter ends: "Two hundred miles away, the boy called Harry Potter woke with a start."
- Characters: Frank Bryce (gardener, Muggle, WWII veteran, stiff leg, deaf), the Riddle family (Tom Riddle Sr. and parents — dead 50 years before), Lord Voldemort (unnamed "cold voice"), Wormtail/Peter Pettigrew ("Wormtail," small balding man, graying hair, pointed nose, watery eyes), Nagini (the snake), Bertha Jorkins (mentioned, killed by Voldemort), Dot and other unnamed villagers at the Hanged Man pub.
- Places/objects: Little Hangleton (village), Riddle House (on a hill), the Hanged Man (village pub), Great Hangleton (neighboring town with police station).
- Numbers: Riddles killed 50 years before present events (at daybreak, summer morning); Frank nearing his 77th birthday; snake "at least twelve feet long"; Voldemort says "A week... Perhaps longer" they'll stay; Voldemort says "I have waited thirteen years."
- Obscure details: Frank drank cocoa/had a hot water bottle for his bad knee; rusty old key on a hook; Frank mistook "Quidditch" as needing to clean earwax out of his ear; Wormtail nearly convinces Voldemort to use another wizard instead of Harry but is rebuffed; Voldemort mentions "my faithful servant at Hogwarts" will help with "one more murder."

## Chapter 2: The Scar
- Plot: Harry (age 14) wakes at Privet Drive with his scar burning after dreaming the graveyard/Riddle House scene (dream mirrors Ch.1's events from his POV, distorted). He recalls Voldemort, Wormtail, an old man falling. Decides to write to Sirius Black, his godfather, for advice. Reflects on backstory: Voldemort killed his parents when Harry was one; Harry's lightning scar; Sirius was wrongly imprisoned in Azkaban for Wormtail's crimes, escaped last year; Sirius now in hiding, sends letters via large tropical birds (not owls). Harry writes a lighthearted letter to Sirius mentioning the scar pain but downplaying worry.
- Characters: Harry Potter, Dudley Dursley (snoring, on a diet, smuggling doughnuts), Uncle Vernon, Aunt Petunia, Hermione Granger (imagined advice), Ron Weasley (imagined advice), Sirius Black (godfather), Hedwig (owl), Buckbeak (hippogriff, mentioned).
- Places/objects: Privet Drive, Hogwarts (referenced), Azkaban, book "Flying with the Cannons," book "Common Magical Ailments and Afflictions."
- Numbers: Harry is 14; has lost all bones from right arm once, fallen 50 feet from broomstick; a fortnight left before school; Dudley's cousin backstory: Harry attends (fictionally, per Dursleys) "St. Brutus's Secure Center for Incurably Criminal Boys."
- Obscure: Dudley throws his PlayStation out the window in anger over diet; Harry imagines Dumbledore sunbathing; Ron is a twin brother situation referenced (Fred and George, 16-year-old twins).

## Chapter 3: The Invitation
- Plot: At breakfast, Dudley's diet (ordered by Smeltings school nurse) is enforced on the whole family. A letter arrives from Mrs. Weasley inviting Harry to the Quidditch World Cup final (Ireland vs Bulgaria) and to stay rest of summer; envelope covered in too many stamps, postman notices. Uncle Vernon reluctantly agrees after Harry threatens (by implication) to write to his "godfather." Ron's letter arrives via tiny owl Pigwidgeon, confirming pick-up at 5pm Sunday. Harry replies.
- Characters: Molly Weasley (letter), Arthur Weasley (got tickets via Ministry connections), Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger (arriving at Burrow that afternoon per Ron's letter), Percy Weasley (started job at Department of International Magical Cooperation), Dudley, Vernon, Petunia.
- Places/objects: The Burrow (implied), Department of Magical Games and Sports, Hogwarts Express (almost said aloud).
- Numbers: Britain hasn't hosted the Cup in 30 years; pickup time 5 o'clock Sunday.
- Obscure: Dudley's school report mentions bullying concerns from the nurse; Dudley is "size of a young killer whale"; Harry already secretly fed via food parcels from friends (sugar-free snacks from Hermione, rock cakes from Hagrid, fruitcake/meat pies from Mrs. Weasley via owl Errol, birthday cakes from Ron/Hermione/Hagrid/Sirius).

## Chapter 4: Back to the Burrow
- Plot: Weasleys (Mr. Weasley, Fred, George, Ron) arrive via Floo powder but the Dursleys' fireplace is blocked by an electric fire; comedic chaos as Mr. Weasley blasts a hole in the wall ("Incendio," then floo powder). Fred's Ton-Tongue Toffee (joke sweet he invented with George) is "accidentally" dropped and Dudley eats it — his tongue swells to four feet before Mr. Weasley starts to fix it with an Engorgement Charm counter-spell; Dursleys panic and throw ornaments. Harry escapes via Floo to the Burrow.
- Characters: Arthur Weasley, Fred & George Weasley, Ron Weasley, the three Dursleys.
- Places/objects: Floo Network, Floo powder, Ministry of Magic car (mentioned, last year), Ford Anglia (flying car, now wild in Forbidden Forest), Invisibility Cloak (Harry's father's), Marauder's Map-type enchanted Hogwarts map (from Fred & George, last year).
- Numbers: Dudley's tongue reaches four feet before shrinking; Harry due to be picked up at 5 pm.
- Obscure: Mr. Weasley is fascinated with Muggle artifacts/plugs, collects batteries; asks if the fireplace is "eclectic."

## Chapter 5: Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes
- Plot: Harry arrives at the Burrow; meets Bill (curse-breaker for Gringotts, ponytail, earring with fang, ex-Head Boy) and Charlie (dragon-handler in Romania) for the first time properly. Learns about Fred & George's joke-shop ambitions (Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes) which Mrs. Weasley disapproves of after finding order forms; mentions O.W.L.s (Ordinary Wizarding Levels, taken at age 15). Percy works on cauldron-bottom-thickness report for Department of International Magical Cooperation under Mr. Crouch; mentions Ludo Bagman (Head of Dept of Magical Games & Sports) and Bertha Jorkins' disappearance (missing over a month, last seen heading to Albania). Dinner outdoors; discussion of Quidditch World Cup teams (Ireland favored over Bulgaria/Krum); Harry is Gryffindor Seeker, owns a Firebolt.
- Characters: Bill Weasley, Charlie Weasley, Percy Weasley, Fred & George, Ron, Ginny Weasley, Hermione Granger, Molly & Arthur Weasley, Crookshanks (Hermione's cat), Pigwidgeon (Ron's tiny owl, named by Ginny), Scabbers (old rat, "eaten" by Crookshanks — actually Wormtail, known to readers), Mr. Crouch (mentioned, Percy's boss), Ludo Bagman (mentioned), Bertha Jorkins (missing).
- Places/objects: The Burrow, gnomes in garden, Chudley Cannons posters (Ron's favorite Quidditch team).
- Numbers: England lost to Transylvania 390-10 in Cup qualifiers (mentioned next chapter actually, but Quidditch chat starts here); last World Cup match went on for five days.
- Obscure: Percy calls the cauldron leak rate "almost three percent a year"; George teases Percy about the dragon dung sent to his in-tray (Fred/George's prank); Bill's dragon-hide boots.

## Chapter 6: The Portkey
- Plot: Early morning departure to the Quidditch World Cup. Mr. Weasley explains Apparition (requires license/test; "splinching" if done wrong — leaves body parts behind) and Portkeys (enchanted ordinary objects that transport groups at a set time; 200 placed around Britain). Fred and George try to smuggle Ton-Tongue Toffees past Mrs. Weasley (confiscated via Summoning Charm "Accio"). The group (Weasleys, Harry, Hermione) walks to Stoatshead Hill and takes a Portkey (an old boot) with the Diggorys. Introduces Amos Diggory (Ministry, Dept for Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures) and his son Cedric Diggory (17, Hufflepuff Quidditch Captain/Seeker, beat Gryffindor last year — Harry fell off broom due to Dementors).
- Characters: Amos Diggory, Cedric Diggory, Mr. Weasley, Mrs. Weasley (stays with Bill/Charlie/Percy to come later), Lovegoods and Fawcetts (mentioned, not present).
- Places/Objects: Stoatshead Hill, Ottery St. Catchpole (village near the Burrow), Portkey (old boot).
- Numbers: 100,000 wizards attend the World Cup; 200 Portkeys placed around Britain; Portkey activates at "seven past five."
- Obscure: The two wizards who splinched themselves tried to pay a Muggle with giant gold coins; Charlie failed his Apparition test once (landed 5 miles off, on an old woman); Percy apparates downstairs every morning to show off since passing 2 weeks prior.

## Chapter 7: Bagman and Crouch
- Plot: Arrival at the campsite; meet Muggle campsite manager Mr. Roberts (memory modified later by Ministry wizard in "plus-fours"). Tour the magical tent city; meet Seamus Finnigan and his mother, Dean Thomas; see Bulgaria (Krum posters) vs Ireland fan camps. Buy Omnioculars (10 Galleons) from a salesman. Meet Ludo Bagman (Head of Dept of Magical Games & Sports, ex-Beater for Wimbourne Wasps, betting enthusiast) and Barty Crouch Sr. (Head of Dept of International Magical Cooperation, speaks ~150-200 languages, strict/formal, calls Percy "Weatherby" by mistake). Fred and George make a bet with Bagman (37 Galleons, 15 Sickles, 3 Knuts) that Ireland wins but Krum gets the Snitch, plus a fake wand (rubber chicken) thrown in for 5 more Galleons. Mention of goblins wanting Barty Crouch to find an interpreter; Bertha Jorkins still missing, Bagman unconcerned.
- Characters: Mr. Roberts (Muggle site manager), Ludo Bagman, Barty Crouch Sr., Fred & George, Percy ("Weatherby"), Seamus Finnigan & his mother, Dean Thomas, Ali Bashir (mentioned, flying carpet smuggler), Cuthbert Mockridge (Head of Goblin Liaison Office), Gilbert Wimple (Committee on Experimental Charms, has horns), Arnold Peasegood (Obliviator), Bode and Croaker (Department of Mysteries "Unspeakables"), Cho Chang (Ravenclaw Seeker, waves at Harry), Oliver Wood (ex-Gryffindor Captain, now Puddlemere United reserve), Ernie Macmillan (Hufflepuff), old wizard "Archie" in a flowered nightgown.
- Places/objects: Campsite, Salem Witches' Institute banner, Omnioculars, shamrock-covered tents, Bulgarian flag tents with Krum posters.
- Numbers: Omnioculars cost 10 Galleons each; bet amount 37 Galleons 15 Sickles 3 Knuts + 5 Galleons for fake wand.
- Obscure: Krum is "only just eighteen"; Bill once had a Brazilian pen-friend who sent a cursed hat that shriveled ears; Percy bows so low to Fudge later his glasses break (next chapter).

## Chapter 8: The Quidditch World Cup
- Plot: The match itself — Ireland vs Bulgaria, 422nd Quidditch World Cup Final. Top Box seating; meet house-elf Winky (Crouch's elf, saving his seat, terrified of heights). Minister Cornelius Fudge greets Harry; Lucius, Narcissa, and Draco Malfoy arrive and sit nearby (tension with Mr. Weasley). Veela (Bulgarian mascots) perform; Irish leprechaun mascots make it "rain" gold. Players introduced: Bulgaria — Dimitrov, Ivanova, Zograf, Levski, Vulchanov, Volkov, Krum; Ireland — Connolly, Ryan, Troy, Mullet, Moran, Quigley, Lynch. Referee: Hassan Mostafa (Egyptian). Match details: Krum performs the "Wronski Feint" on Lynch (who crashes twice); Ireland leads but Krum catches the Snitch anyway to end it on his terms — final score Bulgaria 160, Ireland 170 — Ireland wins the Cup but Krum gets Snitch.
- Characters: Winky (house-elf), Cornelius Fudge (Minister of Magic), Lucius/Narcissa/Draco Malfoy, Ludo Bagman (commentator), all named players above, Hassan Mostafa (referee).
- Places/objects: Top Box, Golden Snitch, Quaffle, Bludgers, giant stadium (seats 100,000), scoreboard ads (Bluebottle broom, Mrs. Skower's Stain Remover, Bertie Bott's Every Flavor Beans, Gladrags Wizardwear).
- Numbers: 422nd Quidditch World Cup; stadium seats 100,000; Ministry task force of 500 built it; final score Ireland 170 – Bulgaria 160; Ireland leads 10-0 early, then 30-0, then 100-10, then 130-10.
- Obscure: Dobby is mentioned via Winky's recognition of Harry (Winky knows "Dobby" talks of Harry constantly); Mostafa gets distracted by dancing veela and has to be kicked in shins by a mediwizard; Krum's nose is broken by a Bludger from Quigley; leprechauns spell "HA HA HA" and a rude gesture in the air; veela turn into bird-like creatures when angry.

## Chapter 9: The Dark Mark
- Plot: That night, Death Eaters (masked wizards, revealed as such retroactively) riot through the campsite, levitating and tormenting the Roberts Muggle family. Harry, Ron, Hermione flee into the woods; encounter Draco Malfoy (taunts them, implies his father is one of the masked men, uses the word "Mudblood" about Hermione). Harry loses his wand. They meet Ludo Bagman (dazed, Disapparates when told of riot) and overhear drunk wizards (incl. Stan Shunpike of the Knight Bus) boasting. The Dark Mark (Voldemort's symbol: skull with snake tongue) is conjured in the sky by an unknown person after someone shouts "Morsmordre!" — causing panic (it hasn't been seen in 13 years). Ministry wizards (including Mr. Weasley, Mr. Crouch, Amos Diggory) surround Harry, Ron, Hermione, suspecting them. Winky the house-elf is found nearby holding Harry's wand (which was used to conjure the Mark); Prior Incantato reveals the wand's last spell was the Dark Mark. Crouch, humiliated, sacks Winky on the spot despite her innocence (house-elves can be freed only by being given clothes). Term "Death Eaters" explained by Bill as Voldemort's followers.
- Characters: Draco Malfoy, Winky, Barty Crouch Sr., Amos Diggory, Mr. Weasley, Bill, Charlie, Percy, Ludo Bagman, Stan Shunpike (Knight Bus conductor).
- Places/objects: The Dark Mark (skull + snake), Morsmordre (spell to conjure it), Prior Incantato spell, "Deletrius" (counter-spell to dispel the shadow image), Beauxbatons students mentioned (French-speaking girl asking about Madame Maxime).
- Numbers: Dark Mark "hasn't been seen for thirteen years"; roughly 20 Ministry wizards surround the trio; Winky's clothing = freedom (house-elf law).
- Obscure: Goblins seen laughing over a sack of betting gold, unbothered by the riot; the term "Death Eaters" is introduced here by Bill; Mr. Weasley explains the terror the Mark used to inspire.

## Chapter 10: Mayhem at the Ministry
- Plot: Morning after; Mr. Roberts has had memory modified ("Merry Christmas" said out of season). Daily Prophet (Rita Skeeter byline) reports "Scenes of Terror at the Quidditch World Cup," criticizing Ministry security; doesn't name Mr. Weasley but quotes him. Mr. Weasley and Percy must go into work despite holiday. Harry confides in Ron and Hermione about his scar hurting and the dream (Voldemort/Wormtail plotting a murder) and recalls Professor Trelawney's end-of-last-year prophecy that "the Dark Lord will rise again... because his servant was going to go back to him." The Weasley grandfather clock (nine hands, no numerals — shows "home/school/work/traveling/lost/hospital/prison/mortal peril") is described. Packing for term; Ron's hideous secondhand maroon dress robes with lace revealed (new Hogwarts requirement this year); Harry's bottle-green dress robes (new).
- Characters: Mrs. Weasley, Mr. Weasley, Percy, Ron, Hermione, Ginny, Fred, George, Rita Skeeter (byline only).
- Places/objects: Grandfather clock with nine hands, Standard Book of Spells Grade 4 (Miranda Goshawk), dress robes (new Hogwarts requirement), Daily Prophet headline "SCENES OF TERROR AT THE QUIDDITCH WORLD CUP."
- Numbers: Nine Weasley family clock hands.
- Obscure: Mundungus Fletcher's fraudulent compensation claim for a "twelve-bedroomed tent with en-suite Jacuzzi" (he actually slept under a cloak on sticks); Dudley/Ton-Tongue Toffee referenced again.

## Chapter 11: Aboard the Hogwarts Express
- Plot: Amos Diggory's head appears in the fire reporting Mad-Eye Moody set off "exploding dustbins," fearing an intruder; Mr. Weasley rushes off to smooth it over with Improper Use of Magic office. Journey to King's Cross via Muggle taxis; onto Platform 9¾ and Hogwarts Express. Mrs. Weasley hints at a big secret ("you'll find out this evening... it's going to be very exciting... they've changed the rules"). On the train, they overhear Malfoy telling Crabbe/Goyle that his father considered sending him to Durmstrang (which teaches Dark Arts openly) instead of Hogwarts. Discussion of Durmstrang and Beauxbatons as other wizarding schools (Hogwarts is also hidden/Unplottable per "Hogwarts, A History"). Neville Longbottom, Seamus, Dean visit; miniature Krum figurine. Malfoy taunts Ron about entering "the tournament" for money and glory — first mention of the event to the trio.
- Characters: Amos Diggory (head in fire), Mad-Eye Moody (mentioned, new DADA teacher, ex-Auror, paranoid), Bill, Charlie, Fred, George, Neville Longbottom, Seamus Finnigan, Dean Thomas, Draco Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle.
- Places/objects: King's Cross, Platform 9¾, Hogwarts Express, "Hogwarts, A History" (book), Durmstrang (far north, cold, fur capes), Beauxbatons.
- Numbers: None major.
- Obscure: Hagrid greets them at Hogsmeade station in pouring rain; mention that first-years cross the lake with Hagrid.

## Chapter 12: The Triwizard Tournament
- Plot: Arrival at Hogwarts in a storm; Peeves drops water balloons in the entrance hall, scolded by Professor McGonagall. Sorting Hat song (new song each year) explains house values: Gryffindor (brave), Ravenclaw (clever), Hufflepuff (hard-working), Slytherin (ambitious). New first years sorted (named: Stewart Ackerley-Ravenclaw, Malcolm Baddock-Slytherin, Eleanor Branstone-Hufflepuff, Owen Cauldwell-Hufflepuff, Dennis Creevey-Gryffindor (Colin's younger brother, fell in the lake, delighted), Emma Dobbs, Laura Madley-Hufflepuff, Natalie McDonald-Gryffindor, Graham Pritchard-Slytherin, Orla Quirke-Ravenclaw, Kevin Whitby-Hufflepuff). Dumbledore announces: no Inter-House Quidditch Cup this year; instead the Triwizard Tournament returns after a century, hosted by Hogwarts, with Beauxbatons and Durmstrang. Age restriction: only students 17+ may enter. Mysterious hooded stranger arrives mid-speech during a thunderstorm: revealed as "Mad-Eye" Moody — new DADA professor, heavily scarred, magical rotating blue eye, wooden leg. Fred and George vow to find a way around the age line.
- Characters: Peeves, Professor McGonagall, Professor Dumbledore, Nearly Headless Nick, Hagrid, Colin & Dennis Creevey, Professor Flitwick, Professor Sprout, Professor Sinistra, Professor Snape, Mad-Eye Moody (introduced), Fred & George, the newly sorted first years (named above), Cho Chang (mentioned).
- Places/objects: Sorting Hat, Great Hall decorations, Bloody Baron (Slytherin ghost), Fat Lady portrait (password "Balderdash").
- Numbers: Triwizard Tournament established ~700 years ago; age restriction = 17+; champion prize = 1,000 Galleons personal prize money + Triwizard Cup + school glory; tournament held once every 5 years historically (discontinued after death toll); Gryffindor has won Inter-House Championship 3 years running.
- Obscure: Filch's list of banned objects now has 437 items (Screaming Yo-yos, Fanged Frisbees, Ever-Bashing Boomerangs added); Dumbledore's joke about a troll/hag/leprechaun walking into a bar is interrupted by McGonagall.

## Chapter 13: Mad-Eye Moody
- Plot: First day of classes. Fred/George/Lee Jordan discuss Aging Potion schemes to enter the Tournament underage. Herbology: Bubotubers (squeeze for valuable pus, cures acne; mention of Eloise Midgen who tried to curse her acne off). Care of Magical Creatures: Hagrid introduces Blast-Ended Skrewts (newly hatched, unidentified creature, smell of rotting fish, sting/blast ends, no visible heads) — Malfoy mocks them. Divination: Professor Trelawney predicts doom for Harry as usual ("born under the baleful influence of Saturn" — though Harry was actually born in July, not midwinter as she guesses). At dinner, Draco Malfoy reads aloud a Daily Prophet article (Rita Skeeter) mocking Arthur Weasley ("Arnold Weasley") over the Mad-Eye Moody "dustbins" incident. A fight nearly breaks out; Moody intervenes, transfigures Draco Malfoy into a white ferret and bounces him around the entrance hall ("the amazing bouncing ferret") until Professor McGonagall stops him and un-transfigures Malfoy, scolding Moody for using Transfiguration as punishment.
- Characters: Lee Jordan, Fred & George, Hagrid, Professor Sprout, Hannah Abbott, Eloise Midgen (mentioned), Professor Trelawney, Draco Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle, Mad-Eye Moody, Professor McGonagall.
- Places/objects: Greenhouse 3, Bubotuber pus, Blast-Ended Skrewts, Daily Prophet article "FURTHER MISTAKES AT THE MINISTRY OF MAGIC."
- Numbers: ~100 Blast-Ended Skrewts per crate initially, each ~6 inches at hatching.
- Obscure: Moody says Snape is "an old friend" he's been looking forward to seeing; the ferret bounces repeatedly as punishment for a cowardly attack from behind.

## Chapter 14: The Unforgivable Curses
- Plot: Moody's first full DADA lesson: demonstrates the three Unforgivable Curses on spiders — Imperius Curse (total control, makes spider dance), Cruciatus Curse (torture — Neville reacts with visible horror, implying personal connection), Avada Kedavra (the Killing Curse — "There's no countercurse... Only one known person has ever survived it, and he's sitting right in front of me" — Harry). Harry recalls his parents' deaths in detail (James tried to hold off Voldemort, Lily begged to be killed instead of Harry, then was murdered; Voldemort's curse rebounded off Harry). Later, Moody privately comforts Neville, lends him a book (Magical Water Plants of the Mediterranean — will matter later for gillyweed). That evening Ron and Harry invent fake Divination predictions of doom for homework; Hermione founds S.P.E.W. (Society for the Promotion of Elfish Welfare — "Stop the Outrageous Abuse of Our Fellow Magical Creatures and Campaign for a Change in Their Legal Status" is the actual slogan, S.P.E.W. is the short name); badges cost 2 Sickles; Ron made treasurer, Harry secretary. Hedwig returns with Sirius's reply: Sirius is "flying north immediately" having heard rumors, alarmed by Harry's scar pain. Harry panics and (next chapter) sends a retraction lie to stop Sirius' return.
- Characters: Mad-Eye Moody, Neville Longbottom, Fred & George (working secretly on something — later revealed to be the Ludo Bagman blackmail letter), Hermione (founds S.P.E.W.), Sirius Black (letter).
- Places/objects: The three Unforgivable Curses (Imperius, Cruciatus, Avada Kedavra), S.P.E.W. badges, "Unfogging the Future" (Divination textbook), "Magical Water Plants of the Mediterranean" (book Moody lends Neville).
- Numbers: S.P.E.W. membership fee = 2 Sickles; life sentence in Azkaban is the penalty for using an Unforgivable Curse on a human.
- Obscure: Neville volunteers information in class for only the second time ever (usually only in Herbology); it is hinted (not yet stated) that Neville's parents were tortured with Cruciatus — revealed properly in Ch.30.

## Chapter 15: Beauxbatons and Durmstrang
- Plot: Harry sends a (false) retraction letter to Sirius claiming he "imagined" the scar pain, to stop Sirius from returning — Hermione calls this a lie. Weeks pass; Moody teaches the class to resist the Imperius Curse (Harry is the only one who can throw it off almost entirely after repeated attempts — "They'll have trouble controlling you!"). Increased academic workload across subjects (O.W.L. prep mentioned for 5th year, though they're in 4th). Hagrid proposes students help "observe" the growing Skrewts. Sign posted: Beauxbatons and Durmstrang delegations arrive Friday 30 October at 6pm. On arrival day: Madame Maxime (headmistress of Beauxbatons, as large as Hagrid, arrives via giant flying powder-blue carriage pulled by a dozen palomino winged horses the size of elephants, each needing single-malt whisky); Professor Igor Karkaroff (headmaster of Durmstrang, silver furs, goatee) and his ship (rises from the lake) arrive with students including Viktor Krum. Karkaroff recognizes Harry's scar; Mad-Eye Moody and Karkaroff have a hostile history (Moody caught Karkaroff, a former Death Eater, mentioned implicitly).
- Characters: Madame Maxime, Professor Karkaroff, Viktor Krum, Sirius (letter), Moody.
- Places/objects: Beauxbatons carriage (crest: two crossed golden wands, each emitting three stars), Durmstrang ship (skeletal, portholes), palomino winged horses, single-malt whisky (horse feed).
- Numbers: Beauxbatons delegation ~12 students; arrival at 6 pm Oct 30.
- Obscure: Harry sneaks out under Invisibility Cloak to Hogsmeade with Hermione; Moody can see through Invisibility Cloaks with his magical eye; Hagrid asks Harry to meet him at midnight — leads to the dragons reveal next chapter (technically start of Ch.19 events narratively but the dragon enclosure visit is told within Ch.19, not 15 — note: actually the dragon viewing happens in Chapter 19, "The Hungarian Horntail," not 15; corrected below).

## Chapter 16: The Goblet of Fire
- Plot: Welcoming feast with foreign students; Beauxbatons sit at Ravenclaw table, Durmstrang at Slytherin table (Krum's fan club forms). Fleur Delacour (part-Veela, Beauxbatons) is introduced informally serving food. Dumbledore introduces Barty Crouch and Ludo Bagman as tournament organizers/judges (alongside Dumbledore, Karkaroff, Maxime). The Goblet of Fire (wooden cup with blue-white flames) is unveiled; rules explained: name + school on parchment dropped in within 24 hours; Age Line (drawn by Dumbledore) prevents under-17s; binding magical contract once chosen. That night, Fred and George attempt to cross the Age Line using an aging potion — fail spectacularly, thrown out and grow instant white beards (along with two other students, Fawcett of Ravenclaw and Summers of Hufflepuff, who did the same). Next morning: candidates submit names; Angelina Johnson (Gryffindor Chaser, just turned 17) enters. At the Halloween selection: Goblet names Viktor Krum (Durmstrang), Fleur Delacour (Beauxbatons), Cedric Diggory (Hogwarts) — then unexpectedly also "Harry Potter," despite being underage, shocking everyone.
- Characters: Fleur Delacour, Viktor Krum, Cedric Diggory, Angelina Johnson, Fred & George (failed aging potion attempt), Lee Jordan, Barty Crouch, Ludo Bagman, Karkaroff, Madame Maxime, Dumbledore.
- Places/objects: Goblet of Fire, Age Line, bouillabaisse (French dish), the casket (jeweled wooden chest).
- Numbers: Age Line requires 17+; prize 1,000 Galleons; champions chosen at Halloween; three tasks across the school year.
- Obscure: Rita Skeeter's quill picked up later — not yet introduced; Dumbledore jokes about a "beautifully proportioned room" containing chamber pots he found by accident (early Room of Requirement reference).

## Chapter 17: The Four Champions
- Plot: Harry, shocked, is sent to the champions' chamber; Fleur, Cedric, Krum doubt/dismiss his presence as impossible since he's underage. Ludo Bagman confirms Harry's name genuinely came from the Goblet. Dumbledore, Crouch, Karkaroff, Maxime, McGonagall, Snape, Moody convene; heated argument — Karkaroff and Maxime protest a "fourth champion"; Snape blames Harry for rule-breaking; Moody argues someone put Harry's name in under a fourth, fake school name via a powerful Confundus Charm, intending to kill him via the tournament. Crouch declares the magical contract binding — Harry must compete. First task announced for Nov 24, testing "daring"; champions get no end-of-year exams. That night, Ron doesn't believe Harry didn't enter himself; big rift begins between Harry and Ron.
- Characters: Ludo Bagman, Barty Crouch, Karkaroff, Madame Maxime, McGonagall, Snape, Moody, Dumbledore, Cedric, Fleur, Krum, Ron (rift begins).
- Numbers: First task date = November 24th.
- Obscure: Portraits in the chamber (wizened witch "Violet," friend of the Fat Lady) gossip about the news and race through paintings to spread it to Gryffindor Tower before Harry arrives.

## Chapter 18: The Weighing of the Wands

Reaching the Triwizard Cup together, both refuse to take individual credit and agree to grab it simultaneously, making it a tied Hogwarts victory — but the Cup is a Portkey, instantly transporting both of them away.
- Characters: Ron, Hermione, Sirius (by owl), Draco Malfoy, Rita Skeeter, Amos Diggory, Cedric's parents, Krum's parents, Fleur's mother, Gabrielle Delacour, Mrs. Weasley, Bill Weasley, Professor McGonagall, Professor Moody, Hagrid, Professor Flitwick (maze patrollers), Ludo Bagman (commentator), Cornelius Fudge (fifth judge, replacing absent Crouch), Viktor Krum (Imperiused by fake-Moody, unknowingly), Cedric Diggory, the Sphinx.
- Places/objects: The maze (20-foot hedges), the Triwizard Cup (revealed as a Portkey), Sphinx riddle.
- Numbers: Standings before task 3: Harry & Cedric tied at 85 points, Krum 80, Fleur third (unspecified exact score). Task date: June 24th, dusk.
- Obscure: Percy is "under a lot of strain," being questioned about whether Crouch's owl instructions are genuinely from him; Fudge fills in as 5th judge since Crouch is officially still missing/absent.

## Chapter 32: Flesh, Blood, and Bone
- Plot: The Portkey (Triwizard Cup) deposits Harry and Cedric in a dark graveyard near a hill with an old house — Little Hangleton (location from Ch.1, though not named again here directly). A hooded figure (Peter Pettigrew/Wormtail) approaches carrying a bundle. Harry's scar explodes in pain. A cold voice orders "Kill the spare" — Wormtail/another casts Avada Kedavra and Cedric Diggory is instantly killed. Wormtail binds Harry to a headstone marked "TOM RIDDLE" (Voldemort's father's grave) and gags him. A dark magical ritual follows: a large stone cauldron is prepared; the bundle is revealed to be a hideous, barely-formed infant-like Voldemort (reduced to this state since his first downfall); it's lowered into the potion. Wormtail performs the resurrection spell with three ingredients: "Bone of the father, unknowingly given" (dust from Tom Riddle Sr.'s grave), "Flesh of the servant, willingly given" (Wormtail cuts off his own hand with a dagger), "Blood of the enemy, forcibly taken" (Wormtail cuts Harry's arm, collects blood). Lord Voldemort rises from the cauldron in a new body, fully restored.
- Characters: Peter Pettigrew/Wormtail, Cedric Diggory (killed), Lord Voldemort (resurrection), Tom Riddle Sr. (grave).
- Places/objects: The graveyard, Tom Riddle's gravestone, the stone cauldron, the resurrection ritual (three ingredients).
- Numbers: Three ingredients for the potion (bone, flesh, blood).
- Obscure: Wormtail's missing finger (cut off himself in year 3 to fake his own death) is referenced as he reaches with "the hand with the missing finger"; the infant-Voldemort form has red eyes, snake-like flat face, is described as "ugly, slimy, and blind."

## Chapter 33: The Death Eaters
- Plot: Voldemort (now restored, white skull-like face, red slit-pupiled eyes, flat snake-like nose) examines his new body, rewards Wormtail with a magical silver hand (replacing his cut-off hand) for his service, then summons the Death Eaters by touching Wormtail's Dark Mark. Death Eaters Apparate in a circle (masked, hooded); many gaps remain where absent/dead/imprisoned members should stand. Voldemort addresses them bitterly for their disloyalty during his 13-year absence, punishes Avery with Cruciatus for groveling, discusses the Lestranges (still loyal, imprisoned in Azkaban, to be freed "beyond their dreams"), addresses Lucius Malfoy (chastises him for not searching harder), Macnair, Crabbe, Goyle (Sr.), Nott. Reveals that "one faithful servant" at Hogwarts arranged for Harry's capture (not yet named to the Death Eaters or reader, though it is Barty Crouch Jr. disguised as Moody — confirmed next chapter). Voldemort narrates his own history at length: how his father (Muggle, Tom Riddle Sr.) abandoned his witch mother (who died giving birth to him in a Muggle orphanage); how he was reduced to "less than spirit" after his first attempt to kill Harry failed (rebounded curse); hid in Albania; possessed Professor Quirrell in Harry's first year (attempt to steal the Sorcerer's Stone, failed); nearly gave up hope until Wormtail found him, with Bertha Jorkins' capture and interrogation providing crucial information about the Triwizard Tournament and a "faithful Death Eater" at Hogwarts; explains how Harry's blood was needed specifically to overcome his mother's protective magic; describes how the Goblet of Fire scheme worked (his spy ensured Harry's entry and made the Cup a Portkey). Voldemort then tortures Harry with Cruciatus to demonstrate his power before their duel.
- Characters: Lord Voldemort, Wormtail, Lucius Malfoy, Macnair, Crabbe (Sr.), Goyle (Sr.), Nott, Avery, the Lestranges (mentioned, imprisoned), Bertha Jorkins (backstory, killed by Voldemort after interrogation), Professor Quirrell (mentioned, year-1 possession backstory), Frank Bryce (mentioned, "my late father" killed — Voldemort reveals he killed his own Muggle father Tom Riddle Sr. and grandparents).
- Places/objects: The Dark Mark (used to summon Death Eaters), the silver hand (given to Wormtail).
- Numbers: "Thirteen years" since Death Eaters last gathered; six Death Eaters noted as "missing" from the circle (3 dead in his service, 1 too cowardly, 1 who left forever, 1 [Crouch Jr.] "already reentered my service").
- Obscure: Voldemort explicitly confirms he killed his own father and grandparents (the Ch.1 murders) for revenge after being abandoned; the "faithful servant" at Hogwarts is dramatically teased without being named in this chapter.

## Chapter 34: Priori Incantatem
- Plot: Voldemort unties Harry, returns his wand, insists on a formal duel ("the niceties must be observed... Bow to death, Harry"), forces Harry to bow via spell, then uses Cruciatus again and the Imperius Curse (commanding Harry to say "no" to a taunt — Harry resists, shouting "I WON'T!"). Voldemort attempts Avada Kedavra as Harry casts Expelliarmus simultaneously — the two wands' twin phoenix-feather cores (both from Dumbledore's phoenix Fawkes) cause "Priori Incantatem" (the Reverse Spell effect): a golden thread connects the wands, they're lifted into the air inside a dome of light, and phoenix song (from the connection itself) bolsters Harry. Forcing the effect, "echoes" of Voldemort's most recent victims emerge in reverse order from his wand: Cedric Diggory, an old man (a Muggle, unnamed, killed to test a curse), Bertha Jorkins, Harry's mother Lily, and finally Harry's father James. These spectral echoes advise Harry to hold on and then, when the connection breaks, to run for the Cup/Portkey while they briefly shield him from Voldemort. Harry breaks the connection, dodges curses from the Death Eaters, grabs Cedric's body, Summons the Triwizard Cup ("Accio!") and escapes via Portkey with Cedric's body, just evading Voldemort's final strike.
- Characters: Lord Voldemort, Harry, the "echoes" (Cedric Diggory, unnamed old man, Bertha Jorkins, Lily Potter, James Potter), Death Eaters (background).
- Places/objects: Priori Incantatem (spell-reversal phenomenon), twin phoenix-feather wand cores (Fawkes), the golden "cage of light," the Triwizard Cup (Portkey).
- Numbers: Five spectral "echoes" emerge in total (Cedric, old man, Bertha Jorkins, Lily, James) — in reverse chronological order of the wand's killings.
- Obscure: The "old man" echo (unnamed) tells Harry "He was a real wizard, then?... Killed me, that one did"; Harry's parents' shadows explicitly instruct him on the escape plan.

## Chapter 35: Veritaserum
- Plot: Harry and Cedric's body land back at Hogwarts maze edge; chaos as Dumbledore, Fudge, and the crowd realize Cedric is dead and Harry claims "He's back... Voldemort." Mad-Eye Moody carries Harry to his office, questions him intensely about events — revealed to be suspiciously eager for details. Harry recounts the graveyard events; when he mentions a "Death Eater at Hogwarts," Moody calmly reveals HE is that Death Eater — he put Harry's name in the Goblet of Fire (under a fake fourth school), manipulated events throughout the year (tipped Hagrid to show the dragons, had Cedric pass on the egg-bath clue, planted the gillyweed book with Neville, Stunned Fleur and placed Krum under Imperius during the final task) — all to deliver Harry to Voldemort and then kill him personally to win Voldemort's favor. He is revealed to be Barty Crouch Jr. (the son believed dead, in Polyjuice-Potion disguise as Moody) — raises his wand to finish Harry off, but Dumbledore, Snape, and McGonagall burst in and Stun him just in time. The real (frail, imprisoned, starved) Mad-Eye Moody is found locked in a hidden compartment of the magical trunk; the imposter (as Polyjuice wears off) is revealed as young Barty Crouch Jr. Under Veritaserum (Truth Potion), administered by Dumbledore, Crouch Jr. confesses his full history: his dying mother swapped places with him via Polyjuice to free him from Azkaban (she died in his place, disguised as him, in Azkaban); his father kept him hidden/controlled via the Imperius Curse and the house-elf Winky as caretaker; Bertha Jorkins discovered him accidentally and was Memory-Charmed (too strongly, damaging her mind) by Crouch Sr.; Winky convinced Crouch Sr. to let him attend the World Cup under the Invisibility Cloak, where he briefly broke free of the Imperius, stole Harry's wand, and later cast the Dark Mark; Voldemort (via the tortured, broken Bertha Jorkins) learned Crouch Jr. was alive and freed him by placing Crouch Sr. under Imperius in his place; Crouch Jr. then went to Hogwarts, helped Wormtail ambush and imprison the real Moody, used Polyjuice (with Moody's hair) to impersonate him all year, planted ingredients and clues to guide Harry, and finally killed his own father (who had briefly escaped and was heading to confess to Dumbledore) when the father tried to intervene, hiding the body (transfigured into a bone) buried in front of Hagrid's cabin.
- Characters: Mad-Eye Moody (impostor, revealed as Barty Crouch Jr.), the real Alastor Moody (found in trunk, starved), Winky, Snape, McGonagall, Dumbledore, Cornelius Fudge (background), Barty Crouch Sr. (murdered, confirmed).
- Places/objects: Polyjuice Potion (hip flask), the seven-locked magical trunk, Veritaserum (Truth Potion), the Foe-Glass, Secrecy Sensor, Sneakoscope (Moody's Dark Detectors).
- Numbers: Crouch's trunk has seven locks/compartments; Veritaserum dosed as three drops (mentioned earlier by Snape in Ch.27 as the threatening amount).
- Obscure: Crouch Jr. mentions he and Harry share a first name coincidence point (Barty/Harry's note about "my father hated Dark wizards" ruse); the real Moody's wooden leg and magical eye are found removed/lying on his bedside table once rescued.

## Chapter 36: The Parting of the Ways
- Plot: Dumbledore brings Harry to his office, where Sirius (as Snuffles, having come at Dumbledore's earlier summons) is waiting and transforms back to human. Harry recounts the full graveyard story to Dumbledore and Sirius, aided/comforted by Fawkes the phoenix (whose tears heal Harry's spider-bite wound). Dumbledore explains the Priori Incantatem phenomenon (twin-core wands) and confirms the "echoes" were not resurrection but reversed-spell echoes retaining victims' characters. They move to the hospital wing; Mrs. Weasley, Ron, Hermione, Bill are there. A confrontation erupts: Cornelius Fudge, having brought a Dementor into the castle for "protection," had it administer the fatal "Kiss" to the captured Barty Crouch Jr. (soul destroyed) before he could testify to the Ministry — enraging McGonagall and Dumbledore (no formal proof now exists). Fudge refuses to believe Voldemort has returned, dismissing Harry (influenced by Rita Skeeter's "disturbed" article) and Crouch Jr. as unreliable; a dramatic ideological break occurs between Dumbledore and Fudge ("we have reached a parting of the ways"). Snape dramatically reveals his own Dark Mark to Fudge as proof, explaining its renewed clarity, and that Karkaroff fled upon feeling it burn. Fudge leaves unconvinced, delivering Harry's 1,000-Galleon prize winnings. Dumbledore organizes immediate countermeasures: tells Mrs. Weasley the Order's "old crowd" must be alerted; sends Bill to warn Arthur Weasley discreetly; sends Sirius to alert Remus Lupin, Arabella Figg, and Mundungus Fletcher, to "lie low at Lupin's"; sends Snape on an unspecified dangerous mission (implied to be returning to spy among Death Eaters) — the two historic enemies, Sirius and Snape, are made to shake hands at Dumbledore's insistence.
- Characters: Sirius Black, Fawkes, Mrs. Weasley, Ron, Hermione, Bill Weasley, Cornelius Fudge, Professor McGonagall, Severus Snape, Madam Pomfrey, Remus Lupin (named, not present), Arabella Figg (named, not present), Mundungus Fletcher (named, not present).
- Places/objects: Dementor's Kiss (administered to Crouch Jr. — "worse than dead," soul sucked out), the hospital wing, Fawkes's healing tears, the 1,000-Galleon prize bag.
- Numbers: Prize money = 1,000 Galleons.
- Obscure: This chapter contains the first explicit mention by name of Remus Lupin, Arabella Figg, and Mundungus Fletcher as part of Dumbledore's trusted network (the future "Order of the Phoenix," unnamed here); Dumbledore's famous speech about valuing "what someone grows to be" over "purity of blood."

## Chapter 37: The Beginning
- Plot: Aftermath — Harry meets the grieving Diggorys (they don't blame him; thank him for returning Cedric's body); tries to give them the prize money, they decline. Harry returns to classes; most students avoid him, some believe Skeeter's "disturbed" narrative. At the Leaving Feast, the Great Hall is decorated with black drapes (mourning Cedric) rather than house colors. Dumbledore gives a landmark speech: publicly announces (against Ministry wishes) that Cedric Diggory was murdered by Lord Voldemort, praises Harry's courage, toasts "Cedric Diggory," stresses inter-school unity against Voldemort's return, and delivers the famous "Remember Cedric Diggory" charge to choose "what is right" over "what is easy." Final goodbyes: Fleur Delacour says goodbye to Harry (hopes to work in Britain to improve her English) and shakes Ron's hand, charming him; Krum says goodbye to Hermione privately and tells Harry he liked Cedric; reveals Karkaroff fled his ship/post, abandoning his students to crew it themselves. On the train home, Hermione reveals her discovery: Rita Skeeter is an unregistered Animagus who turns into a beetle to spy (explaining all her "impossible" scoops); Hermione has trapped her in an unbreakable jar and blackmails her into not writing for a year. Malfoy, Crabbe, and Goyle taunt the trio in their compartment and are promptly hexed unconscious by Harry, Ron, Hermione, Fred, and George together. Fred and George reveal (finally) that the person they were "blackmailing" was Ludo Bagman, who had paid their World Cup betting winnings in leprechaun gold (which vanished overnight) and then refused to pay them back in real gold; revealed Bagman is deeply in debt to goblins from gambling, bet heavily (and illegally, as a judge) on Harry to win outright, and had to flee after Harry only tied with Cedric rather than winning outright. At King's Cross, Harry gives Fred and George his full 1,000-Galleon Triwizard winnings to fund their joke shop, on condition they buy Ron new dress robes. Harry returns to the Dursleys with Mrs. Weasley's promise that he'll join the Weasleys again later in summer. Final line: "...he would have to meet it when it did."
- Characters: Amos & Mrs. Diggory, Dumbledore, Fleur Delacour, Viktor Krum, Hermione (Rita Skeeter reveal), Draco Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle, Fred & George (Bagman blackmail reveal), Mrs. Weasley, Uncle Vernon.
- Places/objects: The Leaving Feast (black drapes, no house-color décor), Rita Skeeter's Animagus beetle form (trapped in an Unbreakable-Charmed glass jar).
- Numbers: Harry gives away his full 1,000 Galleons prize to Fred and George for their joke shop (Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes).
- Obscure: Krum reveals Karkaroff "stayed in his cabin and let us do the vork" steering the ship home; Fred and George's successful product so far is the "Canary Creams"; the chapter confirms the Daily Prophet suppressed the truth about Cedric's death and Voldemort's return under Ministry pressure — only a small piece on Harry "winning" the tournament was printed, no mention of Cedric.

# Cross-Chapter Facts (for franchise-wide ledger)

**Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher this year:** "Mad-Eye" Moody (Alastor Moody) — but revealed at year's end to have been an impostor the entire time: Barty Crouch Jr. using Polyjuice Potion to impersonate the real Moody, who was imprisoned (starved, missing leg/eye in the real sense — his wooden leg and magical eye were literally removed and used by the impostor) inside a hidden compartment of his own enchanted seven-locked trunk for most of the school year. Fake-Moody taught the class the three Unforgivable Curses (demonstrated on spiders) and how to resist the Imperius Curse.

**Quidditch World Cup:** Final match is Ireland vs Bulgaria (422nd Quidditch World Cup). Final score: Ireland 170 – Bulgaria 160. Krum (Bulgaria's Seeker, age 18) catches the Golden Snitch but Bulgaria still loses on points; Krum chooses to end the match on his own terms rather than let Ireland extend its lead further. Players: Ireland — Connolly, Ryan, Troy, Mullet, Moran, Quigley, Lynch (Seeker, Aidan Lynch, knocked out twice by Krum's Wronski Feint). Bulgaria — Dimitrov, Ivanova, Zograf, Levski, Vulchanov, Volkov, Krum. Referee: Hassan Mostafa (Egyptian). Mascots: Bulgaria brings veela; Ireland brings leprechauns (rain real gold, which vanishes). That night, Death Eaters (unmasked identity unconfirmed in text except implied Lucius Malfoy) riot, torment the Roberts Muggle family, and the Dark Mark is cast in the sky by Barty Crouch Jr. (using Harry's stolen wand) — first appearance of the Mark in 13 years.

**Triwizard Tournament details:**
- Participating schools: Hogwarts, Beauxbatons (headmistress Madame Maxime), Durmstrang (headmaster Igor Karkaroff).
- Champions: Viktor Krum (Durmstrang), Fleur Delacour (Beauxbatons), Cedric Diggory (Hogwarts), and — unprecedented 4th champion — Harry Potter (underage, entered by Barty Crouch Jr. via a rigged Goblet of Fire, impersonating Mad-Eye Moody).
- Prize: 1,000 Galleons personal prize money + the Triwizard Cup + house/school glory; champions are exempt from end-of-year exams.
- Judges: Albus Dumbledore, Igor Karkaroff, Madame Maxime, Ludo Bagman (Head of Dept of Magical Games & Sports), and Barty Crouch Sr. (Head of Dept of International Magical Cooperation) — Crouch is absent for most of the tournament (imprisoned/controlled/eventually murdered by his own impersonating son) and is replaced at various points by Percy Weasley and finally Cornelius Fudge as 5th judge.
- Task 1 (Nov 24): Retrieve a golden egg from a nest guarded by a real dragon. Each champion draws a miniature dragon + number from a bag determining order/opponent: Cedric/Swedish Short-Snout (#1), Fleur/Common Welsh Green (#2), Krum/Chinese Fireball (#3), Harry/Hungarian Horntail (#4). Harry summons his Firebolt broomstick to evade the Horntail. Scores: Maxime 8, Crouch 9, Dumbledore 9, Bagman 10, Karkaroff 4 (Harry 38/40 shown via description — but overall points tally: ties Harry with Krum for first at that stage).
- Task 2 (per schedule, ~Feb 24, 9:30 am): Retrieve a "hostage" each champion will "sorely miss" from the merpeople's underwater village within one hour: Ron Weasley (Harry's hostage), Hermione Granger (Krum's), Cho Chang (Cedric's), Gabrielle Delacour (Fleur's, Fleur's 8-year-old sister). Harry uses gillyweed (given by house-elf Dobby) to breathe underwater; delays past the time limit to rescue all hostages, not just Ron, after Fleur fails to arrive (attacked by grindylows). Scores: Fleur 25, Cedric 47, Krum 40, Harry 45 — standings entering Task 3: Harry and Cedric tied first at 85 points total, Krum 80, Fleur third.
- Task 3 (June 24, dusk): A living, moving 20-foot hedge maze on the Quidditch pitch containing a Blast-Ended Skrewt, a boggart-dementor, a reversed-gravity enchanted golden mist, a Sphinx with a riddle (answer: "spider"), a giant spider, and (secretly) Mad-Eye-Moody-impostor's sabotage (Imperius-cursed Krum attacks Cedric with Cruciatus; Fleur is Stunned). Harry and Cedric reach the Cup together and agree to take it jointly for a tied Hogwarts victory — but the Cup is secretly a Portkey rigged by the Crouch-Jr.-as-Moody impostor, transporting both to a graveyard where Voldemort is reborn and Cedric is murdered.

**New Weasley-family details:** Introduces Bill Weasley (eldest, works as a curse-breaker for Gringotts in Egypt, cool/rebellious style, earring with a fang, ponytail, ex-Hogwarts Head Boy) and Charlie Weasley (second-eldest, dragon-handler in Romania, stocky/freckled, brought the 4 dragons for Task 1) in person for the first time in the series. Percy Weasley becomes personal assistant to Barty Crouch Sr. at the Ministry (Department of International Magical Cooperation) after Crouch's disappearance, is briefly a stand-in 5th Triwizard judge, and is nicknamed "Weatherby" by Crouch Sr. (who frequently forgets his name). Ginny Weasley attends the Yule Ball with Neville Longbottom (her first Hogwarts dance). Fred and George Weasley spend the year developing "Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes" joke products (Ton-Tongue Toffee, Canary Creams), blackmail Ludo Bagman over unpaid gambling debts (revealed at the very end), and receive Harry's full 1,000-Galleon Triwizard winnings at the end of the book to fund their joke shop.

**Prefects/Head Boy-Girl mentioned:** Bill Weasley was Head Boy in his time at Hogwarts (mentioned by Harry). Percy Weasley was previously a prefect (established in earlier books; referenced). No new Hogwarts prefects/Head Boy-Girl are explicitly named for the current year in this book, though Cedric Diggory is confirmed to be a prefect.

**Named spells introduced in this book:** Morsmordre (conjures the Dark Mark), Priori Incantatem (the "Reverse Spell" effect occurring between twin-core wands — also the name used loosely for the spell revealing a wand's last-cast spell, demonstrated by Mr. Diggory on Harry's wand at the World Cup), Point Me (the Four-Point Spell, makes wand point north), Impedimenta (Impediment Curse), Reducto (Reductor Curse), Accio (Summoning Charm, central to Task 1), Rennervate (revives Stunned/unconscious people), Relashio (releases a grip, used against grindylows), Densaugeo (tooth-growing hex, Malfoy's curse on Hermione), Furnunculus (boil-causing curse, Harry's hex that hit Goyle), Engorgement Charm (used accidentally on Dudley's tongue via Ton-Tongue Toffee), the three Unforgivable Curses explicitly taught/named: Imperius Curse, Cruciatus Curse, Avada Kedavra (Killing Curse).

**Deaths in this book:** Cedric Diggory (murdered by Voldemort's order, "Kill the spare," cast by Wormtail/an unnamed Death Eater in the graveyard) — the central, significant death of the book. Also: the Riddle family (Tom Riddle Sr. and his Muggle parents) killed by Voldemort 50 years before the story's present (backstory, revealed fully in Ch.33). Frank Bryce (Muggle gardener) killed by Voldemort in Chapter 1. Barty Crouch Jr. is not killed outright but receives the Dementor's Kiss (soul destroyed, "worse than dead") at Cornelius Fudge's order before he can be properly tried — arguably a second major "death" of the book. Barty Crouch Sr. is murdered by his own son (Barty Crouch Jr.) near the end of the school year, his body transfigured into a bone and buried in Hagrid's pumpkin patch. Mrs. Crouch (Barty Crouch Jr.'s mother) died in Azkaban a year or so earlier, having swapped places with her son via Polyjuice Potion to free him (died in his place, disguised as him). Bertha Jorkins (Ministry witch) is killed by Voldemort after being tortured for information.

**Central antagonist/plot device of this book:** Lord Voldemort's full resurrection/rebirth, orchestrated across the entire school year by his secret agent Barty Crouch Jr. (disguised via Polyjuice Potion as the real Mad-Eye Moody). The Triwizard Tournament itself — specifically the rigged entry of underage Harry Potter into the Goblet of Fire and the sabotage of the final task so the Triwizard Cup becomes a Portkey — is the central plot device used to lure Harry to a graveyard in Little Hangleton, where Voldemort uses a dark ritual (bone of the father, flesh of the servant, blood of the enemy) to regain a full physical body, kill Cedric Diggory as an unwanted witness ("the spare"), and reassemble his Death Eaters. The book ends with Voldemort fully returned to power and the wizarding world (via the Ministry's denial under Cornelius Fudge) unprepared to face it — setting up the series' shift into open conflict.
