# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*26 questions. Mostly open, with a handful of trickier multiple-choice — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Numbers) (Open)** Including the fare itself, how many Sickles does a hot chocolate cost a passenger on the Knight Bus?

2. **(Plot) (Open)** What body part did Peter Pettigrew cut from himself to fake his own death and frame Sirius Black?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of the Weasley brothers was "the legendary" Gryffindor Seeker whose years-ago departure is blamed for the team's long Quidditch Cup drought?

4. **(Creatures) (Open)** On what date is Buckbeak's hearing before the Committee for the Disposal of Dangerous Creatures scheduled?

5. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many points had Gryffindor built up before the dementors appeared and Harry fell, during the match against Hufflepuff?

6. **(Magic) (Open)** What password opens the secret passage hidden behind the one-eyed witch statue?

7. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the street where Harry is waiting, alone at night, when the Knight Bus arrives to pick him up?

8. **(Numbers) (Open)** Besides Peter Pettigrew, how many Muggles did Sirius Black's single curse kill?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** At what time of day is Buckbeak's execution finally scheduled to take place?

10. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Complete the name of the Honeydukes gum: "Drooble's Best ___"?

11. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What was the score, Gryffindor to Slytherin, in the moments before Harry caught the Snitch in the Quidditch final?

12. **(Characters) (Open)** Who is the Slytherin Quidditch captain who fouls Angelina Johnson during the final?

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What potion, brewed monthly by Snape, does Lupin miss taking on the night he transforms in front of the group?

14. **(Places) (Open)** According to Dumbledore, exactly where is the Fat Lady hiding after her portrait is slashed?

15. **(Plot) (Open)** Roughly how long did it take James, Sirius, and Peter to master becoming Animagi, and in which school year did they finish?

16. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Ron, what is the maximum natural lifespan of an ordinary pet rat like Scabbers?

17. **(Magic)** How many hours back in time does Dumbledore instruct Hermione to take Harry, using the Time-Turner?
    A) Three  B) Two  C) Four  D) One

18. **(Places) (Open)** Counting from the right, which window of the West Tower is Sirius Black held behind?

19. **(Creatures)** Approximately how many dementors surround Harry, Hermione, and Black at the lakeshore?
    A) About twenty  B) About fifty  C) Exactly twelve  D) At least a hundred

20. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons does Hermione have to spend on an early birthday present when she buys Crookshanks?
    A) Five  B) Twenty  C) Ten  D) Fifteen

21. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many years had it been since Gryffindor last won the Quidditch Cup, before this year's final?

22. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Name the Honeydukes sweet, favored alongside Fizzing Whizbees, that is literally shaped like mice.

23. **(Characters) (Open)** Which retiring professor does Hagrid replace as the Care of Magical Creatures teacher?

24. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to the book, how long are a hippogriff's talons?

25. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Trelawney's genuine trance-prophecy during Harry's Divination exam, what is "the servant" (Pettigrew) due to do before midnight that night?

26. **(Quidditch)** By how many points does Gryffindor need to beat Slytherin in the final to win the Quidditch Cup, given Slytherin's exact 200-point lead?
    A) More than 100 points  B) More than 200 points  C) Exactly 150 points  D) More than 50 points
