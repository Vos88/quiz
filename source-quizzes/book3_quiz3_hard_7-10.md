# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*20 questions. Mostly open, with a handful of trickier multiple-choice — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons did Arthur Weasley win in the Daily Prophet Grand Prize Draw?

2. **(Numbers) (Open)** Including the fare itself, how many Sickles does a hot chocolate cost a passenger on the Knight Bus?

3. **(Plot) (Open)** Besides Peter Pettigrew, how many Muggles did Sirius Black's single curse kill?

4. **(Creatures) (Open)** On what date is Buckbeak's hearing before the Committee for the Disposal of Dangerous Creatures scheduled?

5. **(Plot) (Open)** At what time of day is Buckbeak's execution finally scheduled to take place?

6. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many points had Gryffindor built up before the dementors appeared and Harry fell, during the match against Hufflepuff?

7. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What was the score, Gryffindor to Slytherin, in the moments before Harry caught the Snitch in the Quidditch final?

8. **(Magic) (Open)** What password opens the secret passage hidden behind the one-eyed witch statue?

9. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Percy Weasley's Ravenclaw girlfriend?

10. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers works as a curse-breaker for Gringotts in Egypt?

11. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Complete the name of the Honeydukes gum: "Drooble's Best ___"?

12. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many years had it been since Gryffindor last won the Quidditch Cup, before this year's final?

13. **(Plot) (Open)** Roughly how long did it take James, Sirius, and Peter to master becoming Animagi, and in which school year did they finish?

14. **(Magic)** How many hours back in time does Dumbledore instruct Hermione to take Harry, using the Time-Turner?
    A) Three  B) Two  C) Four  D) One

15. **(Places) (Open)** Counting from the right, which window of the West Tower is Sirius Black held behind?

16. **(Creatures)** Approximately how many dementors surround Harry, Hermione, and Black at the lakeshore?
    A) At least a hundred  B) About twenty  C) About fifty  D) Exactly twelve

17. **(Magic) (Open)** What potion, brewed monthly by Snape, does Lupin miss taking on the night he transforms in front of the group?

18. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons does Hermione have to spend on an early birthday present when she buys Crookshanks?
    A) Ten  B) Five  C) Twenty  D) Fifteen

19. **(Plot) (Open)** What body part did Peter Pettigrew cut from himself to fake his own death and frame Sirius Black?

20. **(Quidditch)** By how many points does Gryffindor need to beat Slytherin in the final to win the Quidditch Cup, given Slytherin's 200-point lead?
    A) More than 200 points  B) More than 100 points  C) Exactly 150 points  D) More than 50 points
