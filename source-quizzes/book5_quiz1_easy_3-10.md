# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*20 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who becomes the new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher at Hogwarts this year?
   A) Remus Lupin  B) Mad-Eye Moody  C) Dolores Umbridge  D) Gilderoy Lockhart

2. **(Places)** What is the address of the Order of the Phoenix's secret headquarters?
   A) The Burrow  B) Number Twelve, Grimmauld Place  C) Spinner's End  D) 4 Privet Drive

3. **(Plot)** What name does the secret student Defense group founded by Harry take?
   A) The Inquisitorial Squad  B) S.P.E.W.  C) The Order of the Phoenix  D) Dumbledore's Army

4. **(Characters)** Who is made a Gryffindor prefect alongside Ron Weasley?
   A) Lavender Brown  B) Parvati Patil  C) Hermione Granger  D) Ginny Weasley

5. **(Creatures)** What are the skeletal, winged horses that pull the Hogwarts carriages, visible only to those who have witnessed death?
   A) Hippogriffs  B) Thestrals  C) Nifflers  D) Blast-Ended Skrewts

6. **(Quidditch)** What position does Ron Weasley play when he joins the Gryffindor Quidditch team this year?
   A) Seeker  B) Chaser  C) Beater  D) Keeper

7. **(Magic)** What is the incantation for the charm that conjures a Patronus to repel Dementors?
   A) Riddikulus  B) Alohomora  C) Expecto Patronum  D) Expelliarmus

8. **(Numbers)** How old is Harry for most of this book?
   A) Thirteen  B) Fourteen  C) Sixteen  D) Fifteen

9. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the Weasley twins' joke sweet that makes you vomit from one half and cures you instantly from the other?
   A) Ice Mice  B) Fizzing Whizbees  C) Chocolate Frogs  D) Puking Pastilles

10. **(Characters)** What is the name of Hagrid's giant half-brother, kept hidden in the Forbidden Forest?
    A) Norbert  B) Aragog  C) Grawp  D) Grip

11. **(Plot)** Who is appointed Headmistress of Hogwarts after Dumbledore is forced to flee?
    A) Professor McGonagall  B) Professor Snape  C) Professor Trelawney  D) Dolores Umbridge

12. **(Characters) (Open)** Who is the centaur who becomes Hogwarts's new Divination teacher this year?

13. **(Plot)** What small glass object, kept on a shelf deep inside the Ministry of Magic, do Voldemort and the Death Eaters want Harry to retrieve for them?
    A) A sword  B) A map  C) A prophecy  D) A locket

14. **(Characters)** Which Weasley is promoted to Junior Assistant to the Minister and becomes estranged from his family this year?
    A) Percy Weasley  B) Bill Weasley  C) Charlie Weasley  D) Fred Weasley

15. **(Quidditch)** What mocking chant do Slytherin fans sing about Ron's goalkeeping during Quidditch matches?
    A) "Weasley Can't Fly"  B) "Weasley Is Our King"  C) "Keeper King Ron"  D) "Ron the Loser"

16. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Argus Filch's cat?
    A) Crookshanks  B) Fang  C) Mrs. Norris  D) Mr. Tibbles

17. **(Places)** What is the name of the magical room that appears only when someone has genuine need of it, used by the D.A. for practice?
    A) The Chamber of Secrets  B) The Shrieking Shack  C) The Hall of Prophecy  D) The Room of Requirement

18. **(Plot) (Open)** Put these three events in the order they happen in the book: (i) Fred and George fly out of Hogwarts for good on their brooms; (ii) Harry casts his first corporeal Patronus against the dementors in Little Whinging; (iii) Dumbledore escapes Hogwarts on Fawkes rather than be arrested.

19. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Neville Longbottom's pet toad?
    A) Scabbers  B) Crookshanks  C) Pigwidgeon  D) Trevor

20. **(Magic) (Open)** What everyday object does Hermione magically alter so that D.A. members each carry one that grows warm to show the date and time of the next meeting?
