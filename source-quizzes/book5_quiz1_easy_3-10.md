# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*32 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who becomes the new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher at Hogwarts this year?
   A) Dolores Umbridge  B) Remus Lupin  C) Alastor Moody  D) Gilderoy Lockhart

2. **(Places)** What is the address of the Order of the Phoenix's secret headquarters?
   A) The Burrow  B) Spinner's End  C) 4 Privet Drive  D) Number Twelve, Grimmauld Place

3. **(Plot)** What name does the secret student Defense group founded by Harry take?
   A) The Inquisitorial Squad  B) S.P.E.W.  C) Dumbledore's Army  D) The Order of the Phoenix

4. **(Magic)** What is the incantation for the charm that conjures a Patronus to repel Dementors?
   A) Riddikulus  B) Expecto Patronum  C) Alohomora  D) Expelliarmus

5. **(Quidditch)** What position does Ron Weasley play when he joins the Gryffindor Quidditch team this year?
   A) Seeker  B) Chaser  C) Keeper  D) Beater

6. **(Creatures)** What are the skeletal, winged horses that pull the Hogwarts carriages, visible only to those who have witnessed death?
   A) Hippogriffs  B) Thestrals  C) Nifflers  D) Blast-Ended Skrewts

7. **(Numbers)** How old is Harry for most of this book?
   A) Fourteen  B) Sixteen  C) Fifteen  D) Thirteen

8. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the Weasley twins' joke sweet that makes you vomit from one half and cures you instantly from the other?
   A) Puking Pastilles  B) Ice Mice  C) Fizzing Whizbees  D) Chocolate Frogs

9. **(Characters) (Open)** Which two fifth-year Gryffindors are made prefects at the start of the book?

10. **(Places)** What magical room appears only when someone has genuine need of it, used by the D.A. for practice?
    A) The Chamber of Secrets  B) The Shrieking Shack  C) The Room of Requirement  D) The Hall of Prophecy

11. **(Plot)** Who is appointed Headmistress of Hogwarts after Dumbledore is forced to flee?
    A) Dolores Umbridge  B) Professor McGonagall  C) Professor Snape  D) Professor Trelawney

12. **(Magic)** What is the name of the mental-defense discipline Harry learns to stop Voldemort reading his mind?
    A) Legilimency  B) Divination  C) Occlumency  D) Arithmancy

13. **(Quidditch)** What mocking chant do Slytherin fans sing about Ron's goalkeeping during Quidditch matches?
    A) "Weasley Can't Fly"  B) "Weasley Is Our King"  C) "Keeper King Ron"  D) "Ron the Loser"

14. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Argus Filch's cat?
    A) Crookshanks  B) Fang  C) Mr. Tibbles  D) Mrs. Norris

15. **(Numbers)** What is the Ministry's bounty on Sirius Black's head?
    A) Ten thousand Galleons  B) One thousand Galleons  C) Five thousand Galleons  D) Fifty thousand Galleons

16. **(Food/Sweets)** At Easter, what sweet does Harry find packed inside the chocolate egg Ginny delivers from Mrs. Weasley?
    A) Ice Mice  B) Fizzing Whizbees  C) Puking Pastilles  D) Sugar Quills

17. **(Characters)** Who is Hagrid's giant half-brother, kept hidden in the Forbidden Forest?
    A) Grawp  B) Aragog  C) Norbert  D) Buckbeak

18. **(Places)** In which Hogsmeade pub do Harry, Ron, and Hermione first gather interested students to form their Defense group?
    A) The Three Broomsticks  B) Zonko's Joke Shop  C) Madam Puddifoot's Tea Shop  D) The Hog's Head

19. **(Plot)** What small glass object, kept on a shelf deep inside the Ministry of Magic, do Voldemort and the Death Eaters want Harry to retrieve for them?
    A) A prophecy  B) A sword  C) A locket  D) A map

20. **(Magic)** What charm does Hermione repeatedly cast to seal doors behind the group as they flee Death Eaters in the Ministry?
    A) Alohomora  B) Incarcerous  C) Protego  D) Colloportus

21. **(Quidditch)** Which Weasley becomes Gryffindor's new Seeker after Harry is banned from Quidditch?
    A) Ginny Weasley  B) Fred Weasley  C) George Weasley  D) Ron Weasley

22. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Neville Longbottom's pet toad?
    A) Scabbers  B) Trevor  C) Pigwidgeon  D) Crookshanks

23. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many students originally sign the Dumbledore's Army membership parchment at the Hog's Head?

24. **(Food/Sweets)** What drink does Fred order twenty-five rounds of for the assembled group at the first Hog's Head meeting?
    A) Firewhisky  B) Pumpkin Juice  C) Gillywater  D) Butterbeer

25. **(Characters)** Which Weasley is promoted to Junior Assistant to the Minister and becomes estranged from his family this year?
    A) Bill Weasley  B) Charlie Weasley  C) Percy Weasley  D) Fred Weasley

26. **(Places)** What hospital treats Arthur Weasley after he is attacked by Voldemort's snake?
    A) St. Mungo's Hospital for Magical Maladies and Injuries  B) Hogwarts Hospital Wing  C) The Janus Thickey Clinic  D) Azkaban Infirmary

27. **(Plot)** What do Fred and George Weasley do partway through the school year, leaving Hogwarts for good?
    A) Join the Ministry  B) Open a joke shop in Diagon Alley  C) Run away to Romania  D) Become Aurors

28. **(Magic)** What is the name of Ginny Weasley's signature hex, used devastatingly on opponents?
    A) The Leg-Locker Curse  B) The Jelly-Legs Jinx  C) The Bat-Bogey Hex  D) The Tickling Charm

29. **(Quidditch)** How many points does a Seeker's team score for catching the Golden Snitch?
    A) One hundred  B) Fifty  C) Two hundred  D) One hundred and fifty

30. **(Creatures)** Which creature is kept secretly in Sirius's mother's old bedroom at Grimmauld Place and fed dead rats by Sirius?
    A) Fang  B) Buckbeak the hippogriff  C) Grawp  D) Norbert

31. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons of his Triwizard Tournament winnings does Harry secretly give Fred and George to help start their joke shop?

32. **(Food/Sweets)** What dessert does Mrs. Weasley serve three helpings of at the Grimmauld Place welcome dinner?
    A) Treacle tart  B) Steak-and-kidney pie  C) Chocolate gateau  D) Rhubarb crumble and custard
