# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*26 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Which house-elf appears in Harry's bedroom to warn him not to return to Hogwarts?
   A) Kreacher  B) Winky  C) Dobby  D) Hokey

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Name the two Weasley twins who rescue Harry from the Dursleys' house in a flying car.

3. **(Characters)** What is the name of Ron Weasley's pet rat?
   A) Trevor  B) Crookshanks  C) Errol  D) Scabbers

4. **(Characters)** Who is the boastful new Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher this year?
   A) Gilderoy Lockhart  B) Remus Lupin  C) Alastor Moody  D) Quirinus Quirrell

5. **(Places)** What is the name of the Weasley family's home?
   A) Grimmauld Place  B) The Burrow  C) Shell Cottage  D) The Hollow

6. **(Places)** In which room is the secret entrance to the Chamber of Secrets hidden?
   A) Moaning Myrtle's bathroom  B) The Owlery  C) The Great Hall  D) The Potions dungeon

7. **(Places)** Which platform's entrance is magically sealed, forcing Harry and Ron to fly to Hogwarts instead?
   A) Platform Six  B) The Hogwarts Express platform  C) Platform Thirteen  D) Platform Nine and Three-Quarters

8. **(Plot)** What object does Harry find and begin writing in, not realising it belongs to Tom Riddle?
   A) A mirror  B) A diary  C) A locket  D) A map

9. **(Plot)** What warning message is painted on a corridor wall early in the book?
   A) "Beware the Basilisk"  B) "Mudbloods will die"  C) "Hogwarts will fall"  D) "The Chamber of Secrets has been opened"

10. **(Plot)** Which magical tree violently attacks the flying Ford Anglia as it crash-lands at Hogwarts?
    A) The Whomping Willow  B) Devil's Snare  C) The Forbidden Forest's oaks  D) A Venomous Tentacula

11. **(Plot)** Which younger Weasley sibling is taken into the Chamber of Secrets near the end of the book?
    A) Percy  B) Ron  C) Ginny  D) Fred

12. **(Magic)** What rare magical ability allows Harry to speak to snakes?
    A) Legilimency  B) Parseltongue  C) Occlumency  D) Divination

13. **(Magic)** What spell does Snape use to swiftly disarm Lockhart during the Duelling Club demonstration?
    A) Expelliarmus  B) Rictusempra  C) Tarantallegra  D) Finite Incantatem

14. **(Magic)** What method of magical travel does Harry use for the first time, leading him to mispronounce his destination?
    A) Apparition  B) Floo powder  C) A Portkey  D) A broomstick

15. **(Quidditch)** What position does Harry play on the Gryffindor Quidditch team?
    A) Keeper  B) Beater  C) Seeker  D) Chaser

16. **(Quidditch)** Which team does Gryffindor face in their first Quidditch match of the season?
    A) Hufflepuff  B) Ravenclaw  C) Durmstrang  D) Slytherin

17. **(Quidditch)** What golden, winged magical object does the Seeker chase to end a Quidditch match?
    A) The Golden Snitch  B) The Quaffle  C) A Bludger  D) The Keeper's hoop

18. **(Creatures)** What type of giant serpent is the monster hidden in the Chamber of Secrets?
    A) A Nundu  B) An Acromantula  C) A Basilisk  D) A Runespoor

19. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the giant spider living deep in the Forbidden Forest?
    A) Fang  B) Aragog  C) Mosag  D) Norbert

20. **(Creatures)** Which of Filch's pets is found petrified, hanging from a torch bracket?
    A) Crookshanks  B) Fang  C) Mrs Norris  D) Trevor

21. **(Numbers) (Open)** How old does Harry turn at the very start of the book?

22. **(Numbers)** According to the book, how many players in total fly on broomsticks during a Quidditch match (both teams combined)?
    A) Fourteen  B) Twelve  C) Twenty-two  D) Ten

23. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons is Mr Weasley fined by the Ministry for bewitching the family's flying car?
    A) Twenty Galleons  B) Ten Galleons  C) A hundred Galleons  D) Fifty Galleons

24. **(Food/Sweets)** What treat does Hagrid bring along as an offering in this book?
    A) Chocolate frogs  B) Butterbeer  C) Treacle toffee  D) Pumpkin pasties

25. **(Food/Sweets)** What two ice-cream flavours does Harry treat Ron and Hermione to in Diagon Alley?
    A) Chocolate and vanilla  B) Strawberry and peanut butter  C) Butterbeer and treacle  D) Pumpkin and spearmint

26. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What food item, besides a hand-knitted jumper, does Mrs Weasley send Harry for Christmas?
