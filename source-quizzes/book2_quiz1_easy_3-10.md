# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*20 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Which house-elf appears in Harry's bedroom to warn him not to return to Hogwarts?
   A) Dobby  B) Kreacher  C) Winky  D) Hokey

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Name the two Weasley twins who rescue Harry from the Dursleys' house in a flying car.

3. **(Characters)** What is the name of Ron Weasley's pet rat?
   A) Scabbers  B) Trevor  C) Crookshanks  D) Errol

4. **(Characters)** Who is the boastful new Defence Against the Dark Arts teacher this year?
   A) Gilderoy Lockhart  B) Remus Lupin  C) Alastor Moody  D) Quirinus Quirrell

5. **(Places)** What is the name of the Weasley family's home?
   A) The Burrow  B) Grimmauld Place  C) Shell Cottage  D) The Hollow

6. **(Places)** In which room is the secret entrance to the Chamber of Secrets hidden?
   A) Moaning Myrtle's bathroom  B) The Owlery  C) The Great Hall  D) The Potions dungeon

7. **(Plot)** What object does Harry find and begin writing in, not realising it belongs to Tom Riddle?
   A) A diary  B) A mirror  C) A locket  D) A map

8. **(Plot)** What warning message is painted on a corridor wall early in the book?
   A) "The Chamber of Secrets has been opened"  B) "Beware the Basilisk"  C) "Mudbloods will die"  D) "Hogwarts will fall"

9. **(Plot)** Which magical tree violently attacks the flying Ford Anglia as it crash-lands at Hogwarts?
   A) The Whomping Willow  B) Devil's Snare  C) The Forbidden Forest's oaks  D) A Venomous Tentacula

10. **(Plot)** Which younger Weasley sibling is taken into the Chamber of Secrets near the end of the book?
    A) Ginny  B) Percy  C) Ron  D) Fred

11. **(Magic)** What rare magical ability allows Harry to speak to snakes?
    A) Parseltongue  B) Legilimency  C) Occlumency  D) Divination

12. **(Magic)** What spell does Snape use to swiftly disarm Lockhart during the Duelling Club demonstration?
    A) Expelliarmus  B) Rictusempra  C) Tarantallegra  D) Finite Incantatem

13. **(Quidditch)** What position does Harry play on the Gryffindor Quidditch team?
    A) Seeker  B) Keeper  C) Beater  D) Chaser

14. **(Quidditch)** Which team does Gryffindor face in their first Quidditch match of the season?
    A) Slytherin  B) Hufflepuff  C) Ravenclaw  D) Durmstrang

15. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the giant spider living deep in the Forbidden Forest?
    A) Aragog  B) Fang  C) Mosag  D) Norbert

16. **(Creatures)** What type of giant serpent is the monster hidden in the Chamber of Secrets?
    A) A Basilisk  B) A Nundu  C) An Acromantula  D) A Runespoor

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How old does Harry turn at the very start of the book?

18. **(Numbers)** How many Weasley children appear in this book in total, including Ginny?
    A) Six  B) Five  C) Seven  D) Four

19. **(Food/Sweets)** What treat does Hagrid bring along as an offering in this book?
    A) Treacle toffee  B) Chocolate frogs  C) Butterbeer  D) Pumpkin pasties

20. **(Food/Sweets)** What two ice-cream flavours does Harry treat Ron and Hermione to in Diagon Alley?
    A) Strawberry and peanut butter  B) Chocolate and vanilla  C) Butterbeer and treacle  D) Pumpkin and spearmint
