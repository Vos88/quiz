# Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*20 questions, entirely open — this round rewards close readers, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Magic) (Open)** What wood and core make up Harry's original wand, broken beyond repair during the escape from Bathilda Bagshot's house — and how long is it?

2. **(Plot) (Open)** What exact words does Harry carve onto the headstone he makes for Dobby?

3. **(Magic) (Open)** What are the exact wood, core, length, and flexibility of Bellatrix Lestrange's wand, as identified by Ollivander?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** Sirius Black's younger brother turns out to be the Horcrux-thief known only by the initials "R.A.B." What is his full name?

5. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to the Snatchers, what additional bounty (in Galleons) is offered for turning in a Muggle-born caught alongside Harry?

6. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Which professional international Quidditch Seeker, a wedding guest of the Delacour family, reveals that Gellert Grindelwald killed his grandfather?

7. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which goblin, a companion of Ted Tonks and Dirk Cresswell while in hiding, is confirmed killed via a Potterwatch broadcast?

8. **(Magic) (Open)** What are the exact wood, core, and length of Mary Cattermole's wand, as stated during her Ministry courtroom hearing?

9. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does Hermione use to duplicate the locket Horcrux, leaving a fake in Dolores Umbridge's possession?

10. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Griphook, which legendary goblin king was the sword of Gryffindor originally made for?

11. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Rita Skeeter's book, how many pages long is "The Life and Lies of Albus Dumbledore," and how many weeks did she reportedly take to write it?

12. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which Hogwarts professor is killed on Voldemort's orders by Nagini's bite, inside the Shrieking Shack?

13. **(Places) (Open)** In which hidden chamber beneath Hogwarts do Ron and Hermione retrieve the Basilisk fang used to destroy the Hufflepuff cup Horcrux?

14. **(Plot) (Open)** What cursed, sentient magical fire, accidentally conjured by Vincent Crabbe, both destroys the diadem Horcrux and kills Crabbe himself?

15. **(Numbers) (Open)** What exact date (day and month) do James and Lily Potter die at Godric's Hollow, as inscribed on their gravestone?

16. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of the enchanted, light-absorbing object Dumbledore leaves Ron in his will, which later guides Ron back to Harry and Hermione?

17. **(Plot) (Open)** Which goblin agrees to help the trio break into Gringotts, but ultimately betrays them and steals back the sword of Gryffindor?

18. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Ravenclaw House's ghost, the "Grey Lady," who turns out to be Rowena Ravenclaw's own daughter?

19. **(Numbers) (Open)** Roughly how many Death Eaters ambush the seven "Potters" in the air during the escape from Privet Drive?

20. **(Plot) (Open)** Name, in order of destruction within this book (from first to last), the four Horcruxes that Harry's side destroys.

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 3 (7/10)

1. Holly, with a phoenix feather core, eleven inches
2. "Here lies Dobby, a Free Elf"
3. Walnut, with a dragon heartstring core, twelve and three-quarter inches, unyielding
4. Regulus Arcturus Black
5. Ten Galleons
6. Viktor Krum
7. Gornuk
8. Cherry wood, with a unicorn hair core, eight and three-quarter inches
9. Geminio (the Duplication Charm)
10. Ragnuk the First
11. Nine hundred pages, written in four weeks
12. Severus Snape
13. The Chamber of Secrets
14. Fiendfyre
15. 31 October 1981
16. The Deluminator
17. Griphook
18. Helena Ravenclaw
19. About thirty
20. The locket, then Hufflepuff's cup, then Ravenclaw's diadem, then Nagini
