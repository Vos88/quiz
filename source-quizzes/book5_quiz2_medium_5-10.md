# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*30 questions, an even split of multiple choice and open questions, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is Luna Lovegood's father, the editor of The Quibbler?
   A) Cygnus Lovegood  B) Ignatius Lovegood  C) Xenophilius Lovegood  D) Xenophon Lovegood

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Who presides over Harry's disciplinary hearing as Head of the Department of Magical Law Enforcement, alongside Fudge and Umbridge?

3. **(Characters)** Which Order member is sentenced to six months in Azkaban after being caught trying to force his way through a top-security Ministry door?
   A) Mundungus Fletcher  B) Dedalus Diggle  C) Emmeline Vance  D) Sturgis Podmore

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the ancient, ill-tempered house-elf who lives at Grimmauld Place?

5. **(Places)** What is the number of the old, disused Ministry courtroom where Harry's hearing is unexpectedly moved to?
   A) Courtroom Seven  B) Courtroom Ten  C) Courtroom Twelve  D) Courtroom One

6. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the fake Muggle department store that conceals the entrance to St. Mungo's Hospital?

7. **(Places)** At what Diagon Alley address do Fred and George open their joke shop?
   A) Number 12  B) Number 4  C) Number 93  D) Number 42

8. **(Plot)** What does Educational Decree Number Twenty-four do?
   A) It fires Professor Trelawney  B) It bans all student clubs, societies, and teams without Umbridge's approval  C) It bans possession of The Quibbler  D) It creates the Inquisitorial Squad

9. **(Plot) (Open)** Which disgraced journalist does Hermione blackmail into writing Harry's true account of Voldemort's return?

10. **(Plot)** What form does the boggart in the drawing room's locked writing desk take when it confronts Mrs. Weasley?
    A) The dead bodies of her family members  B) A dementor  C) Voldemort  D) A giant spider

11. **(Plot) (Open)** What does Harry accidentally witness after sneaking a look into Snape's unguarded Pensieve?

12. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of the ability Voldemort uses to invade and read other people's minds?

13. **(Magic)** What type of charm does Hermione use to enchant the fake Galleons that signal D.A. meeting times?
    A) A Cheering Charm  B) A Switching Spell  C) A Memory Charm  D) A Protean Charm

14. **(Magic) (Open)** What charm does Harry use to block Bellatrix Lestrange's attempt to Summon the prophecy straight out of his hands?

15. **(Quidditch)** Who is appointed the new Gryffindor Quidditch Captain this year, replacing the graduated Oliver Wood?
    A) Katie Bell  B) Angelina Johnson  C) Alicia Spinnet  D) Ginny Weasley

16. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What is the final score when Gryffindor loses to Hufflepuff despite Ginny Weasley catching the Snitch?

17. **(Quidditch)** What brand of broomstick does Ron receive from his parents as his Prefect reward?
    A) A Nimbus Two Thousand  B) A Comet Two Sixty  C) A Cleansweep Eleven  D) A Firebolt

18. **(Creatures) (Open)** What small, biting pests infest the curtains at Grimmauld Place and are later harvested by Fred and George for their venom?

19. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the first thestral born into the Hogwarts herd, and Hagrid's particular favorite?
    A) Tenebrus  B) Buckbeak  C) Firenze  D) Fluffy

20. **(Creatures) (Open)** What small, twig-like creatures that guard wand-quality trees does Professor Grubbly-Plank have the class handle, offering wood lice as a calming treat?

21. **(Numbers) (Open)** How much does Hermione pay for a new quill at Scrivenshaft's?

22. **(Numbers)** How many O.W.L.s did Fred and George Weasley each earn?
    A) Five  B) Seven  C) Three  D) None

23. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Extendable Ears do Fred and George produce to eavesdrop on the adults outside Arthur Weasley's hospital ward?

24. **(Numbers)** How old is Sturgis Podmore, the Order member sentenced to Azkaban?
    A) Twenty-nine  B) Forty-five  C) Fifty-two  D) Thirty-eight

25. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the Skiving Snackbox sweet that gives the eater an uncontrollable nosebleed?
    A) Nosebleed Nougat  B) Fainting Fancies  C) Puking Pastilles  D) Fever Fudge

26. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What untested Skiving Snackbox prototype causes painful, unremovable boils, making it a poor choice right before a Quidditch practice?

27. **(Food/Sweets)** What birthday gift from Ron and Hermione does Harry regretfully throw away unopened at the very start of the summer?
    A) Pumpkin pasties  B) Honeydukes chocolate  C) A box of Chocolate Frogs  D) Ice Mice

28. **(Deaths) (Open)** Who kills Sirius Black in the Department of Mysteries?

29. **(Deaths)** Which two of Molly Weasley's brothers were killed in the First War, overwhelmed only after it took five Death Eaters to bring them down?
    A) Gideon and Fabian Prewett  B) Frank and Alice Longbottom  C) Edgar and Amelia Bones  D) Caradoc and Benjy Dearborn

30. **(Deaths) (Open)** What happens to Neville's parents, Frank and Alice Longbottom, that leaves them permanently hospitalized rather than killed?
