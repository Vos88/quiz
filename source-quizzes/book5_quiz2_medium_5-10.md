# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*20 questions, an even split of multiple choice and open questions, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is Luna Lovegood's father, the editor of The Quibbler?
   A) Xenophilius Lovegood  B) Cygnus Lovegood  C) Ignatius Lovegood  D) Xenophon Lovegood

2. **(Magic)** What is the name of the mental-defense discipline Snape teaches Harry, meant to stop Voldemort reading his mind?
   A) Divination  B) Arithmancy  C) Occlumency  D) Legilimency

3. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the shabby Hogsmeade pub where the first D.A. recruitment meeting is held?

4. **(Creatures)** Which small, biting pests infest the curtains at Grimmauld Place and are later harvested by Fred and George for their venom?
   A) Doxies  B) Bowtruckles  C) Knarls  D) Nifflers

5. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many students sign the original Dumbledore's Army membership parchment at the Hog's Head?

6. **(Quidditch)** What is the final score when Gryffindor loses to Hufflepuff despite Ginny Weasley catching the Snitch?
   A) Hufflepuff 240 – Gryffindor 230  B) Gryffindor 240 – Hufflepuff 230  C) Hufflepuff 200 – Gryffindor 190  D) Gryffindor 250 – Hufflepuff 240

7. **(Characters)** Who is appointed the new Gryffindor Quidditch Captain this year, replacing the graduated Oliver Wood?
   A) Katie Bell  B) Ginny Weasley  C) Alicia Spinnet  D) Angelina Johnson

8. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is the name of the Skiving Snackbox sweet that gives the eater an uncontrollable nosebleed?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** Which Order member is badly bitten by Voldemort's snake while on guard duty at the Ministry?

10. **(Plot) (Open)** What secret signaling device does Hermione invent to alert D.A. members to the time and place of the next meeting?

11. **(Magic)** What is the incantation for the Killing Curse?
    A) Crucio  B) Avada Kedavra  C) Imperio  D) Sectumsempra

12. **(Characters) (Open)** What magical talent allows Tonks to change her hair and face at will?

13. **(Places)** What is the number of the old, disused Ministry courtroom where Harry's disciplinary hearing is held?
    A) Courtroom Seven  B) Courtroom Twelve  C) Courtroom Ten  D) Courtroom One

14. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons of his Triwizard Tournament winnings does Harry secretly give Fred and George to start their joke shop?

15. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the first thestral born into the Hogwarts herd, and Hagrid's particular favorite?
    A) Buckbeak  B) Firenze  C) Fluffy  D) Tenebrus

16. **(Plot) (Open)** Match each decree to its effect: what did Educational Decree Number Twenty-Four do, and what did Educational Decree Number Twenty-Five do?

17. **(Magic)** What charm does Hermione repeatedly cast to seal doors behind the group as they flee Death Eaters in the Ministry?
    A) Alohomora  B) Incarcerous  C) Colloportus  D) Protego

18. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What brand of broomstick does Ron receive from his parents as his Prefect reward?

19. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which Death Eater casts the curse that kills Sirius Black?

20. **(Characters)** Which Ministry official, Head of the Department of Magical Law Enforcement, presides over Harry's hearing alongside Fudge and Umbridge?
    A) Rufus Scrimgeour  B) Amelia Bones  C) Kingsley Shacklebolt  D) Barty Crouch

## Answer Key — Quiz 2 (5/10)

1. A) Xenophilius Lovegood
2. C) Occlumency
3. The Hog's Head
4. A) Doxies
5. Twenty-eight
6. A) Hufflepuff 240 – Gryffindor 230
7. D) Angelina Johnson
8. Nosebleed Nougat
9. Arthur Weasley
10. Enchanted (fake) Galleons, altered with a Protean Charm
11. B) Avada Kedavra
12. She's a Metamorphmagus
13. C) Courtroom Ten
14. 1,000 Galleons
15. D) Tenebrus
16. Decree Twenty-Four banned all student clubs, societies, and teams without Umbridge's personal approval; Decree Twenty-Five gave the High Inquisitor supreme authority to override any other staff member's punishments
17. C) Colloportus
18. A Cleansweep Eleven
19. Bellatrix Lestrange
20. B) Amelia Bones

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** Exactly how old is Sturgis Podmore, the Order member sentenced to six months in Azkaban for trying to force his way through a Ministry door? → **Thirty-eight**
