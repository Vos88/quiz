# Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*26 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is appointed the new Minister of Magic at the start of the book, replacing Cornelius Fudge?
   A) Rufus Scrimgeour  B) Kingsley Shacklebolt  C) Arthur Weasley  D) Amos Diggory

2. **(Plot)** Which retired Hogwarts professor does Dumbledore convince to come out of retirement and teach Potions this year?
   A) Alastor Moody  B) Gilderoy Lockhart  C) Horace Slughorn  D) Quirinus Quirrell

3. **(Plot)** Which subject is Severus Snape finally given the chance to teach this year, after years of being refused?
   A) Potions  B) Herbology  C) Transfiguration  D) Defense Against the Dark Arts

4. **(Places)** What is the name of the street where Snape lives, which Narcissa and Bellatrix visit at the start of the book?
   A) Privet Drive  B) Spinner's End  C) Diagon Alley  D) Grimmauld Place

5. **(Characters)** Which teenage Slytherin is given a dangerous secret task by Voldemort this year?
   A) Blaise Zabini  B) Vincent Crabbe  C) Draco Malfoy  D) Gregory Goyle

6. **(Magic)** What is the name of the rare lucky potion Harry wins as a prize in Slughorn's first Potions class?
   A) Felix Felicis  B) Veritaserum  C) Amortentia  D) Polyjuice Potion

7. **(Characters)** What nickname did the mysterious former owner of Harry's secondhand Potions book give himself?
   A) The Chosen One  B) The Dark Lord  C) The Half-Blood Prince  D) The Boy Who Lived

8. **(Quidditch)** What new role is Harry given on the Gryffindor Quidditch team this year, alongside Seeker?
   A) Team Manager  B) Captain  C) Reserve Keeper  D) Prefect-Captain

9. **(Creatures)** What kind of creature is Buckbeak, whom Harry inherits and who is renamed "Witherwings" for safety?
   A) A hippogriff  B) A phoenix  C) A unicorn  D) A dragon

10. **(Places)** What is the name of the London house Harry inherits from Sirius Black?
    A) Spinner's End  B) The Burrow  C) Malfoy Manor  D) 12 Grimmauld Place

11. **(Food/Sweets)** Which Hogsmeade/Diagon Alley sweetshop is owned by Ambrosius Flume, one of Slughorn's old students?
    A) Zonko's  B) Honeydukes  C) The Three Broomsticks  D) The Leaky Cauldron

12. **(Characters)** Which two Weasley brothers are running their own joke shop in Diagon Alley this year?
    A) Percy and Ron  B) Charlie and George  C) Bill and Charlie  D) Fred and George

13. **(Plot)** What cursed object, bought in Knockturn Alley, nearly kills Katie Bell?
    A) A silver ring  B) An opal necklace  C) An enchanted mirror  D) A poisoned quill

14. **(Numbers)** How old does Harry turn during this book?
    A) Fourteen  B) Fifteen  C) Seventeen  D) Sixteen

15. **(Deaths) (Open)** Who is killed at the end of the book, struck down atop the Astronomy Tower?

16. **(Magic)** What term does Dumbledore use for an object in which a person has hidden a fragment of their own soul?
    A) A Horcrux  B) A Portkey  C) An Animagus  D) A Patronus

17. **(Quidditch)** Who wins the position of Gryffindor Keeper after tryouts, beating Cormac McLaggen?
    A) Dean Thomas  B) Seamus Finnigan  C) Ron Weasley  D) Cormac McLaggen

18. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Hagrid's giant Acromantula spider, who dies of old age this year?
    A) Fluffy  B) Norbert  C) Fang  D) Aragog

19. **(Plot) (Open)** Name both members of the couple who become engaged early in the book and plan to marry.

20. **(Food/Sweets)** What does Harry pretend to slip into Ron's pumpkin juice before his first match as Captain, to boost his confidence?
    A) Veritaserum  B) A love potion  C) Felix Felicis (liquid luck)  D) Polyjuice Potion

21. **(Places)** In which forest does Hagrid's colony of giant spiders live?
    A) The Forbidden Forest  B) Hogsmeade  C) The Shrieking Shack  D) The Great Lake

22. **(Characters)** What is Mrs. Weasley's embarrassing pet name, revealed as the answer to a family security question?
    A) Honeybun  B) Mollywobbles  C) Puddingheart  D) Snugglebum

23. **(Places)** In which village does Horace Slughorn hide out in a Muggle house at the start of the book?
    A) Little Hangleton  B) Godric's Hollow  C) Ottery St Catchpole  D) Budleigh Babberton

24. **(Magic)** What magical device does Dumbledore use to show Harry memories of Voldemort's past during their private lessons?
    A) A Foe-Glass  B) A Pensieve  C) A Remembrall  D) A Sneakoscope

25. **(Magic)** What alarming phrase do all nine hands of the Weasleys' family clock point to for most of the book?
    A) Mortal Peril  B) Traveling  C) Lost  D) Home

26. **(Numbers)** In which month does Ron turn seventeen, becoming old enough to take his Apparition Test?
    A) January  B) June  C) March  D) September
