# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*20 questions. For die-hard re-readers only — exact numbers, names and lines.*

1. **(Numbers) (Open)** Mrs Weasley's Howler is described as how many times louder than her usual voice?

2. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many raids does Mr Weasley mention having conducted, in his exasperated aside about Mundungus Fletcher?

3. **(Creatures) (Open)** Aragog's spider offspring are compared in size to what large animal, while Aragog himself is compared to the size of what animal?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the elderly warlock who works alongside Mr Weasley in the Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office?

5. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What single joke gift do the Dursleys send Harry for Christmas, along with a note?

6. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Moaning Myrtle's childhood tormentor, who used to tease her about her glasses?

7. **(Places) (Open)** What address is printed on the back cover of Tom Riddle's diary, as its stationer's/newsagent's location?

8. **(Places) (Open)** From which English county does the "Wailing Widow" reportedly travel to attend Nearly Headless Nick's Deathday Party?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** Why does Mrs Mason flee the Dursleys' dinner party in terror, ruining Uncle Vernon's business deal?

10. **(Plot) (Open)** What prior honour, awarded fifty years earlier, first tips Ron off that the name "Riddle" sounds familiar?

11. **(Plot) (Open)** How does Hermione create the diversion that lets her steal Boomslang skin and Bicorn horn from Snape's store cupboard?

12. **(Magic) (Open)** What magical mishap does an enchanted Muggle teapot inflict on its owner, requiring Memory Charms to cover up?

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What exact Parseltongue command does Harry speak to make the sink in Myrtle's bathroom sink away, revealing the Chamber's entrance?

14. **(Magic) (Open)** How many Muggle owners had the cursed opal necklace in Borgin and Burkes reportedly killed, according to its price tag?

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What number position in the league does Ron's favourite team, the Chudley Cannons, hold?

16. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Gryffindor won the Quidditch Cup the previous year by beating Slytherin for the first time in how many years?

17. **(Creatures) (Open)** Who gave Hagrid the egg that hatched into his giant spider, and what was Hagrid's status at Hogwarts at the time?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many O.W.L.s did both Percy and Bill Weasley get, fuelling jokes about "another Head Boy in the family"?

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What dessert, shaped like a tombstone, is served at Nearly Headless Nick's 500th Deathday Party?

20. **(Characters) (Open)** Name all seven Weasley children mentioned in this book, in order from oldest to youngest.

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 4 (9/10)

1. A hundred times
2. Nine
3. Offspring: the size of carthorses; Aragog: the size of a small elephant
4. Perkins
5. A toothpick (with a note asking if he could stay at Hogwarts over the summer too)
6. Olive Hornby
7. Vauxhall Road, London
8. Kent
9. A Ministry owl drops a warning letter onto her head, and she is terrified of birds
10. Special Services to the School
11. She throws a Filibuster firework into Goyle's cauldron, making his Swelling Solution explode
12. It squirts boiling tea and clamps sugar tongs onto the owner's nose
13. "Open up"
14. Nineteen
15. Ninth
16. Seven years
17. An unnamed traveller gave it to him while he was still a student
18. Twelve each
19. A tombstone-shaped cake
20. Bill, Charlie, Percy, Fred, George, Ron, Ginny

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** According to the book, how many times was Nearly Headless Nick struck in the neck with the blunt axe? → **45**
