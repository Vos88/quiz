# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*24 questions. For die-hard re-readers only — exact numbers, names and precise details.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the elderly warlock who works alongside Mr Weasley in the Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Who signs the Ministry's official warning letter to Harry about the Hover Charm?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Moaning Myrtle's childhood tormentor, who used to tease her about her glasses?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** Name all seven Weasley children mentioned in this book, in order from oldest to youngest.

5. **(Places) (Open)** What address is printed on the back cover of Tom Riddle's diary, as its stationer's location?

6. **(Places) (Open)** From which English county does the "Wailing Widow" reportedly travel to attend Nearly Headless Nick's Deathday Party?

7. **(Places) (Open)** According to Dumbledore, in which country is Lord Voldemort currently hiding?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** Why does Mrs Mason flee the Dursleys' dinner party in terror, ruining Uncle Vernon's business deal?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** What prior honour, awarded fifty years earlier, first tips Ron off that the name "Riddle" sounds familiar?

10. **(Plot) (Open)** Whose memory is wiped when Lockhart's Obliviate spell backfires off Ron's damaged wand in the tunnel?

11. **(Magic) (Open)** What magical mishap does an enchanted Muggle teapot inflict on its owner, requiring Memory Charms to cover up?

12. **(Magic) (Open)** What exact Parseltongue command does Harry speak to make the sink in Myrtle's bathroom sink away, revealing the Chamber's entrance?

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does the Ministry's warning letter say was detected at the Dursleys' house?

14. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Gryffindor won the Quidditch Cup the previous year by beating Slytherin for the first time in how many years?

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What position does Lockhart claim he was asked to try for on the National Quidditch Squad, before choosing to fight Dark forces instead?

16. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Where exactly is the Golden Snitch hovering when Harry makes his match-winning catch despite his broken arm?

17. **(Creatures) (Open)** What size are Aragog's spider offspring compared to, and what size is Aragog himself compared to?

18. **(Creatures) (Open)** Who gave Hagrid the egg that hatched into his giant spider?

19. **(Creatures) (Open)** In Knockturn Alley, what creatures are kept in a cage in a shop window two doors down from Borgin and Burkes?

20. **(Numbers) (Open)** Mrs Weasley's Howler is described as how many times louder than her usual voice?

21. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many raids does Mr Weasley mention having conducted, in his exasperated aside about Mundungus Fletcher?

22. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many O.W.L.s did both Percy and Bill Weasley get, fuelling jokes about "another Head Boy in the family"?

23. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What single joke gift do the Dursleys send Harry for Christmas, along with a note?

24. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What dessert, shaped like a tombstone, is served at Nearly Headless Nick's 500th Deathday Party?
