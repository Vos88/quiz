# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*20 questions, all open — this round rewards deep plot knowledge over memorized numbers, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Plot) (Open)** What excuse does Snape give Harry for why James's "rescue" of him as a teenager wasn't really an act of heroism?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** What was Sirius Black's own reasoning for recommending Peter Pettigrew replace him as the Potters' Secret-Keeper?

3. **(Plot) (Open)** What two specific rescues does Harry accomplish using Hermione's Time-Turner on the night of Buckbeak's execution?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What is Sirius Black's Animagus form, and the nickname his school friends gave it?

5. **(Characters) (Open)** What is Peter Pettigrew's Animagus form, and his school nickname?

6. **(Characters) (Open)** What is James Potter's Animagus form and school nickname — and what does it turn out to share with Harry's own Patronus?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** Why were the Shrieking Shack and the tunnel beneath the Whomping Willow originally built at Hogwarts in the first place?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** Besides his own earlier stint in Azkaban, name the other two painful memories Hagrid admits to dwelling on when the trio visits his hut.

9. **(Characters) (Open)** Who does Hermione suspect sent Harry the anonymous Firebolt, prompting her to report it to Professor McGonagall?

10. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Hagrid, why is Buckbeak's hearing before the Committee for the Disposal of Dangerous Creatures effectively a foregone conclusion?

11. **(Plot) (Open)** What single argument does Harry make to stop Lupin and Black from killing Peter Pettigrew on the spot?

12. **(Creatures) (Open)** Name both physical marks on Scabbers that identify him as the same rat Percy once owned — a detail that becomes a major clue.

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What phrase activates the Marauder's Map, and what phrase wipes it blank again?

14. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Why does Professor McGonagall take fifty points from Slytherin and hand out detentions to Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle, and Flint during the Ravenclaw match?

15. **(Plot) (Open)** What realization does Harry come to, during the Time-Turner sequence, about who actually cast the stag Patronus that saved him earlier that night?

16. **(Places) (Open)** Lupin holds Harry's private Patronus lessons in a specific disused classroom — which subject was it previously used to teach?

17. **(Plot) (Open)** Why does Hermione storm out of Divination for good, and how does Trelawney later claim this fulfilled an earlier prediction of hers?

18. **(Characters) (Open)** What reward does Fudge recommend for Snape after the Shrieking Shack incident — and how does Snape's resulting bitterness end up costing Lupin his job?

19. **(Creatures) (Open)** How does Crookshanks help Harry and Hermione get into the Shrieking Shack, and what does he do once inside to protect Sirius?

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What Honeydukes sweet is specifically described as tasting like blood?

## Answer Key — Quiz 4 (9/10)

1. He claims it was Sirius's reckless prank that nearly got Snape killed near the Whomping Willow, and that James only saved him to avoid trouble for himself — not out of real heroism.
2. He reasoned no one would ever suspect "weak, talentless" Pettigrew, making him the perfect decoy in a double-bluff — a plan that backfired completely since Pettigrew was the real traitor.
3. He and Hermione save Buckbeak from execution, then use Buckbeak to fly up and free Sirius Black from his tower cell.
4. A large black dog, nicknamed "Padfoot."
5. A rat, nicknamed "Wormtail."
6. A stag, nicknamed "Prongs" — the same form Harry's own Patronus takes.
7. To give Remus Lupin, a werewolf since childhood, a safe and isolated place to transform every month without endangering anyone.
8. The day he was expelled from Hogwarts, and the day his father died.
9. Sirius Black.
10. Because the Committee is "in Lucius Malfoy's pocket," according to Hagrid.
11. Harry insists Pettigrew be handed over to the dementors/Ministry rather than killed, saying he doesn't think his father would have wanted his friends to become killers.
12. A torn/ragged left ear and a missing toe.
13. "I solemnly swear that I am up to no good" activates it; "Mischief managed" wipes it blank.
14. Because the three "dementors" that appeared were actually Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle, and Flint in disguise, attempting to sabotage Harry mid-match.
15. He realizes the rescuer wasn't his father — it was himself, having gone back in time and cast the Patronus himself.
16. History of Magic.
17. Trelawney insults her mind as "hopelessly mundane" after another Grim prediction; she later claims Hermione storming out fulfilled her earlier Easter prediction that "one of our number will leave us forever."
18. Fudge recommends Snape for an Order of Merlin; when the embarrassing escape overshadows his reward, Snape spitefully lets slip Lupin's werewolf status to the Slytherins, forcing Lupin to resign.
19. Crookshanks presses a hidden knot on the Whomping Willow to freeze it so they can enter the tunnel; once inside, he sits protectively on Sirius, stopping Harry from attacking him.
20. Blood-flavored lollipops.

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** How many years did it secretly take James, Sirius, and Peter to master becoming Animagi? → **Three (achieved in their fifth year)**
