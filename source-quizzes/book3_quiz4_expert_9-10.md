# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*26 questions, all open — this round rewards deep plot knowledge over memorized numbers, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Plot) (Open)** What excuse does Snape give Harry for why James's "rescue" of him as a teenager wasn't really an act of heroism?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** What was Sirius Black's own reasoning for recommending Peter Pettigrew replace him as the Potters' Secret-Keeper?

3. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is Peter Pettigrew's Animagus form, and his school nickname?

4. **(Places) (Open)** Why were the Shrieking Shack and the tunnel beneath the Whomping Willow originally built at Hogwarts?

5. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Why does Oliver Wood treat this particular Quidditch season as his "last chance," and how does that shape his approach as captain?

6. **(Plot) (Open)** What two specific rescues does Harry accomplish using Hermione's Time-Turner on the night of Buckbeak's execution?

7. **(Characters) (Open)** What is Sirius Black's Animagus form, and the nickname his school friends gave it?

8. **(Magic) (Open)** What is James Potter's Animagus form and school nickname — and what does it turn out to share with Harry's own Patronus?

9. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many years had Peter Pettigrew been hiding as Ron's pet rat, and why, according to his own confession, had he stayed hidden that long rather than resurfacing sooner?

10. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What Honeydukes sweet is specifically described as tasting like blood?

11. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Hagrid, why is Buckbeak's hearing before the Committee for the Disposal of Dangerous Creatures effectively a foregone conclusion?

12. **(Creatures) (Open)** Name both physical marks on Scabbers that identify him as the same rat Percy once owned — a detail that becomes a major clue.

13. **(Characters) (Open)** Who does Hermione suspect sent Harry the anonymous Firebolt, prompting her to report it to Professor McGonagall?

14. **(Magic) (Open)** According to Lupin, why were he and his Animagus friends able to roam the grounds safely with him every month despite his being a werewolf?

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Why does Professor McGonagall take fifty points from Slytherin and hand out detentions to Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle, and Flint during the Ravenclaw match?

16. **(Plot) (Open)** What single argument does Harry make to stop Lupin and Black from killing Peter Pettigrew on the spot?

17. **(Places) (Open)** Lupin holds Harry's private Patronus lessons in a specific disused classroom — which subject was it previously used to teach?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** By the end of the book, how many genuine predictions has Professor Trelawney actually made, and what does Dumbledore jokingly suggest doing about it?

19. **(Characters) (Open)** What reward does Fudge recommend for Snape after the Shrieking Shack incident — and how does Snape's resulting bitterness end up costing Lupin his job?

20. **(Creatures) (Open)** How does Crookshanks help Harry and Hermione get into the Shrieking Shack, and what does he do once inside to protect Sirius?

21. **(Plot) (Open)** What realization does Harry come to, during the Time-Turner sequence, about who actually cast the stag Patronus that saved him earlier that night?

22. **(Magic) (Open)** What does Lupin reveal is the true explanation behind Hogsmeade's rumors that the Shrieking Shack was haunted?

23. **(Places) (Open)** Why does Harry come to believe that the "Grim" he kept glimpsing throughout the year wasn't really a death omen?

24. **(Characters) (Open)** Why had Lupin been battling guilt about Sirius Black for most of the school year?

25. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Why did Mrs. Weasley's sandwiches for the Hogwarts Express leave out corned beef?

26. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Why was Gryffindor's first Quidditch match switched from Slytherin to Hufflepuff, and what excuse let Slytherin off the hook?

## Answer Key — Quiz 4 (9/10)

1. He claims it was Sirius's reckless prank that nearly got Snape killed near the Whomping Willow, and that James only saved him to avoid trouble for himself — not out of real heroism.
2. He reasoned no one would ever suspect "weak, talentless" Pettigrew, making him the perfect decoy in a double-bluff.
3. A rat, nicknamed "Wormtail."
4. To give Remus Lupin, a werewolf since childhood, a safe and isolated place to transform every month without endangering anyone.
5. Because he is in his seventh and final year at Hogwarts, so it's his last opportunity to win the Quidditch Cup before leaving — driving him to push the team unusually hard.
6. He and Hermione save Buckbeak from execution, then use Buckbeak to fly up and free Sirius Black from his tower cell.
7. A large black dog, nicknamed "Padfoot."
8. A stag, nicknamed "Prongs" — the same form Harry's own Patronus takes.
9. Twelve years; he was cowardly "keeping an ear out" for signs of Voldemort's return, intending to resurface only once it seemed safe to do so.
10. Blood-flavored lollipops.
11. Because the Committee is "in Lucius Malfoy's pocket," according to Hagrid.
12. A torn/ragged left ear and a missing toe.
13. Sirius Black.
14. Because werewolves are dangerous mainly to humans, not to animals, so his friends could safely keep him company — and under control — in their animal forms.
15. Because the three "dementors" that appeared were actually Malfoy, Crabbe, Goyle, and Flint in disguise, attempting to sabotage Harry mid-match.
16. Harry insists Pettigrew be handed over to the dementors/Ministry rather than killed, saying he doesn't think his father would have wanted his friends to become killers.
17. History of Magic.
18. Two; Dumbledore jokes about offering her a pay raise.
19. Fudge recommends Snape for an Order of Merlin; when the embarrassing escape overshadows his reward, Snape spitefully lets slip Lupin's werewolf status to the Slytherins, forcing Lupin to resign.
20. Crookshanks presses a hidden knot on the Whomping Willow to freeze it so they can enter the tunnel; once inside, he sits protectively on Sirius, stopping Harry from attacking him.
21. He realizes the rescuer wasn't his father — it was himself, having gone back in time and cast the Patronus himself.
22. The screams villagers heard over the years were actually Lupin crying out in pain during his own werewolf transformations; Dumbledore let the "haunted house" rumor stand to protect his secret.
23. Because it was actually Sirius Black in his dog Animagus form, secretly watching over his godson rather than foretelling Harry's death.
24. He suspected Sirius had found a way into the castle as an unregistered Animagus but kept quiet, fearing that reporting it would expose his own schoolboy law-breaking to Dumbledore.
25. Because Ron had specifically asked her not to include it.
26. Malfoy claimed his hippogriff-injured arm was too severe to play, so Slytherin avoided a match in bad weather and Gryffindor faced Hufflepuff instead, with Cedric Diggory as Hufflepuff's new captain and Seeker.

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** What percentage did Hermione score on her Muggle Studies exam, according to Ron? → **Three hundred and twenty percent**
