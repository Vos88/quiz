# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*26 questions. Mostly open questions — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Magic) (Open)** What wood and core make up Harry's wand, and how long is it?

2. **(Plot) (Open)** What number is the secret Gringotts vault that Hagrid empties on Harry's birthday — the same one later reported broken into in the Daily Prophet?

3. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Sickles make up one wizarding Galleon?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers originally owned Ron's pet rat, Scabbers, before passing him down?

5. **(Characters)** Which of Ron's older brothers works for Gringotts bank in Africa?
   A) Charlie  B) Bill  C) Percy  D) Fred

6. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers is in Romania studying dragons?

7. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What was the final score of Harry's very first Quidditch match, Gryffindor versus Slytherin?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** What object, sent by his grandmother, does Neville own that glows red when its owner has forgotten something?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** What password gets the students back into Gryffindor Tower on the night of the planned "midnight duel" with Malfoy?

10. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is hidden inside a slice of Christmas pudding that Percy nearly bites down on at the Hogwarts Christmas feast?

11. **(Places) (Open)** Whose portrait guards the entrance to the Gryffindor common room?

12. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of the stone, taken from a goat's stomach, that acts as an antidote to most poisons?

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What curse does Draco Malfoy use on Neville in the lead-up to the Hufflepuff Quidditch match?

14. **(Numbers) (Open)** How old is Nicolas Flamel, and roughly how old is his wife, Perenelle, according to the book?

15. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the young centaur who rescues Harry in the Forbidden Forest after defying the other centaurs?

16. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many points does Professor McGonagall award Harry and Ron each, immediately after they rescue Hermione from the troll?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many total house points does Professor McGonagall deduct from Gryffindor after catching Harry, Hermione, Neville and Malfoy out of bed — and how many points is that per student?

18. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of the plant, encountered just beyond the trapdoor, that binds and strangles anyone who struggles against it?

19. **(Magic) (Open)** Whose old wizard chess set does Ron use — the set he later plays on against McGonagall's enchanted chessboard?

20. **(Places) (Open)** What hotel, in the town of Cokeworth, does Uncle Vernon take the family to in a failed attempt to escape the letters?

21. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the secondary school Harry is due to attend before his Hogwarts letter arrives?

22. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Name Gryffindor's three Chasers during Harry's first Quidditch match.

23. **(Quidditch)** Who captains the Slytherin Quidditch team during Harry's first year?
    A) Marcus Flint  B) Terence Higgs  C) Adrian Pucey  D) Bletchley

24. **(Creatures) (Open)** According to Firenze, what does drinking unicorn blood do to the drinker, even though it keeps them alive?

25. **(Creatures)** Which centaur angrily compares Firenze to "a common mule" for carrying Harry?
    A) Ronan  B) Firenze  C) Bane  D) Griphook

26. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What two ice-cream flavours does Hagrid buy for Harry in Diagon Alley?

## Answer Key — Quiz 3 (7/10)

1. Holly, with a phoenix feather core, 11 inches
2. Vault seven hundred and thirteen (713)
3. 17
4. Percy
5. B) Bill
6. Charlie
7. Gryffindor won, 170–60
8. A Remembrall
9. "Pig snout"
10. A silver Sickle
11. The Fat Lady
12. A bezoar
13. The Leg-Locker Curse (Locomotor Mortis)
14. 665 years old; Perenelle is 658
15. Firenze
16. 5 points each
17. 150 points total — 50 points from each of the four students
18. Devil's Snare
19. His grandfather's chess set
20. The Railview Hotel
21. Stonewall High
22. Angelina Johnson, Alicia Spinnet, and Katie Bell
23. A) Marcus Flint
24. It curses them to a half-life
25. C) Bane
26. Chocolate and raspberry (with chopped nuts)
