# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*20 questions. Mostly open questions — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Magic) (Open)** What wood and core make up Harry's wand, and how long is it?

2. **(Plot) (Open)** What number is the secret Gringotts vault that Hagrid empties on Harry's birthday — the same one later reported broken into in the Daily Prophet?

3. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Sickles make up one Galleon, and how many Knuts make up one Sickle?

4. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons does Harry's wand cost at Ollivanders?

5. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Ron's pet rat, a hand-me-down from an older brother?

6. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers works for Gringotts bank in Africa?

7. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers is in Romania studying dragons?

8. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What was the final score of Harry's very first Quidditch match, Gryffindor versus Slytherin?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** What object, sent by his grandmother, does Neville own that glows red when its owner has forgotten something?

10. **(Plot) (Open)** What password gets the students back into Gryffindor Tower on the night of the planned "midnight duel" with Malfoy?

11. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is hidden inside a slice of Christmas pudding that Percy nearly bites down on at the Hogwarts Christmas feast?

12. **(Places) (Open)** Whose portrait guards the entrance to the Gryffindor common room?

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of the stone, taken from a goat's stomach, that acts as an antidote to most poisons?

14. **(Magic) (Open)** What curse does Draco Malfoy use on Neville in the lead-up to the Hufflepuff Quidditch match?

15. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to the book, how old is Nicolas Flamel (and roughly how old is his wife, Perenelle)?

16. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the young centaur who rescues Harry in the Forbidden Forest after defying the other centaurs?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many total house points does Professor McGonagall deduct from Gryffindor after catching Harry, Hermione, Neville and Malfoy out of bed — and how many points is that per student?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many points does Neville get awarded at the end-of-year feast — the decisive points that win Gryffindor the House Cup?

19. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of the plant, encountered just beyond the trapdoor, that binds and strangles anyone who struggles against it?

20. **(Characters) (Open)** Whose old wizard chess set does Ron use — the set he later plays on against McGonagall's enchanted chessboard?

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 3 (7/10)

1. Holly, with a phoenix feather core, 11 inches
2. Vault seven hundred and thirteen (713)
3. 17 Sickles = 1 Galleon; 29 Knuts = 1 Sickle
4. 7 Galleons
5. Scabbers
6. Bill
7. Charlie
8. Gryffindor won, 170–60
9. A Remembrall
10. "Pig snout"
11. A silver Sickle
12. The Fat Lady
13. A bezoar
14. The Leg-Locker Curse (Locomotor Mortis)
15. 665 years old; Perenelle is 658
16. Firenze
17. 150 points total — 50 points from each of the four students
18. 10 points
19. Devil's Snare
20. His grandfather's (it was Ron's grandfather's chess set)

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** How many bottles, in total, are in Snape's final logic-puzzle room? → **7**
