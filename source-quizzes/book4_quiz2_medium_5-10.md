# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*20 questions, roughly half multiple choice / half open (medium tier = ~50/50), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** What is the name of the Daily Prophet reporter famous for her exaggerated and invented quotes?
   A) Rita Skeeter  B) Bathilda Bagshot  C) Sybill Trelawney  D) Gilderoy Lockhart

2. **(Characters)** Which house-elf is sacked by Barty Crouch Sr. after being found holding Harry's wand at the Quidditch World Cup?
   A) Winky  B) Dobby  C) Kreacher  D) Hokey

3. **(Characters)** Who is the Headmaster of Durmstrang Institute?
   A) Igor Karkaroff  B) Alastor Moody  C) Cornelius Fudge  D) Ludo Bagman

4. **(Places)** In which bathroom does Harry finally work out the golden egg's underwater clue?
   A) The Prefects' Bathroom  B) Moaning Myrtle's bathroom  C) The Room of Requirement  D) The Chamber of Secrets

5. **(Places)** What does the Durmstrang delegation arrive at Hogwarts in?
   A) A ship that rises from the lake  B) A flying carriage  C) The Knight Bus  D) A fleet of broomsticks

6. **(Magic)** What spell does Harry use to summon his Firebolt broomstick during the first task?
   A) Accio  B) Wingardium Leviosa  C) Expelliarmus  D) Alohomora

7. **(Quidditch)** What magical device, bought for 10 Galleons at the World Cup, lets the user replay and slow down moments of a match?
   A) Omnioculars  B) A Remembrall  C) A Foe-Glass  D) A Sneakoscope

8. **(Creatures)** What are the small water-dwelling creatures that attack Harry's ankles during the second task?
   A) Grindylows  B) Kappas  C) Merpeople  D) Nifflers

9. **(Numbers)** How many champions had originally been chosen for the Triwizard Tournament before Harry's name unexpectedly came out of the Goblet?
   A) Three  B) Two  C) Four  D) Five

10. **(Food/Sweets)** What drink does the house-elf Winky drink heavily and guiltily throughout the book?
    A) Butterbeer  B) Firewhisky  C) Pumpkin juice  D) Mulled mead

11. **(Plot) (Open)** What magical plant does Dobby bring Harry so he can breathe underwater during the second task?

12. **(Plot) (Open)** Which Triwizard champion is killed in the graveyard near the end of the book?

13. **(Plot) (Open)** For the second task, name which hostage each of the four champions had to rescue: Harry, Cedric, Krum, and Fleur.

14. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the incantation that conjures the Dark Mark in the night sky above the World Cup campsite?

15. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of Rita Skeeter's self-writing quill that embellishes and invents quotes?

16. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What is the name of the dangerous diving move Viktor Krum uses against the Irish Seeker in the World Cup final?

17. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Who is the Egyptian wizard who referees the Quidditch World Cup final?

18. **(Creatures) (Open)** What giant winged animals pull Madame Maxime's flying carriage to Hogwarts?

19. **(Numbers) (Open)** About how many years had passed since the Triwizard Tournament was last held, before this book?

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What French seafood dish is served at the Welcoming Feast for the visiting schools?

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 2 (5/10)

1. A) Rita Skeeter
2. A) Winky
3. A) Igor Karkaroff
4. A) The Prefects' Bathroom
5. A) A ship that rises from the lake
6. A) Accio
7. A) Omnioculars
8. A) Grindylows
9. A) Three
10. A) Butterbeer
11. Gillyweed
12. Cedric Diggory
13. Harry → Ron Weasley; Cedric → Cho Chang; Krum → Hermione Granger; Fleur → Gabrielle Delacour (her little sister)
14. Morsmordre
15. The Quick-Quotes Quill
16. The Wronski Feint
17. Hassan Mostafa
18. Palomino winged horses
19. About a hundred years (a century)
20. Bouillabaisse
