# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*20 questions. Mostly (here, entirely) open questions — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** Who is secretly disguised as Mad-Eye Moody for most of the school year?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Which house-elf secretly gives Harry gillyweed before the second task?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** Whose Bludger breaks Viktor Krum's nose during the Quidditch World Cup final?

4. **(Places) (Open)** What password lets Harry into the Prefects' Bathroom to open the golden egg?

5. **(Places) (Open)** Which statue stands near the entrance to the Prefects' Bathroom?

6. **(Plot) (Open)** What mistaken nickname does Barty Crouch Sr. repeatedly call Percy Weasley?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** How do Harry, Ron and Hermione get into the Hogwarts kitchens?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** Name the three ingredients used in Voldemort's rebirth potion, and how each is described as being given (e.g. "willingly given").

9. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does Mr Ollivander use on Harry's wand at the World Cup campsite to reveal its last-cast spell?

10. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of the rare magical phenomenon caused by Harry and Voldemort's twin-core wands connecting during their duel?

11. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What score out of ten does Karkaroff give Harry for the first task — conspicuously the lowest, and booed by the crowd?

12. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What score does Fleur Delacour receive for the second task, after being attacked by grindylows and failing to retrieve her hostage?

13. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How much (in Galleons, Sickles and Knuts) do Fred and George bet Ludo Bagman that Ireland would win but Krum would catch the Snitch?

14. **(Creatures) (Open)** Which dragon breed is Harry assigned to face in the first task?

15. **(Creatures) (Open)** Match each champion to the dragon they face in the first task: Cedric, Fleur, Krum, Harry.

16. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many wizards strong was the Ministry task force that built the Quidditch World Cup stadium?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many banned items are on Argus Filch's list by the start of this school year?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** Going into the third task, Harry and Cedric are tied for first place with how many points each?

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** How many butterbeers a day is Winky reported to be drinking in her depression?

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** How many barrels of mulled mead are rumoured to have been bought for the Yule Ball?

## Answer Key — Quiz 3 (7/10)

1. Barty Crouch Jr. (using Polyjuice Potion)
2. Dobby
3. Quigley (the Irish Chaser)
4. "Pine fresh"
5. The statue of Boris the Bewildered
6. "Weatherby"
7. By tickling the pear in the painting of a fruit bowl
8. Bone of the father (unknowingly given), flesh of the servant (willingly given), blood of the enemy (forcibly taken)
9. Prior Incantato
10. Priori Incantatem
11. 4
12. 25 points
13. 37 Galleons, 15 Sickles, and 3 Knuts
14. The Hungarian Horntail
15. Cedric – Swedish Short-Snout; Fleur – Common Welsh Green; Krum – Chinese Fireball; Harry – Hungarian Horntail
16. 500 wizards
17. 437
18. 85 points
19. About 6 a day
20. 800 barrels

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** According to Voldemort's graveyard speech, how many Death Eaters are "missing" from the circle he gathers? → **Six**
