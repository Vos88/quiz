# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*20 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Plot)** What is the name of the magical competition held at Hogwarts this year, last held a century earlier?
   A) The Triwizard Tournament  B) The Quidditch World Cup  C) The Duelling Championship  D) The Tournament of the Four Houses

2. **(Places)** Besides Hogwarts, which two other wizarding schools take part in the tournament?
   A) Beauxbatons and Durmstrang  B) Ilvermorny and Castelobruxo  C) Salem and Mahoutokoro  D) Uagadou and Beauxbatons

3. **(Characters)** Who is chosen as the Hogwarts champion by the Goblet of Fire?
   A) Cedric Diggory  B) Harry Potter  C) Oliver Wood  D) Neville Longbottom

4. **(Characters)** Who is the Durmstrang champion, also a famous international Quidditch Seeker?
   A) Viktor Krum  B) Igor Karkaroff  C) Cho Chang  D) Barty Crouch Jr.

5. **(Characters)** Who is Harry's godfather, who spends much of the book in hiding and keeps in touch by letter?
   A) Sirius Black  B) Remus Lupin  C) Mad-Eye Moody  D) Arthur Weasley

6. **(Places)** In which body of water does the Triwizard Tournament's second task take place?
   A) The Hogwarts lake  B) The Forbidden Forest  C) The Chamber of Secrets  D) Hogsmeade

7. **(Plot) (Open)** Put Harry's three Triwizard Tournament tasks in the order they happen: the hedge maze, the dragon challenge, the underwater lake rescue.

8. **(Plot)** Which Dark wizard is restored to a full body by the end of the book?
   A) Lord Voldemort  B) Gellert Grindelwald  C) Igor Karkaroff  D) Barty Crouch Sr.

9. **(Magic)** What ordinary-looking object turns out to be a Portkey at the end of the final task, whisking Harry and Cedric away?
   A) The Triwizard Cup  B) The Golden Egg  C) The Goblet of Fire  D) Harry's wand

10. **(Magic)** What is the name of the skull-and-serpent symbol used by Voldemort's followers, which appears terrifyingly in the sky above the World Cup campsite?
    A) The Dark Mark  B) The Grim  C) The Deathly Hallows symbol  D) The Sign of the Serpent

11. **(Quidditch)** Which two countries play in the Quidditch World Cup final at the start of the book?
    A) Ireland and Bulgaria  B) Ireland and England  C) Bulgaria and Germany  D) England and France

12. **(Quidditch)** Which country wins the Quidditch World Cup final on points?
    A) Ireland  B) Bulgaria  C) It ends in a draw  D) The match is abandoned

13. **(Quidditch)** Which player catches the Golden Snitch in the World Cup final, even though his team still loses?
    A) Viktor Krum  B) Cedric Diggory  C) Oliver Wood  D) Aidan Lynch

14. **(Creatures)** What dangerous creatures must each champion get past to steal a golden egg in the first task?
    A) Dragons  B) Trolls  C) A basilisk  D) Giant spiders

15. **(Creatures)** What are the beautiful mascots of the Bulgarian national Quidditch team, who turn ugly and bird-like when angry?
    A) Veela  B) Banshees  C) Sirens  D) Harpies

16. **(Numbers)** How many champions end up competing in the Triwizard Tournament once Harry's name is unexpectedly added?
    A) Four  B) Three  C) Two  D) Five

17. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons does the Triwizard Tournament's winner receive as prize money?
    A) 1,000 Galleons  B) 500 Galleons  C) 2,000 Galleons  D) 100 Galleons

18. **(Food/Sweets)** What sweet, invented by Fred and George, causes Dudley's tongue to swell to four feet long?
    A) A Ton-Tongue Toffee  B) A Canary Cream  C) A Chocolate Frog  D) A Fizzing Whizzbee

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is the name of Fred and George's joke sweet that temporarily turns the eater into a canary?

20. **(Food/Sweets)** What treat does Ron win in Care of Magical Creatures class, for his Niffler digging up the most gold?
    A) Honeydukes chocolate  B) A Chocolate Frog  C) Bertie Bott's Every Flavour Beans  D) Pumpkin pasties

---

1. A) The Triwizard Tournament
2. A) Beauxbatons and Durmstrang
3. A) Cedric Diggory
4. A) Viktor Krum
5. A) Sirius Black
6. A) The Hogwarts lake
7. Dragon challenge (Task 1), underwater lake rescue (Task 2), hedge maze (Task 3)
8. A) Lord Voldemort
9. A) The Triwizard Cup
10. A) The Dark Mark
11. A) Ireland and Bulgaria
12. A) Ireland
13. A) Viktor Krum
14. A) Dragons
15. A) Veela
16. A) Four
17. A) 1,000 Galleons
18. A) A Ton-Tongue Toffee
19. Canary Creams
20. A) Honeydukes chocolate
