# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*24 questions, entirely open — for the true Order of the Phoenix obsessives.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** What baby-talk nickname does Aunt Petunia use for Dudley?

2. **(Places) (Open)** In which pub, in which town, does the water-skiing budgie "Bungy" live, according to the Muggle news item Harry overhears?

3. **(Plot) (Open)** What fake competition does Tonks claim the Dursleys have been shortlisted for, in order to lure them out of the house before the Order's rescue party arrives?

4. **(Magic) (Open)** What object sits inside the crystal bell jar in the Time Room of the Department of Mysteries, endlessly aging and reverting?

5. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What position does Ginny Weasley say she'd actually prefer to play, having secretly borrowed her brothers' brooms since age six?

6. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Mrs. Figg's cat, who serves as her lookout on Privet Drive?

7. **(Numbers) (Open)** In what years did Healer-turned-Hogwarts-Headmistress Dilys Derwent serve at St. Mungo's, according to her portrait's caption?

8. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** In Fred and George's Puking Pastilles, which colored half makes the eater vomit, and which half cures it instantly?

9. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which Order member, killed two weeks after Moody's old photograph was taken, died along with her entire family?

10. **(Characters) (Open)** What nickname does Sirius use for his Animagus dog-form alias, the disguise he wears when he secretly escorts Harry to King's Cross?

11. **(Places) (Open)** What is the exact name of the "Dangerous" St. Mungo's ward — Serious Bites — where Arthur Weasley recovers from his snakebite?

12. **(Plot) (Open)** What item is found in Mundungus Fletcher's bundle the night the dementors attack Harry and Dudley, implying he'd stolen or traded for it?

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What potion must Arthur Weasley take hourly at St. Mungo's to counteract the snake venom that keeps his wound from closing?

14. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What Quidditch team does Cho Chang support, shown on the badge Ron rudely questions her about?

15. **(Creatures) (Open)** What term do centaurs use for human children, meaning they are exempt from centaur violence as "innocents"?

16. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Sickles did Mundungus Fletcher once charge Fred and George for a bag of knarl quills, cited by Fred as proof of his overpricing?

17. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What disguised plant — mistaken by St. Mungo's staff for a harmless Flitterbloom — is used to strangle Ministry employee Broderick Bode in his hospital bed?

18. **(Deaths) (Open)** Who vanished six months after Moody's old Order photograph was taken, his body never found?

19. **(Characters) (Open)** What is Percy Weasley's full name, as used for his role as Court Scribe at Harry's hearing?

20. **(Places) (Open)** What object, used as an umbrella stand in the Grimmauld Place front hallway, is made from a severed troll's leg?

21. **(Plot) (Open)** What small object does Neville secretly pocket and keep, rather than discard, after his mother gives it to him on their St. Mungo's visit?

22. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does a much-younger Professor Flitwick use to sweep over a hundred exam parchments into his arms at once, comically knocking himself off his feet, in Snape's memory?

23. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons had Harry originally planned to donate to the Fountain of Magical Brethren if he wasn't expelled?

24. **(Deaths) (Open)** What was Benjy Fenwick's grim fate in the First War, according to Moody?

2. The Five Feathers pub, in Barnsley

4. A tiny jeweled egg that hatches into a hummingbird, ages, and reverts back into an egg
5. Chaser
6. Mr. Tibbles
7. 1722–1741
8. Orange makes you vomit; purple cures it
9. Marlene McKinnon
10. "Snuffles"
11. The Dai Llewellyn Ward
12. An Invisibility Cloak
13. Blood-Replenishing Potion
14. The Tutshill Tornados
15. Foals
16. Six Sickles
17. Devil's Snare
18. Caradoc Dearborn
19. Percy Ignatius Weasley
20. An umbrella stand made from a severed troll's leg
21. An empty Drooble's Blowing Gum wrapper
22. Accio
