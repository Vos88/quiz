# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*20 questions, entirely open — for the true Order of the Phoenix obsessives.*

1. **(Quidditch) (Open)** According to Ron's own boast, what speed can his new Cleansweep Eleven broom reach — naught to how many miles an hour, in how many seconds?

2. **(Places) (Open)** In which pub, in which town, does the water-skiing budgie "Bungy" live, according to the Muggle news item Harry overhears?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** What baby-talk nickname does Aunt Petunia use for Dudley?

4. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Mrs. Figg's cat, who serves as her lookout on Privet Drive?

5. **(Plot) (Open)** What item is found in Mundungus Fletcher's bundle the night the dementors attack Harry and Dudley, implying he'd stolen or traded for it?

6. **(Plot) (Open)** What fake competition does Tonks claim the Dursleys have been shortlisted for, in order to lure them out of the house before the Order's rescue party arrives?

7. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many years before the events of this book does Dumbledore say he first guessed that Harry's scar signified a magical connection to Voldemort?

8. **(Magic) (Open)** What object sits inside the crystal bell jar in the Time Room of the Department of Mysteries, endlessly aging and reverting?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** What two sets of initials appear on the handwritten label beneath Harry's prophecy orb?

10. **(Characters) (Open)** Name at least six of the Death Eaters explicitly identified by name during the Battle of the Department of Mysteries.

11. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the name of Ginny Weasley's signature hex, used devastatingly on Draco Malfoy during their escape from the Inquisitorial Squad?

12. **(Characters) (Open)** Who is the Healer-in-Charge of the ward treating Arthur Weasley at St. Mungo's?

13. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** In Fred and George's Puking Pastilles, which colored half makes the eater vomit, and which half cures it instantly?

14. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Extendable Ears do Fred and George produce to eavesdrop on the adults outside Arthur Weasley's hospital ward?

15. **(Magic) (Open)** What potion must Arthur Weasley take hourly at St. Mungo's to counteract the snake venom that keeps his wound from closing?

16. **(Characters) (Open)** Which former Hogwarts headmaster's portrait — later revealed to be Sirius's own great-great-grandfather — hangs in both Dumbledore's office and Sirius's bedroom at Grimmauld Place?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** In what years did Healer-turned-Hogwarts-Headmistress Dilys Derwent serve at St. Mungo's, according to her portrait's caption?

18. **(Plot) (Open)** What small object does Neville secretly pocket and keep, rather than discard, after his mother gives it to him on their St. Mungo's visit?

19. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the St. Mungo's ward reserved for permanent, long-term spell damage, where the Longbottoms live?

20. **(Deaths) (Open)** According to Mad-Eye Moody's old Order of the Phoenix photograph, which First War member was killed personally by Voldemort himself, rather than by other Death Eaters?

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 4 (9/10)

1. Naught to seventy miles an hour in ten seconds
2. The Five Feathers pub, in Barnsley
3. "Ickle Diddykins" (also called "Dinky Diddydums" and "Big D")
4. Mr. Tibbles
5. An Invisibility Cloak
6. The All-England Best-Kept Suburban Lawn Competition
7. Fifteen years
8. A tiny jeweled egg that hatches into a hummingbird, ages, and reverts back into an egg
9. S.P.T. and A.P.W.B.D.
10. Any six of: Lucius Malfoy, Bellatrix Lestrange, Antonin Dolohov, Walden Macnair, Rookwood, Nott, Jugson, Rabastan Lestrange, Avery, Mulciber, Crabbe (Sr.)
11. The Bat-Bogey Hex
12. Hippocrates Smethwyck
13. Orange makes you vomit; purple cures it
14. Five
15. Blood-Replenishing Potion
16. Phineas Nigellus (Black)
17. 1722–1741
18. An empty Drooble's Blowing Gum wrapper
19. The Janus Thickey Ward
20. Dorcas Meadowes

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** Exactly how many Sickles did Mundungus Fletcher once charge Fred and George for a bag of knarl quills, cited by Fred as proof of his overpricing? → **Six Sickles**
