# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*30 questions, roughly half multiple choice / half open (medium tier = ~50/50), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** What is Harry's mother's first name?
   A) Petunia  B) Lavender  C) Lily  D) Ginny

2. **(Characters)** What is Harry's father's first name?
   A) James  B) Sirius  C) Remus  D) Arthur

3. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the village where Harry's parents lived and died, mentioned when Dumbledore and McGonagall discuss Voldemort's downfall?

4. **(Magic) (Open)** What magical object, created by Nicolas Flamel, can turn metal into gold and produce the Elixir of Life?

5. **(Quidditch)** How many points is catching the Golden Snitch worth?
   A) 50  B) 150  C) 100  D) 200

6. **(Places)** What is the name of the wizarding bank, run by goblins, where Harry's money is kept?
   A) The Ministry Vaults  B) Wizengamot Bank  C) The Exchange  D) Gringotts

7. **(Characters) (Open)** Which Gryffindor Quidditch team captain recruits Harry to be the team's new Seeker?

8. **(Places) (Open)** In which room of the school do Harry and Ron accidentally lock in the mountain troll on Hallowe'en, not realising someone is already inside?

9. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Neville Longbottom's toad, which he is constantly losing?
   A) Oswald  B) Norbert  C) Trevor  D) Scabbers

10. **(Plot) (Open)** What magical object, which once belonged to his father, does Harry receive anonymously at Christmas?

11. **(Creatures)** What is the name of caretaker Argus Filch's cat?
    A) Crookshanks  B) Mrs Norris  C) Tibbles  D) Hedwig

12. **(Numbers)** Which three coins make up wizarding currency?
    A) Galleons, Crowns, Pence  B) Sickles, Knuts, Pennies  C) Doubloons, Sickles, Knuts  D) Galleons, Sickles, Knuts

13. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the full name of the pale Slytherin boy who becomes Harry's rival?

14. **(Plot) (Open)** What does the Mirror of Erised show to the person looking into it?

15. **(Plot) (Open)** Name all four Hogwarts houses.

16. **(Characters)** Which professor teaches Transfiguration and is also Gryffindor's Head of House?
    A) Professor Flitwick  B) Professor McGonagall  C) Professor Sprout  D) Professor Snape

17. **(Creatures)** What breed of dragon hatches from the egg Hagrid wins in a card game?
    A) Norwegian Ridgeback  B) Hungarian Horntail  C) Common Welsh Green  D) Chinese Fireball

18. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What brand of wizarding sweet is famous for its unpredictable (and sometimes disgusting) flavours, like earwax or grass?

19. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the forest bordering the Hogwarts grounds where Harry serves a detention?

20. **(Plot)** Which stuttering professor turns out to be hiding Lord Voldemort under his turban?
    A) Professor Snape  B) Professor Lockhart  C) Professor Quirrell  D) Professor Flitwick

21. **(Magic)** What charm, cast with the wand motion "swish and flick," makes objects float?
    A) Wingardium Leviosa  B) Alohomora  C) Petrificus Totalus  D) Expelliarmus

22. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does Hermione cast on Neville in the common room to stop him getting in their way, binding his arms and legs together?

23. **(Magic)** What object does Dumbledore use on Privet Drive to suck the light out of the street lamps?
    A) The Sorting Hat  B) The Remembrall  C) The Mirror of Erised  D) The Put-Outer

24. **(Quidditch)** What position does Oliver Wood play for the Gryffindor Quidditch team?
    A) Seeker  B) Keeper  C) Chaser  D) Beater

25. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Name Gryffindor's joking pair of twin Beaters.

26. **(Quidditch)** What broomstick does Draco Malfoy boast that his family owns?
    A) The Nimbus Two Thousand  B) The Cleansweep Five  C) The Firebolt  D) The Comet Two Sixty

27. **(Numbers)** How many points does a Chaser earn by scoring a goal with the Quaffle?
    A) 10  B) 20  C) 50  D) 100

28. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Dumbledore, how many years had the wizarding world had "precious little to celebrate" before Voldemort's downfall?

29. **(Food/Sweets)** What sweet does Dumbledore offer Professor McGonagall on Privet Drive, calling it his favourite?
    A) A Chocolate Frog  B) A Liquorice Wand  C) A sherbet lemon  D) A Pumpkin Pasty

30. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What homemade treat does Hagrid bake for Harry and Ron that nearly breaks their teeth?
