# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*20 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** What is the name of Harry Potter's cousin, with whom he lives?
   A) Dudley  B) Draco  C) Neville  D) Ron

2. **(Places)** What is the name of the street where Harry lives with the Dursleys?
   A) Privet Drive  B) Diagon Alley  C) Spinner's End  D) Grimmauld Place

3. **(Characters)** Who is the giant of a man who delivers Harry's letter and takes him to buy his school supplies?
   A) Hagrid  B) Dumbledore  C) Snape  D) Filch

4. **(Food/Sweets)** What sweet, with a collectible card inside, does Harry try for the first time on the Hogwarts Express?
   A) A Chocolate Frog  B) A Pumpkin Pasty  C) A Cauldron Cake  D) A Liquorice Wand

5. **(Places)** What is the name of the school Harry attends?
   A) Hogwarts  B) Beauxbatons  C) Durmstrang  D) Ilvermorny

6. **(Plot)** Which Hogwarts house is Harry sorted into?
   A) Gryffindor  B) Slytherin  C) Ravenclaw  D) Hufflepuff

7. **(Characters) (Open)** Name Harry's two best friends at Hogwarts.

8. **(Magic)** What magical object do new students put on to be sorted into their houses?
   A) The Sorting Hat  B) A wand  C) The Mirror of Erised  D) A Remembrall

9. **(Quidditch)** What position does Harry play on his house Quidditch team?
   A) Seeker  B) Keeper  C) Beater  D) Chaser

10. **(Creatures)** What animal does Hagrid give Harry as a birthday present?
    A) An owl  B) A cat  C) A toad  D) A rat

11. **(Characters)** Who is the headmaster of Hogwarts?
    A) Albus Dumbledore  B) Severus Snape  C) Filius Flitwick  D) Rubeus Hagrid

12. **(Places)** What is the name of the magical shopping street in London where Harry buys his school supplies?
    A) Diagon Alley  B) Knockturn Alley  C) Hogsmeade  D) Privet Drive

13. **(Characters)** Who is the strict Potions Master who seems to dislike Harry from the start?
    A) Snape  B) Quirrell  C) Flitwick  D) Sprout

14. **(Plot)** What shape is the scar on Harry's forehead?
    A) A lightning bolt  B) A star  C) A crescent moon  D) A circle

15. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the enormous three-headed dog guarding a trapdoor on the third floor?
    A) Fluffy  B) Fang  C) Norbert  D) Trevor

16. **(Creatures)** What kind of snake escapes from its enclosure when Harry visits the zoo?
    A) A boa constrictor  B) A cobra  C) A python  D) A viper

17. **(Magic)** What is the name of the spell Hermione uses to magically unlock a locked door?
    A) Alohomora  B) Wingardium Leviosa  C) Petrificus Totalus  D) Expelliarmus

18. **(Quidditch)** What is the small, winged golden ball that Seekers chase during a Quidditch match called?
    A) The Golden Snitch  B) The Quaffle  C) A Bludger  D) A Firebolt

19. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the surname of Ron's family?

20. **(Numbers)** From which platform at King's Cross station does the Hogwarts Express depart?
    A) Platform Nine and Three-Quarters  B) Platform Ten  C) Platform One  D) Platform Thirteen
