# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*28 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** What is the name of Harry Potter's cousin, with whom he lives?
   A) Draco  B) Neville  C) Dudley  D) Ron

2. **(Places)** What is the name of the street where Harry lives with the Dursleys?
   A) Privet Drive  B) Diagon Alley  C) Spinner's End  D) Grimmauld Place

3. **(Characters)** Who is the giant of a man who delivers Harry's letter and takes him to buy his school supplies?
   A) Dumbledore  B) Snape  C) Hagrid  D) Filch

4. **(Food/Sweets)** What sweet, with a collectible card inside, does Harry try for the first time on the Hogwarts Express?
   A) A Pumpkin Pasty  B) A Cauldron Cake  C) A Liquorice Wand  D) A Chocolate Frog

5. **(Places)** What is the name of the school Harry attends?
   A) Beauxbatons  B) Hogwarts  C) Durmstrang  D) Ilvermorny

6. **(Plot)** Which Hogwarts house is Harry sorted into?
   A) Slytherin  B) Ravenclaw  C) Gryffindor  D) Hufflepuff

7. **(Characters) (Open)** Name Harry's two best friends at Hogwarts.

8. **(Magic)** What magical object do new students put on to be sorted into their houses?
   A) A wand  B) The Mirror of Erised  C) A Remembrall  D) The Sorting Hat

9. **(Quidditch)** What position does Harry play on his house Quidditch team?
   A) Keeper  B) Beater  C) Seeker  D) Chaser

10. **(Creatures)** What animal does Hagrid give Harry as a birthday present?
    A) A cat  B) An owl  C) A toad  D) A rat

11. **(Characters)** Who is the headmaster of Hogwarts?
    A) Albus Dumbledore  B) Severus Snape  C) Filius Flitwick  D) Rubeus Hagrid

12. **(Places)** What is the name of the magical shopping street in London where Harry buys his school supplies?
    A) Knockturn Alley  B) Hogsmeade  C) Privet Drive  D) Diagon Alley

13. **(Characters)** Who is the strict Potions Master who seems to dislike Harry from the start?
    A) Quirrell  B) Snape  C) Flitwick  D) Sprout

14. **(Plot)** What shape is the scar on Harry's forehead?
    A) A star  B) A crescent moon  C) A lightning bolt  D) A circle

15. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the enormous three-headed dog guarding a trapdoor on the third floor?
    A) Fluffy  B) Fang  C) Norbert  D) Trevor

16. **(Creatures)** What kind of snake escapes from its enclosure when Harry visits the zoo?
    A) A cobra  B) A python  C) A viper  D) A boa constrictor

17. **(Magic)** What is the name of the spell Hermione uses to magically unlock a locked door?
    A) Alohomora  B) Wingardium Leviosa  C) Petrificus Totalus  D) Expelliarmus

18. **(Quidditch)** What is the small, winged golden ball that Seekers chase during a Quidditch match called?
    A) The Quaffle  B) The Golden Snitch  C) A Bludger  D) A Firebolt

19. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the surname of Ron's family?

20. **(Numbers)** From which platform at King's Cross station does the Hogwarts Express depart?
    A) Platform Ten  B) Platform One  C) Platform Thirteen  D) Platform Nine and Three-Quarters

21. **(Quidditch)** What is the name of the brand-new broomstick Harry receives as a gift in his first year?
    A) The Nimbus Two Thousand  B) The Comet Two Sixty  C) The Firebolt  D) The Cleansweep Seven

22. **(Places)** What Great Hall feature is bewitched to look like the sky outside?
    A) The floor  B) The walls  C) The ceiling  D) The windows

23. **(Magic)** What is the name of the magical mirror that shows whoever looks into it their heart's deepest desire?
    A) The Foe-Glass  B) The Mirror of Erised  C) The Pensieve  D) The Sorting Mirror

24. **(Quidditch)** What is the name of the red ball that Chasers throw through the hoops to score goals?
    A) The Quaffle  B) The Golden Snitch  C) A Bludger  D) A Snitchball

25. **(Creatures)** What name does Hagrid give to the dragon he hatches from a won egg?
    A) Fluffy  B) Norbert  C) Aragog  D) Buckbeak

26. **(Numbers)** How many Chasers play on each Hogwarts Quidditch team?
    A) Two  B) Four  C) One  D) Three

27. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What two words, written in green icing, does Hagrid put on the birthday cake he gives Harry?

28. **(Places)** What shop in Diagon Alley sells Harry his owl?
    A) Eeylops Owl Emporium  B) Ollivanders  C) Flourish and Blotts  D) Gringotts
