# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*20 questions, roughly half multiple choice / half open (medium tier = ~50/50), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is the Gryffindor Quidditch team's captain and Keeper this year?
   A) Oliver Wood  B) Marcus Flint  C) Percy Weasley  D) Cedric Diggory

2. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the Weasleys' youngest child, starting her first year at Hogwarts this book?

3. **(Characters)** Which first-year Gryffindor boy is a Muggle-born photography enthusiast who idolises Harry?
   A) Colin Creevey  B) Justin Finch-Fletchley  C) Dean Thomas  D) Seamus Finnigan

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the Hufflepuff boy — a Muggle-born who was "down for Eton" — who becomes suspicious of Harry?

5. **(Places)** Where does Harry accidentally end up after mispronouncing his Floo powder destination?
   A) Knockturn Alley  B) Spinner's End  C) Horizont Alley  D) Hogsmeade

6. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the shop in Knockturn Alley that deals in Dark artefacts, where Harry overhears Lucius Malfoy?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** What dangerous trick does the house-elf Dobby play during Harry's first Quidditch match of the year?

8. **(Plot)** Which of Filch's pets is found petrified, hanging from a torch bracket?
   A) Mrs Norris  B) Crookshanks  C) Fang  D) Trevor

9. **(Magic)** What potion do Hermione, Harry and Ron brew in Moaning Myrtle's bathroom to disguise themselves as Crabbe and Goyle?
   A) Polyjuice Potion  B) Felix Felicis  C) Veritaserum  D) Amortentia

10. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does Draco Malfoy use in the Duelling Club that conjures a snake?

11. **(Magic)** What term describes a wizard who can speak Parseltongue?
    A) A Parselmouth  B) A Legilimens  C) An Animagus  D) A Metamorphmagus

12. **(Quidditch)** Who is the Slytherin team's new Seeker this year, given a brand-new broomstick by his father?
    A) Draco Malfoy  B) Marcus Flint  C) Adrian Pucey  D) Terence Higgs

13. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What model of broomstick does Lucius Malfoy buy for the entire Slytherin Quidditch team?

14. **(Creatures)** What giant, ancient, blind spider do Harry and Ron meet deep in the Forbidden Forest?
    A) Aragog  B) Grawp  C) Mosag  D) Norbert

15. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Hagrid's pet dog, who accompanies the boys into the Forbidden Forest?

16. **(Numbers)** How many House points does Percy deduct from Gryffindor for catching Harry, Ron and Hermione near Moaning Myrtle's bathroom?
    A) Five  B) Ten  C) Fifteen  D) Twenty

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Percy, how many House points does the Quidditch win over Slytherin earn Gryffindor?

18. **(Food/Sweets)** What does Mrs Weasley send Harry for Christmas, alongside a hand-knitted jumper?
    A) A plum cake  B) A treacle tart  C) A chocolate gateau  D) A toffee cake

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Besides rotten fish and burnt cakes, name one other foul dish served at Nearly Headless Nick's Deathday Party.

20. **(Plot) (Open)** Put these three events in the order they happen in the book: (a) Dobby bewitches a Bludger during a Quidditch match; (b) Riddle's diary is stolen from Harry's trunk; (c) Ginny is taken into the Chamber of Secrets.

---

1. A) Oliver Wood
2. Ginny Weasley
3. A) Colin Creevey
4. Justin Finch-Fletchley
5. A) Knockturn Alley
6. Borgin and Burkes
7. He bewitches a Bludger to target only Harry during the match
8. A) Mrs Norris
9. A) Polyjuice Potion
10. Serpensortia
11. A) A Parselmouth
12. A) Draco Malfoy
13. Nimbus Two Thousand and One
14. A) Aragog
15. Fang
16. A) Five
17. 50 points
18. A) A plum cake
19. Maggoty haggis (moldy cheese also accepted)
20. a, b, c
