# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*30 questions, roughly half multiple choice / half open (medium tier = ~50/50), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is the Gryffindor Quidditch team's captain and Keeper this year?
   A) Oliver Wood  B) Marcus Flint  C) Percy Weasley  D) Cedric Diggory

2. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the Weasleys' youngest child, starting her first year at Hogwarts this book?

3. **(Characters)** Which first-year Gryffindor boy is a Muggle-born photography enthusiast who idolises Harry?
   A) Justin Finch-Fletchley  B) Dean Thomas  C) Seamus Finnigan  D) Colin Creevey

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the Hufflepuff boy — a Muggle-born who was "down for Eton" — who becomes suspicious of Harry?

5. **(Places)** Where does Harry accidentally end up after mispronouncing his Floo powder destination?
   A) Spinner's End  B) Horizont Alley  C) Knockturn Alley  D) Hogsmeade

6. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the shop in Knockturn Alley that deals in Dark artefacts, where Harry overhears Lucius Malfoy?

7. **(Places)** In which greenhouse do second-years re-pot screaming Mandrakes while wearing earmuffs?
   A) Greenhouse Three  B) Greenhouse One  C) Greenhouse Five  D) The Herbology shed

8. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the wizard prison, mentioned for the first time in this book?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** What dangerous trick does the house-elf Dobby play during Harry's first Quidditch match of the year?

10. **(Plot)** What potion do Hermione, Harry and Ron brew in Moaning Myrtle's bathroom to disguise themselves as Crabbe and Goyle?
    A) Felix Felicis  B) Veritaserum  C) Polyjuice Potion  D) Amortentia

11. **(Plot) (Open)** What item of clothing does Harry trick Lucius Malfoy into giving Dobby, setting him free?

12. **(Plot)** What cursed item does Draco Malfoy pester his father to buy him in Borgin and Burkes?
    A) A Vanishing Cabinet  B) The Hand of Glory  C) A cursed opal necklace  D) A cursed ring

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What spell does Draco Malfoy use in the Duelling Club that conjures a snake?

14. **(Magic)** What term describes a wizard who can speak Parseltongue?
    A) A Parselmouth  B) A Legilimens  C) An Animagus  D) A Metamorphmagus

15. **(Magic) (Open)** What potion must Madam Pomfrey use to regrow all the bones in Harry's arm overnight?

16. **(Magic)** What plant must mature before Madam Pomfrey can revive the Petrified victims?
    A) Fluxweed  B) Wolfsbane  C) Mandrake  D) Valerian sprigs

17. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What exact model of broomstick does Lucius Malfoy buy for the entire Slytherin Quidditch team?

18. **(Quidditch)** Who is named the new Slytherin Seeker this year, given a brand-new broomstick by his father?
    A) Marcus Flint  B) Draco Malfoy  C) Adrian Pucey  D) Terence Higgs

19. **(Quidditch) (Open)** According to Oliver Wood, why did Gryffindor lose the Quidditch Cup the previous year?

20. **(Quidditch)** What older broomstick model do Fred and George ride, in contrast to Slytherin's brand-new brooms?
    A) Cleansweep Five  B) Nimbus Two Thousand  C) Shooting Star  D) Comet Two Sixty

21. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Hagrid's pet dog, who accompanies the boys into the Forbidden Forest?

22. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Dumbledore's phoenix?
    A) Hedwig  B) Fawkes  C) Errol  D) Hermes

23. **(Creatures)** What small, electric-blue, mischievous creatures does Lockhart release into his first Defence Against the Dark Arts class?
    A) Doxies  B) Bowtruckles  C) Nifflers  D) Cornish pixies

24. **(Creatures) (Open)** What biting, "teething" magical plant grows in Greenhouse Three?

25. **(Numbers)** How many House points does Percy deduct from Gryffindor for catching Harry, Ron and Hermione near Moaning Myrtle's bathroom?
    A) Ten  B) Five  C) Fifteen  D) Twenty

26. **(Numbers)** According to Percy, how many House points does the Quidditch win over Slytherin earn Gryffindor?
    A) Twenty-five  B) Seventy-five  C) One hundred  D) Fifty

27. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many questions are on Gilderoy Lockhart's self-quiz about himself in his first Defence Against the Dark Arts lesson?

28. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Besides rotten fish and burnt cakes, name one other foul dish served at Nearly Headless Nick's Deathday Party.

29. **(Food/Sweets)** What homemade sweet does Hagrid give Harry as a Christmas present?
    A) Chocolate frogs  B) Sugar quills  C) Treacle fudge  D) Pumpkin pasties

30. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What do Harry, Ron and Hermione lace with Sleeping Draught to knock out Crabbe and Goyle?
