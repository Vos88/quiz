# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*20 questions, an even split of multiple choice and open questions, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Magic)** What spell does Lupin use to make Peeves flee by shooting chewing gum up his nose?
   A) Waddiwasi  B) Riddikulus  C) Expelliarmus  D) Alohomora

2. **(Magic) (Open)** What charm does Hermione cast on Harry's glasses to repel rain during the stormy Quidditch match against Hufflepuff?

3. **(Magic) (Open)** What charm does Lupin use to mend Ron's broken leg in the Shrieking Shack?

4. **(Creatures)** What shape-shifting creature does Lupin's class face in his first practical lesson, released from a wardrobe?
   A) A boggart  B) A grindylow  C) A hinkypunk  D) A Red Cap

5. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Hagrid's hippogriff, put on trial after wounding Malfoy?

6. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Hermione's new pet, a huge ginger cat that keeps attacking Scabbers?
   A) Crookshanks  B) Mrs. Norris  C) Hedwig  D) Trevor

7. **(Characters)** Which professor predicts, from tea leaves, that Harry will die, invoking the omen of "the Grim"?
   A) Professor Trelawney  B) Professor Sprout  C) Professor Sinistra  D) Professor Vector

8. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the executioner brought in to kill Buckbeak?

9. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Percy Weasley's Ravenclaw girlfriend?

10. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the dilapidated, supposedly haunted house in Hogsmeade that turns out to be where Lupin transforms?

11. **(Places)** What password lets students back into Gryffindor Tower after the start-of-term feast?
    A) Fortuna Major  B) Pig Snout  C) Dissendium  D) Mischief Managed

12. **(Plot)** Whose portrait does Sirius Black slash when he first breaks into Gryffindor Tower?
    A) The Fat Lady  B) Sir Cadogan  C) The Grey Lady  D) The Bloody Baron

13. **(Plot)** What magical map, once confiscated by Filch years earlier, do Fred and George give Harry?
    A) The Marauder's Map  B) The Invisibility Cloak  C) A Time-Turner  D) A Foe-Glass

14. **(Plot) (Open)** Name all four nicknames of the Marauders, as signed on the map.

15. **(Quidditch)** Who is Hufflepuff's new Seeker and captain, who catches the Snitch when Harry falls off his broom during the storm?
    A) Cedric Diggory  B) Roger Davies  C) Marcus Flint  D) Oliver Wood

16. **(Quidditch) (Open)** By how many points must Gryffindor beat Slytherin in the final to win the Quidditch Cup?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Sickles is the basic Knight Bus fare to London?

18. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons does Percy bet Penelope Clearwater on the outcome of the Ravenclaw Quidditch match?
    A) Ten  B) Five  C) Twenty  D) Fifty

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Name the Hogsmeade sweetshop where students buy treats like Fizzing Whizbees and Ice Mice.

20. **(Food/Sweets)** What drink do Ron and Hermione bring back for Harry from the Three Broomsticks, since he couldn't go to Hogsmeade?
    A) Butterbeer  B) Firewhisky  C) Pumpkin juice  D) Gillywater
