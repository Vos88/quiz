# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*28 questions, an even split of multiple choice and open questions, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Magic)** What spell does Lupin use to make Peeves flee by shooting chewing gum up his nose?
   A) Riddikulus  B) Waddiwasi  C) Expelliarmus  D) Alohomora

2. **(Magic) (Open)** What charm does Hermione cast on Harry's glasses to repel rain during the stormy Quidditch match against Hufflepuff?

3. **(Magic) (Open)** What charm does Lupin use to mend Ron's broken leg in the Shrieking Shack?

4. **(Creatures)** What shape-shifting creature does Lupin's class face in his first practical lesson, released from a wardrobe?
   A) A grindylow  B) A hinkypunk  C) A boggart  D) A Red Cap

5. **(Creatures) (Open)** What water-demon creature, with long brittle fingers, does Harry see kept in a tank in Professor Lupin's office?

6. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Hermione's new pet, a huge ginger cat that keeps attacking Scabbers?
   A) Mrs. Norris  B) Hedwig  C) Trevor  D) Crookshanks

7. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of the executioner brought in to kill Buckbeak?

8. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the name of Percy Weasley's Ravenclaw girlfriend?

9. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the dilapidated, supposedly haunted house in Hogsmeade that turns out to be where Lupin transforms?

10. **(Places)** What password lets students back into Gryffindor Tower after the start-of-term feast?
    A) Fortuna Major  B) Pig Snout  C) Dissendium  D) Mischief Managed

11. **(Plot)** Whose portrait does Sirius Black slash when he first breaks into Gryffindor Tower?
    A) Sir Cadogan  B) The Grey Lady  C) The Bloody Baron  D) The Fat Lady

12. **(Plot)** What magical map, once confiscated by Filch years earlier, do Fred and George give Harry?
    A) The Marauder's Map  B) The Invisibility Cloak  C) A Time-Turner  D) A Foe-Glass

13. **(Plot) (Open)** Name all four nicknames of the Marauders, as signed on the map.

14. **(Quidditch)** Who is Hufflepuff's new Seeker and captain, who catches the Snitch when Harry falls off his broom during the storm?
    A) Roger Davies  B) Cedric Diggory  C) Marcus Flint  D) Oliver Wood

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many players make up a full Quidditch team?

16. **(Numbers)** How many Sickles is the basic Knight Bus fare to London?
    A) Eleven  B) Nine  C) Thirteen  D) Fifteen

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many children does the Weasley family have in total?

18. **(Quidditch)** How many Galleons does Percy bet Penelope Clearwater on the outcome of the Ravenclaw Quidditch match?
    A) Five  B) Ten  C) Twenty  D) Fifty

19. **(Food/Sweets)** What drink do Ron and Hermione bring back for Harry from the Three Broomsticks, since he couldn't go to Hogsmeade?
    A) Butterbeer  B) Firewhisky  C) Pumpkin juice  D) Gillywater

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What cake-shaped treat is sold from the trolley on the Hogwarts Express?

21. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers works as a curse-breaker for Gringotts in Egypt?

22. **(Characters)** Who is the conductor of the Knight Bus, to whom Harry gives the false name "Neville Longbottom"?
    A) Ernie Prang  B) Tom  C) Stan Shunpike  D) Cornelius Fudge

23. **(Places)** What is the name of the ice cream parlor in Diagon Alley where Harry does his homework over the summer?
    A) Honeydukes  B) Zonko's  C) The Three Broomsticks  D) Florean Fortescue's

24. **(Places)** What is the name of the magical pet shop where Hermione buys Crookshanks?
    A) Eeylops Owl Emporium  B) Flourish and Blotts  C) Magical Menagerie  D) Quality Quidditch Supplies

25. **(Magic)** What spell does Sirius Black use to disarm Harry and Hermione in the Shrieking Shack?
    A) Alohomora  B) Expelliarmus  C) Lumos  D) Riddikulus

26. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Professor Trelawney's prediction, what is Lavender Brown supposed to dread happening on "the sixteenth of October"?

27. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many four-poster beds are in the Gryffindor boys' dormitory?

28. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Besides a scarlet sweater, name two of the food items Mrs. Weasley sends Harry for Christmas.
