# Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*20 questions. For die-hard re-readers only — exact numbers, names and lines.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** What creature does Hermione discover Rita Skeeter secretly transforms into, to spy on Hogwarts undetected?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Which Hogwarts student is mentioned as having tried to curse her own acne off?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** According to Sirius, what was Bertha Jorkins's real defining trait at school, despite her reputation for being forgetful?

4. **(Places) (Open)** What is the exact time the Portkey to the Quidditch World Cup activates on Stoatshead Hill?

5. **(Places) (Open)** What false, extravagant insurance claim does Mundungus Fletcher make for his lost tent after the World Cup riot?

6. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Percy's report, what percentage of their thickness do cauldron bottoms leak per year?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** What mistake do the two Splinched wizards make when trying to pay a Muggle for their trouble?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** How much does Dobby earn per week working at Hogwarts, and what did he turn down from Dumbledore?

9. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the exact wood, core, and length of Cedric Diggory's wand, per the Weighing of the Wands?

10. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the exact wood, core, and length of Fleur Delacour's wand?

11. **(Magic) (Open)** How many drops of Veritaserum does Dumbledore use to question Barty Crouch Jr.?

12. **(Quidditch) (Open)** By what score does England lose to Transylvania in a World Cup qualifying match, as recalled at the Burrow?

13. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Put these Quidditch World Cup final running score lines in the order they occurred: 100–10, 10–0, 130–10, 30–0.

14. **(Creatures) (Open)** Roughly how many Blast-Ended Skrewts hatch initially in Hagrid's crate, and about how big is each one at hatching?

15. **(Creatures) (Open)** At what range can Charlie say a Hungarian Horntail has been seen shooting fire, versus the standard range?

16. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many hands does the Weasleys' grandfather clock have, and what do they show instead of numerals?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many locks does Mad-Eye Moody's (really Crouch Jr.'s) magical trunk have?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Sirius, about how long after being sent to Azkaban did Barty Crouch Jr. reportedly die (before being revealed alive)?

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What homemade treat does Hagrid send Harry via owl post during the summer holidays?

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What sugary snack is an overweight, dieting Dudley caught secretly smuggling into his room?

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 4 (9/10)

1. A beetle
2. Eloise Midgen
3. An excellent memory for gossip (she was "a bit dim," not forgetful)
4. Seven past five
5. A twelve-bedroomed tent with an en-suite Jacuzzi
6. Almost three percent a year
7. They try to pay with giant/oversized gold coins
8. 1 Galleon a week (plus one day off a month) — he turned down Dumbledore's offer of 10 Galleons a week and weekends off
9. Ash, unicorn tail hair, 12¼ inches
10. Rosewood, veela hair (from her grandmother), 9½ inches
11. Three drops
12. 390–10
13. 10–0, then 30–0, then 100–10, then 130–10
14. About 100 Skrewts, roughly 6 inches each
15. Standard range twenty feet; this Horntail has been seen doing forty
16. Nine hands, showing things like "home," "school," "lost," and "mortal peril" instead of numerals
17. Seven
18. About a year
19. Rock cakes
20. Doughnuts

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** How old is Harry at the start of the book? → **14**
