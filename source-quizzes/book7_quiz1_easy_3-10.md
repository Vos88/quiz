# Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*28 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Plot)** What magical object does Harry use throughout the book to move around completely unseen?
   A) The Marauder's Map  B) A Disillusionment Charm  C) The Invisibility Cloak  D) Felix Felicis

2. **(Characters)** Who becomes the new Headmaster of Hogwarts after Dumbledore's death?
   A) Severus Snape  B) Minerva McGonagall  C) Horace Slughorn  D) Filius Flitwick

3. **(Plot)** What portable shelter do Harry, Ron and Hermione sleep in for much of their journey?
   A) A caravan  B) A houseboat  C) A cave  D) A tent

4. **(Magic)** What potion allows a witch or wizard to temporarily transform into someone else, using a piece of that person such as a hair?
   A) Felix Felicis  B) Polyjuice Potion  C) Veritaserum  D) Amortentia

5. **(Quidditch)** Which professional Quidditch team does Ron support, as shown by the posters in his bedroom?
   A) Puddlemere United  B) The Wimbourne Wasps  C) The Holyhead Harpies  D) The Chudley Cannons

6. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Voldemort's giant pet snake?
   A) Nagini  B) Norberta  C) Fang  D) Fluffy

7. **(Numbers) (Open)** How old does Harry turn at the start of the book, the age at which the restriction on underage magic finally lifts?

8. **(Plot)** What everyday item does Hermione enchant with an Undetectable Extension Charm to secretly carry camping supplies, books and clothes?
   A) A rucksack  B) A school trunk  C) A hatbox  D) A beaded handbag

9. **(Deaths)** Which house-elf gives his life to rescue Harry and his friends from Malfoy Manor?
    A) Dobby  B) Winky  C) Hokey  D) Kreacher

10. **(Food/Sweets)** Which Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes sweet makes a person's nose bleed violently, allowing Harry to collect hair for Polyjuice Potion?
    A) Puking Pastilles  B) Canary Creams  C) Nosebleed Nougat  D) Fever Fudge

11. **(Characters)** Which of Harry's friends is accidentally "Splinched," losing a chunk of flesh during a botched escape?
    A) Ron  B) Neville  C) Dean  D) Seamus

12. **(Places)** What is the name of Bill and Fleur's seaside cottage, where the trio takes refuge more than once?
    A) The Burrow  B) Shell Cottage  C) Spinner's End  D) Grimmauld Place

13. **(Magic)** What is the name of the curse placed on Voldemort's own name, which instantly betrays the location of anyone who says it aloud?
    A) The Fidelius Charm  B) The Cruciatus Curse  C) The Taboo  D) The Imperius Curse

14. **(Plot) (Open)** According to wizarding legend, what are the three magical objects known collectively as the "Deathly Hallows"?

15. **(Numbers)** Not counting Harry himself, how many Horcruxes did Voldemort deliberately create to split his soul?
    A) Seven  B) Six  C) Four  D) Five

16. **(Characters)** Who is named temporary Minister for Magic at the very end of the book?
    A) Pius Thicknesse  B) Arthur Weasley  C) Kingsley Shacklebolt  D) Rufus Scrimgeour

17. **(Quidditch)** Which famous international Quidditch Seeker attends Bill and Fleur's wedding as a guest of the Delacour family?
    A) Cedric Diggory  B) Viktor Krum  C) Oliver Wood  D) Cho Chang

18. **(Creatures)** What enormous creature guards the deepest vaults at Gringotts, and which the trio eventually rides to escape the bank?
    A) A dragon  B) A Hippogriff  C) A Thestral  D) A Basilisk

19. **(Plot)** Which Hogwarts professor, believed for most of the book to be a traitor and a murderer, is revealed after his death to have been secretly loyal to Dumbledore all along?
    A) Horace Slughorn  B) Argus Filch  C) Filius Flitwick  D) Severus Snape

20. **(Places)** In which village, where his parents are buried, do Harry and Hermione visit on Christmas Eve?
    A) Upper Flagley  B) Hogsmeade  C) Godric's Hollow  D) Ottery St Catchpole

21. **(Characters)** What is the name of Harry and Ginny's eldest son, glimpsed in the epilogue boarding the Hogwarts Express?
    A) James Sirius Potter  B) Albus Severus Potter  C) Scorpius Malfoy  D) Teddy Lupin

22. **(Places)** What hidden room at Hogwarts grows into a sprawling refuge, sheltering students hiding from the Carrows?
    A) The Chamber of Secrets  B) The Shrieking Shack  C) The Hog's Head  D) The Room of Requirement

23. **(Magic)** What ancient, goblin-made sword — capable of destroying Horcruxes — do Harry's friends wield more than once during the hunt?
    A) The Elder Wand  B) The sword of Gryffindor  C) A Basilisk fang  D) Excalibur

24. **(Magic)** What spell does Harry repeatedly use to disarm his opponents, rather than more lethal curses?
    A) Stupefy  B) Avada Kedavra  C) Sectumsempra  D) Expelliarmus

25. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What inscription appears on the Golden Snitch Dumbledore leaves Harry in his will, only once Harry is ready to see it?

26. **(Creatures)** What is the name of Hagrid's giant half-brother, who helps fight during the Battle of Hogwarts?
    A) Grawp  B) Fang  C) Buckbeak  D) Norberta

27. **(Numbers)** Counting the real Harry, how many "Potters" fly from Privet Drive using Polyjuice Potion as decoys?
    A) Five  B) Seven  C) Six  D) Eight

28. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of Fred and George's joke shop, whose products the trio relies on more than once during their adventures?
    A) Zonko's Joke Shop  B) Flourish and Blotts  C) Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes  D) Honeydukes
