# Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*24 questions. For die-hard re-readers only — exact numbers, names and lines.*

1. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What unlucky Bertie Bott's Every Flavour Bean flavour does Dumbledore get at Harry's bedside near the end of the book?

2. **(Magic) (Open)** The inscription on the Mirror of Erised, written backwards, reads "Erised stra ehru oyt ube cafru oyt on wohsi." What does this say when read correctly?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the full name of the ghost known to Gryffindors as "Nearly Headless Nick"?

4. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many staircases does Hogwarts castle have, according to the book?

5. **(Numbers) (Open)** What is Slytherin's house point total at the start of the end-of-year feast, before Dumbledore's last-minute awards?

6. **(Creatures) (Open)** What crucial piece of information about Fluffy does Hagrid accidentally let slip to the hooded stranger who sold him the dragon egg?

7. **(Magic) (Open)** Mr Ollivander says the phoenix whose tail feather sits in Harry's wand gave only one other feather. Whose wand is it in?

8. **(Creatures) (Open)** How much does a single unicorn horn cost at the apothecary in Diagon Alley?

9. **(Creatures) (Open)** How much does dragon liver cost per ounce, according to a complaint Harry overhears in the apothecary?

10. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Exactly how much (in Sickles and Knuts) does Harry spend on sweets from the trolley on the Hogwarts Express?

11. **(Plot) (Open)** On what date does the Daily Prophet report the Gringotts break-in, and why is that date significant?

12. **(Characters) (Open)** What nickname does Mrs Weasley reportedly use for the twins Fred and George, treating them as a single unit?

13. **(Quidditch) (Open)** According to the Quidditch trivia Harry reads, how many ways are there to commit a foul, and in what year's World Cup match did all of them occur at once?

14. **(Numbers) (Open)** In what year, according to Hagrid, was dragon-breeding outlawed in Britain?

15. **(Plot) (Open)** What two items did the Dursleys give Harry for his birthday the year before he started at Hogwarts?

16. **(Plot) (Open)** What single item do the Dursleys give Harry for Christmas, and how is it presented?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** List the exact point totals Dumbledore awards to Ron, Hermione, Harry and Neville at the end-of-year feast.

18. **(Places) (Open)** According to Mr Ollivander, since what year has his shop been making wands?

19. **(Magic) (Open)** What are James's and Lily's wands made of (wood and length each)?

20. **(Plot) (Open)** What does Hagrid keep hidden under a tea cosy on his table, which Harry spots during a visit?

21. **(Quidditch) (Open)** According to the Quidditch trivia Harry reads, in which desert does a vanished referee turn up months later?

22. **(Places) (Open)** According to Griphook, roughly how often are the vaults at Gringotts checked?

23. **(Characters) (Open)** What phrase does Percy Weasley keep repeating as he proudly shepherds students away from the troll on Hallowe'en?

24. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What "rations" does Uncle Vernon bring to the shack on the rock, on the night before Harry's eleventh birthday?

---

## Answer Key — Quiz 4 (9/10)

1. Earwax
2. "I show not your face but your heart's desire"
3. Sir Nicholas de Mimsy-Porpington
4. 142 (a hundred and forty-two)
5. 472 points
6. That playing music sends Fluffy to sleep
7. Lord Voldemort's wand (yew, 13½ inches)
8. 21 Galleons
9. Sixteen Sickles an ounce
10. 11 Sickles and 7 Knuts
11. 31 July — Harry's eleventh birthday, the same day Hagrid emptied vault 713
12. "Gred and Forge"
13. 700 ways to foul; all occurred in one 1473 World Cup match
14. 1709 (the Warlocks' Convention of 1709)
15. A coat-hanger and a pair of Uncle Vernon's old socks
16. A 50-pence piece, taped to a note
17. Ron +50, Hermione +50, Harry +60, Neville +10
18. 382 BC
19. James: mahogany, 11 inches, pliable. Lily: willow, 10¼ inches, swishy
20. The Daily Prophet clipping about the Gringotts vault break-in
21. The Sahara Desert
22. About once every ten years
23. "I'm a Prefect"
24. A packet of crisps each and four bananas

**Tiebreaker (exact number):** How many times does Dumbledore click the Put-Outer to remove all the light from Privet Drive's street lamps? → **12**
