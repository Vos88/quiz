# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*26 questions. Mostly open, with a handful of multiple choice — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers works as a Gringotts curse-breaker in Egypt?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers is in Romania, studying dragons?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** Which Hogwarts staff member is secretly revealed to be taking a remedial "Kwikspell" correspondence course, hinting that he is a Squib?

4. **(Characters)** Who is the Minister for Magic who arrives at Hagrid's cabin to arrest him "as a precaution"?
   A) Rufus Scrimgeour  B) Barty Crouch  C) Pius Thicknesse  D) Cornelius Fudge

5. **(Places) (Open)** In which village is The Burrow, the Weasleys' home, located?

6. **(Places) (Open)** According to Draco, where in Malfoy Manor do the Malfoys keep their own hidden Dark artefacts?

7. **(Places) (Open)** What password, overheard while disguised as Crabbe and Goyle, gets Harry and Ron into the Slytherin common room?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** What password lets Harry and Ron back into Gryffindor Tower on the night they arrive at Hogwarts by flying car?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** Put these three events in the order they happen in the book: (a) Dobby bewitches a Bludger to target Harry during a Quidditch match; (b) Harry and Ron, disguised as Crabbe and Goyle, question Malfoy in the Slytherin common room; (c) Ginny Weasley is taken into the Chamber of Secrets.

10. **(Plot) (Open)** How does Hermione create the diversion that lets her steal Boomslang skin and Bicorn horn from Snape's private stores?

11. **(Plot) (Open)** What cryptic piece of advice does Hagrid give Harry and Ron as he's being arrested, hinting at how to find the truth about the Chamber?

12. **(Magic)** What spell does Hermione unsuccessfully try, to reveal hidden writing in Tom Riddle's diary?
    A) Aparecium  B) Specialis Revelio  C) Revelio  D) Finite Incantatem

13. **(Magic) (Open)** What ineffective spell does Lockhart shout at the escaped Cornish pixies, to no effect?

14. **(Magic) (Open)** Name the two hardest-to-obtain Polyjuice Potion ingredients, taken from Snape's private stores rather than the student cupboard.

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What was the score at one point during the Gryffindor–Slytherin match, before Harry caught the Snitch despite his broken arm?

16. **(Quidditch)** What position in the league do the Chudley Cannons, Ron's favourite team, hold?
    A) First  B) Third  C) Ninth  D) Last

17. **(Quidditch)** According to Colin Creevey, Harry is the youngest person to play Quidditch for his house in how many years?
    A) Fifty years  B) A hundred years  C) Two hundred years  D) Thirty years

18. **(Creatures) (Open)** According to Hermione's note, what is the only thing that can kill a Basilisk?

19. **(Creatures) (Open)** Exactly how long is the giant shed snake skin that Harry, Ron and Lockhart find in the tunnel to the Chamber?

20. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Aragog's spider wife?

21. **(Numbers) (Open)** Exactly how many bones does Madam Pomfrey have to regrow overnight in Harry's arm using Skele-Gro?

22. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Snape, how many Muggles witnessed the flying Ford Anglia?

23. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many school governors sign the Order of Suspension that temporarily removes Dumbledore as headmaster?

24. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is Aunt Petunia's dessert — destroyed by Dobby at the Masons' dinner party — made of?

25. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** According to Mrs Weasley's generous breakfast, how many sausages does she give Harry at the Burrow?

26. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What snack do Harry and Ron rely on, found in the glove compartment, during their long flight to Hogwarts?
