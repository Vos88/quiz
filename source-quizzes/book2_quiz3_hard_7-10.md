# Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*20 questions. Mostly open questions — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers works as a Gringotts curse-breaker in Egypt?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** Which of Ron's older brothers is in Romania, studying dragons?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** Which Hogwarts staff member is secretly revealed to be taking a remedial "Kwikspell" correspondence course, hinting that he is a Squib?

4. **(Places) (Open)** In which village is The Burrow, the Weasleys' home, located?

5. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the Knockturn Alley shop where Harry overhears Lucius Malfoy selling Dark artefacts?

6. **(Plot) (Open)** What password lets Harry and Ron back into Gryffindor Tower on the night they arrive at Hogwarts by flying car?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** What password, overheard while disguised as Crabbe and Goyle, gets Harry and Ron into the Slytherin common room?

8. **(Plot) (Open)** Put these three events in the order they happen in the book: (a) Dobby bewitches a Bludger to target Harry during a Quidditch match; (b) Hermione is Petrified alongside a Ravenclaw prefect; (c) Ginny is taken into the Chamber of Secrets.

9. **(Magic) (Open)** Name the two hardest-to-obtain Polyjuice Potion ingredients, taken from Snape's private stores rather than the student store cupboard.

10. **(Magic)** What spell does Hermione unsuccessfully try, to reveal hidden writing in Tom Riddle's diary?
    A) Aparecium  B) Specialis Revelio  C) Revelio  D) Finite Incantatem

11. **(Magic) (Open)** What ineffective spell does Lockhart shout at the escaped Cornish pixies, to no effect?

12. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What was the score at one point during the Gryffindor–Slytherin match, before Harry caught the Snitch despite his broken arm?

13. **(Quidditch)** What brand-new broomstick model does Lucius Malfoy buy for the entire Slytherin Quidditch team?
    A) Nimbus Two Thousand and One  B) Cleansweep Five  C) Comet Two Sixty  D) Silver Arrow

14. **(Creatures) (Open)** What restorative plant must mature before Madam Pomfrey can revive the Petrified victims?

15. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of the ancient, blind giant spider in the Forbidden Forest who turns out to be innocent of the original Chamber attacks?

16. **(Numbers) (Open)** Exactly how many bones does Madam Pomfrey have to regrow overnight in Harry's arm using Skele-Gro?

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to Snape, how many Muggles witnessed the flying Ford Anglia?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** Exactly how long is the giant shed snake skin that Harry, Ron and Lockhart find in the tunnel to the Chamber?

19. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is Aunt Petunia's dessert — destroyed by Dobby at the Masons' dinner party — made of?

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Besides a hand-knitted jumper, what food item does Mrs Weasley send Harry for Christmas?

2. Charlie Weasley
3. Argus Filch
4. Ottery St Catchpole
5. Borgin and Burkes
6. "Wattlebird"
7. "Pure-blood"
8. a, b, c
9. Powdered Bicorn horn and shredded Boomslang skin
10. A) Aparecium
11. "Peskipiksi Pesternomi"
12. Slytherin led, sixty points to zero (60–0)
13. A) Nimbus Two Thousand and One
14. Mandrake (the Mandrake Restorative Draught)
15. Aragog
16. Thirty-three (33)
17. Six or seven
18. Twenty feet
19. A mound of whipped cream and sugared violets
20. A (large) plum cake
