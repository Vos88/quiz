# Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*30 questions, an even mix of multiple choice and open questions (medium tier = ~50% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Magic)** What is the name of the Killing Curse?
   A) Crucio  B) Avada Kedavra  C) Imperio  D) Sectumsempra

2. **(Characters)** Which Death Eater curses off George Weasley's ear with Sectumsempra during the escape from Privet Drive?
   A) Bellatrix Lestrange  B) Fenrir Greyback  C) Severus Snape  D) Antonin Dolohov

3. **(Places)** In which forest, somewhere Hermione once camped with her parents, does a mysterious silver doe lead Harry to the sword of Gryffindor?
   A) The Forbidden Forest  B) The Forest of Dean  C) The woods near the old Quidditch World Cup campsite  D) A forest in Albania

4. **(Plot) (Open)** What password, confirmed only after weeks of guessing, finally lets Ron tune in to the underground resistance radio show "Potterwatch"?

5. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons is the bounty offered for capturing Harry Potter, together with his wand?

6. **(Deaths)** Which veteran Auror and Order of the Phoenix member, famous for his magical eye, is killed during the escape from Privet Drive?
   A) Mad-Eye Moody  B) Kingsley Shacklebolt  C) Remus Lupin  D) Arthur Weasley

7. **(Characters)** Which house-elf, once loyal to the Black family, finally reveals the full history of the stolen locket Horcrux?
   A) Dobby  B) Kreacher  C) Winky  D) Hokey

8. **(Magic) (Open)** What Charm does Hermione successfully cast for the first time under pressure in the Ministry courtroom, producing a silver otter?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** After the Gringotts break-in, Harry's vision of Voldemort's fury reveals that one remaining Horcrux is hidden somewhere unexpected. At which location?

10. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What unusual, unpleasant purple drink does Xenophilius Lovegood serve the trio at his house?

11. **(Characters)** Who turns out to be Harry's mysterious rescuer at the Hog's Head, and is revealed as Albus Dumbledore's estranged brother?
    A) Elphias Doge  B) Mundungus Fletcher  C) Remus Lupin  D) Aberforth Dumbledore

12. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name and number of the London townhouse the trio hides in after escaping the Ministry?

13. **(Plot)** What does Dumbledore leave to Hermione in his will, written entirely in runes?
    A) The Deluminator  B) The Tales of Beedle the Bard  C) The Golden Snitch  D) A Potions textbook

14. **(Numbers) (Open)** What are the exact wood, core and length of Draco Malfoy's wand, which Harry wins by disarming him at Malfoy Manor?

15. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which of Voldemort's creatures is ordered to kill Severus Snape in the Shrieking Shack?

16. **(Magic)** What is the name of the magical waterfall at Gringotts, a security measure that strips away all concealment charms and disguises?
    A) Homenum Revelio  B) Finite Incantatem  C) Priori Incantatem  D) The Thief's Downfall

17. **(Creatures) (Open)** Which goblin, hired to help the trio break into Gringotts, ultimately betrays them and steals back the sword of Gryffindor?

18. **(Characters)** Which Weasley brother returns to the family during the Battle of Hogwarts, reconciling after years of estrangement over his Ministry career?
    A) Percy  B) Charlie  C) Bill  D) George

19. **(Quidditch)** Which of these groups of former Gryffindor Quidditch players arrive at the Room of Requirement to help defend Hogwarts?
    A) Cho Chang, Cedric Diggory, and Viktor Krum  B) Fred, George, and Lee Jordan  C) Oliver Wood, Katie Bell, and Angelina Johnson  D) Dean Thomas, Seamus Finnigan, and Neville Longbottom

20. **(Numbers) (Open)** Counting Harry himself, how many pieces was Voldemort's soul ultimately split into?

21. **(Places)** Which heavily guarded government building do Harry, Ron and Hermione infiltrate using Polyjuice Potion, to retrieve the locket Horcrux from Dolores Umbridge?
    A) Gringotts  B) Hogwarts  C) Azkaban  D) The Ministry of Magic

22. **(Plot)** What false identity, using Polyjuice Potion, does Ron adopt during the Ministry infiltration?
    A) Vernon Dudley  B) Dragomir Despard  C) Reg Cattermole  D) Stan Shunpike

23. **(Plot) (Open)** What does the Resurrection Stone, hidden inside the Golden Snitch, allow Harry to do just before he walks into the Forbidden Forest to face Voldemort?

24. **(Plot)** What weapon does Neville use to behead Nagini during the final battle, having pulled it from the flaming Sorting Hat?
    A) The sword of Gryffindor  B) A Basilisk fang  C) The Elder Wand  D) Hagrid's crossbow

25. **(Magic) (Open)** What form does Harry's own Patronus take, mirroring his father's Animagus form?

26. **(Magic)** Which Unforgivable Curse does Harry cast for the first time, on the goblin Bogrod and the Death Eater Travers, during the Gringotts break-in?
    A) The Cruciatus Curse  B) The Killing Curse  C) The Imperius Curse  D) Legilimency

27. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Which former Hogwarts Quidditch commentator hosts the underground radio show "Potterwatch" under the codename "River"?

28. **(Creatures)** What creature, sent by Voldemort, is found concealed inside the corpse of Bathilda Bagshot at Godric's Hollow?
    A) Nagini  B) A Boggart  C) An Inferius  D) A Dementor

29. **(Creatures) (Open)** What creatures swarm and apparently carry off Hagrid during the chaos of the Battle of Hogwarts?

30. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Which house-elf's cooking and mood improve dramatically after Harry gives him Regulus's old locket as a keepsake?
