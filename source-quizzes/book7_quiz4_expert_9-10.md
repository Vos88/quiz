# Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*20 questions, entirely open-answer — expert tier, plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Magic) (Open)** What is the exact length of Voldemort's own wand (yew, with a phoenix feather core)?

2. **(Plot) (Open)** Exactly where had Dolores Umbridge kept Mad-Eye Moody's stolen magical eye?

3. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the village where the Dumbledore family lived before moving to Godric's Hollow?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What mocking nickname does Rita Skeeter give Elphias Doge in her biography of Dumbledore?

5. **(Numbers) (Open)** At what age was Ariana Dumbledore attacked by the three Muggle boys, triggering her lifelong magical instability?

6. **(Quidditch) (Open)** What position did Oliver Wood, who arrives to help defend Hogwarts, once play for the Gryffindor Quidditch team?

7. **(Characters) (Open)** Which elderly, famously blunt wedding guest owns the goblin-made tiara lent to Fleur for her wedding?

8. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which underage Hogwarts student is confirmed killed despite being told to evacuate, his body later carried in by Neville Longbottom and Oliver Wood?

9. **(Plot) (Open)** What false Muggle names does Hermione give her Memory-Charmed parents, to protect them by sending them to Australia?

10. **(Magic) (Open)** What are the exact wood, core, and length of the wand Ollivander reveals he made for Peter Pettigrew, later used by Ron?

11. **(Creatures) (Open)** Xenophilius Lovegood's mounted Erumpent horn is mistaken by him for the horn of which fictional creature?

12. **(Characters) (Open)** What is the full name of Harry and Ginny Potter's youngest son, born nineteen years after the Battle of Hogwarts?

13. **(Plot) (Open)** What is the exact title of the chapter, within Rita Skeeter's biography, detailing young Dumbledore's friendship with Gellert Grindelwald?

14. **(Magic) (Open)** What name is given to the bag of jingling metal tools Griphook uses to drive back the blind security dragon at Gringotts?

15. **(Numbers) (Open)** Roughly how long was Garrick Ollivander held captive at Malfoy Manor before his rescue?

16. **(Plot) (Open)** Which member of the student resistance is severely tortured by the Carrows for freeing a chained first-year, escalating the crackdown at Hogwarts?

17. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** Including the extra glass held by Bill, how many people in total drink the Firewhisky toast to Mad-Eye Moody at the Burrow?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** According to his Daily Prophet obituary, how many uses of dragon's blood did Albus Dumbledore discover?

19. **(Creatures) (Open)** What magical condition does Fenrir Greyback have, which marks him as a feared Death Eater operative despite his lacking the Dark Mark?

20. **(Plot) (Open)** Match each Deathly Hallow — the Elder Wand, the Resurrection Stone, and the Cloak of Invisibility — to the Peverell brother who first owned it: Antioch, Cadmus, and Ignotus.
