# Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince — Pub Quiz 4 (Difficulty 9/10)

*26 questions, entirely open-answer — the expert round for true Half-Blood Prince devotees.*

1. **(Characters) (Open)** To whom did the Muggle Prime Minister give the gerbil that Fudge's teacup had been magically turned into?

2. **(Characters) (Open)** What age is Stan Shunpike reported to be in the Daily Prophet article about his arrest?

3. **(Characters) (Open)** Whose mother has had seven husbands, each dying mysteriously and leaving her gold?

4. **(Characters) (Open)** Name the two Death Eater siblings who storm the Astronomy Tower during Dumbledore's murder.

5. **(Plot) (Open)** What flower does Dumbledore compliment Vernon Dursley on, while waiting to collect Harry at the start of the book?

6. **(Places) (Open)** In which exact train compartment does Slughorn hold his first "Slug Club" lunch?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** According to the Prime Minister's recollection, how old was the Brockdale Bridge when it collapsed?

8. **(Deaths) (Open)** How many days passed between Voldemort's visit to admire her treasures and Hepzibah Smith's death by poisoning?

9. **(Numbers) (Open)** What range of numbers were marked on the old disciplinary record boxes Snape assigned Harry to sort through during detention?

10. **(Magic) (Open)** How many ingredients, including a strand of her own hair, did Hermione's prize-winning Golpalott's Third Law antidote contain?

11. **(Magic) (Open)** At what two ages does Slughorn say he personally took Felix Felicis before this year?

12. **(Magic) (Open)** According to its publication date, roughly how old is the Half-Blood Prince's old Potions textbook?

13. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many goals did Ginny Weasley score in Chaser tryouts for the Gryffindor team?

14. **(Quidditch) (Open)** By more than how many points would Gryffindor have needed to win their final match to take the Quidditch Championship outright?

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** By what score was Gryffindor leading Slytherin at the half-hour mark of the season-opening match?

16. **(Creatures) (Open)** How many winged palominos pulled Madame Maxime's carriage to Hogwarts?

17. **(Creatures) (Open)** What kind of pet does Morfin Gaunt speak Parseltongue to during Ogden's visit to the Gaunt shack?

18. **(Characters) (Open)** How many years of intelligence on Dumbledore does Snape admit to giving Voldemort upon his return?

19. **(Plot) (Open)** How many hours did Snape deliberately wait, on Dumbledore's orders, before returning to Voldemort's side after the Dark Mark burned?

20. **(Plot) (Open)** For how many years, according to Dumbledore, did Merope Gaunt endure her abusive father before his and Morfin's imprisonment freed her?

21. **(Places) (Open)** Which pub does Dumbledore claim he and Harry are heading to, as a cover story, right before they Apparate to the cave?

22. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What exact price per pint does Slughorn say fresh acromantula venom is worth?

23. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What trolley sweet does Slughorn dislike, citing his digestion, preferring to pack his own lunch on the train?

24. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** According to Slughorn's drunken toast at Aragog's wake, how much does a single unicorn tail hair sell for?

25. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons and Sickles did Ron still owe Fred for joke-shop merchandise, mentioned during the Diagon Alley trip?

26. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which Order of the Phoenix member was murdered "nearby," around the same time as Amelia Bones, early in the book?
