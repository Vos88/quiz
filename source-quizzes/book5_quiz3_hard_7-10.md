# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*20 questions, mostly open — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Numbers) (Open)** At what exact time does Harry successfully cast the Patronus that gets him summoned to a Ministry hearing?

2. **(Plot) (Open)** To what time, and to which courtroom, is Harry's Ministry hearing abruptly changed at the last minute?

3. **(Numbers)** What is the Ministry's bounty on Sirius Black's head?
   A) 1,000 Galleons  B) 5,000 Galleons  C) 10,000 Galleons  D) 50,000 Galleons

4. **(Characters) (Open)** What two specifications of Harry's wand are confirmed when Ministry security scans it on his way to the hearing?

5. **(Magic)** What charm does Harry use to block Bellatrix Lestrange's attempt to Summon the prophecy straight out of his hands?
   A) Impedimenta  B) Protego  C) Finite Incantatem  D) Expelliarmus

6. **(Magic) (Open)** What everlasting magical flame does Hagrid present as a diplomatic gift to Karkus, the giants' Gurg?

7. **(Plot) (Open)** What potion does Harry ruin in his very first Potions lesson of the year by forgetting to add an ingredient?

8. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons of his Triwizard Tournament winnings does Harry secretly give Fred and George to fund their joke shop?

9. **(Characters) (Open)** How many O.W.L.s did Fred and George Weasley each earn?

10. **(Deaths) (Open)** Name the two of Molly Weasley's brothers who were killed in the First War, overwhelmed only after it took five Death Eaters to bring them down.

11. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many high-security prisoners escape from Azkaban in the mass breakout reported that February?

12. **(Plot) (Open)** What exact row number in the Hall of Prophecy holds the shelf containing Harry's prophecy?

13. **(Numbers) (Open)** How much does Hermione pay for a new quill at Scrivenshaft's?

14. **(Quidditch)**
    What is Slytherin's score at the exact moment Harry catches the Golden Snitch in the Gryffindor–Slytherin match?
    A) 10–0  B) 30–0  C) 50–10  D) 60–20

15. **(Magic)** What potion does Umbridge demand from Snape to interrogate Harry, which he claims to have none of?
    A) Felix Felicis  B) the Draught of Living Death  C) Polyjuice Potion  D) Veritaserum

16. **(Characters) (Open)** How many years has Snape been teaching at Hogwarts, according to Umbridge's inspection of him?

17. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the fake Muggle department store that conceals the entrance to St. Mungo's Hospital?

18. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons' reward does the Ministry offer for information leading to each of the ten escaped Death Eaters?

19. **(Plot) (Open)** Put these four events in the order they occur in the book: Hagrid returns to Hogwarts from his mission to the giants; Dumbledore escapes rather than be arrested; the D.A.'s first meeting is held in the Room of Requirement; Percy's estranged letter urging Ron to drop Harry arrives.

20. **(Deaths) (Open)** What color is the curse that strikes Sirius Black in the chest and sends him through the Veil?

8. 1,000 Galleons

10. Gideon and Fabian Prewett
11. Ten
12. Row ninety-seven
13. 15 Sickles and 2 Knuts
14. A) 10–0
15. D) Veritaserum
