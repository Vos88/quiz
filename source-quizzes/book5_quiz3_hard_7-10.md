# Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix — Pub Quiz 3 (Difficulty 7/10)

*26 questions, mostly open — this round rewards close readers.*

1. **(Numbers)** At what exact time does Harry successfully cast the Patronus that gets him summoned to a Ministry hearing?
   A) Eight fifteen p.m.  B) Ten past ten p.m.  C) Nine twenty-three p.m.  D) Eleven o'clock at night

2. **(Characters) (Open)** What two specifications of Harry's wand are confirmed when Ministry security scans it on his way to the hearing?

3. **(Places) (Open)** What exact row number in the Hall of Prophecy holds the shelf containing Harry's prophecy?

4. **(Plot) (Open)** What two sets of initials appear on the handwritten label beneath Harry's prophecy orb?

5. **(Magic) (Open)** What everlasting magical flame does Hagrid present as a diplomatic gift to Karkus, the giants' Gurg?

6. **(Quidditch)**
   What is Slytherin's score at the exact moment Harry catches the Golden Snitch in the Gryffindor–Slytherin match?
   A) 30–0  B) 10–0  C) 50–10  D) 60–20

7. **(Creatures) (Open)** Roughly how many giants remain in total when Hagrid and Madame Maxime find the giants' camp?

8. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons' reward does the Ministry offer for information leading to each of the ten escaped Death Eaters?

9. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What alcoholic drink does Ron express curiosity about ordering at the Hog's Head, earning Hermione's scolding since he's a prefect?

10. **(Deaths) (Open)** What color is the curse that strikes Sirius Black in the chest and sends him through the Veil?

11. **(Characters) (Open)** How many years has Snape been teaching at Hogwarts, according to Umbridge's inspection of him?

12. **(Places) (Open)** What is the name of the St. Mungo's ward reserved for permanent, long-term spell damage, where the Longbottoms live?

13. **(Plot) (Open)** According to Dumbledore, which two boys — both born at the end of July to parents who had thrice defied Voldemort — could the prophecy have applied to?

14. **(Magic) (Open)** What potion does Harry ruin in his very first Potions lesson of the year by forgetting to add an ingredient?

15. **(Quidditch) (Open)** How many eyewitnesses reported seeing Slytherin Keeper Miles Bletchley hex Alicia Spinnet in the library — a claim Snape chose to ignore?

16. **(Creatures) (Open)** Name the giants' Gurg (chief) who is beheaded in a civil war the night after receiving Hagrid's gift of fire.

17. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many years before the events of this book does Dumbledore say he first guessed that Harry's scar signified a connection to Voldemort?

18. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What raw ingredient does Hagrid press to his swollen eye as a folk remedy after his return from the giants?

19. **(Deaths) (Open)** According to Bellatrix Lestrange, what must a caster genuinely feel for an Unforgivable Curse like the Cruciatus Curse to work properly?

20. **(Characters)** Who is the Healer-in-Charge of the ward treating Arthur Weasley at St. Mungo's?
    A) Hippocrates Smethwyck  B) Augustus Pye  C) Miriam Strout  D) Dilys Derwent

21. **(Places) (Open)** In which part of the Hog's Head Inn does Sybill Trelawney deliver the original prophecy, during her job interview?

22. **(Plot) (Open)** What single word do purple pustules spell out across Marietta Edgecombe's face after she betrays the D.A.?

23. **(Magic)** What potion does Umbridge demand from Snape to interrogate Harry, which he claims (falsely) to have none of?
    A) Polyjuice Potion  B) Felix Felicis  C) the Draught of Living Death  D) Veritaserum

24. **(Quidditch) (Open)** In exactly how many minutes does the disastrous Gryffindor vs. Hufflepuff match end?

25. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many N.E.W.T.s at Exceeds Expectations or higher does Auror training require at minimum, according to Professor McGonagall?

26. **(Deaths) (Open)** According to Mad-Eye Moody's old Order of the Phoenix photograph, which First War member was killed personally by Voldemort himself, rather than by other Death Eaters?

7. About seventy to eighty
8. 1,000 Galleons
9. Firewhisky
10. Red (a jet of red light)
11. Fourteen years
12. The Janus Thickey Ward
13. Harry Potter and Neville Longbottom
14. The Draught of Peace
15. Fourteen
16. Karkus
17. Fifteen years
18. A slab of raw dragon meat (steak)
19. A real desire to cause pain — genuine enjoyment of it
20. A) Hippocrates Smethwyck
21. In a room above the bar
22. SNEAK
23. D) Veritaserum
