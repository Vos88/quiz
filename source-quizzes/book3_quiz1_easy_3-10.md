# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*20 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is the dangerous escaped Azkaban prisoner much of the wizarding world believes is hunting Harry?
   A) Sirius Black  B) Peter Pettigrew  C) Lucius Malfoy  D) Igor Karkaroff

2. **(Places)** What is the name of the all-wizarding village near Hogwarts that third-years may visit at weekends?
   A) Hogsmeade  B) Godric's Hollow  C) Diagon Alley  D) Little Whinging

3. **(Creatures)** What are the hooded, soul-sucking guards stationed around Hogwarts this year?
   A) Dementors  B) Boggarts  C) Death Eaters  D) Inferi

4. **(Characters)** Who is Hogwarts' new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher?
   A) Professor Lupin  B) Professor Snape  C) Professor Quirrell  D) Professor Moody

5. **(Plot)** What does Harry accidentally do to Aunt Marge after she insults his parents?
   A) Inflates her like a balloon  B) Turns her into a toad  C) Sets her hair alight  D) Shrinks her

6. **(Magic)** What is the incantation of the charm used to drive away dementors?
   A) Expecto Patronum  B) Expelliarmus  C) Riddikulus  D) Alohomora

7. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the half-eagle, half-horse creature Hagrid introduces to his class, which Harry successfully rides?
   A) Buckbeak  B) Norbert  C) Fang  D) Fawkes

8. **(Plot)** What broomstick does Harry receive anonymously for Christmas?
   A) The Firebolt  B) The Nimbus Two Thousand  C) The Cleansweep Seven  D) The Comet Two Sixty

9. **(Numbers) (Open)** How old does Harry turn at the very start of the book?

10. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the famous sweetshop in Hogsmeade?
    A) Honeydukes  B) Zonko's  C) Florean Fortescue's  D) The Three Broomsticks

11. **(Magic)** Which phrase activates the Marauder's Map?
    A) "I solemnly swear that I am up to no good"  B) "Mischief Managed"  C) "Dissendium"  D) "Alohomora"

12. **(Plot) (Open)** Ron's pet rat, Scabbers, is revealed in the climax to actually be which person?

13. **(Characters)** Which strict Potions Master clearly dislikes Harry and resents the new Defense teacher?
    A) Professor Snape  B) Professor Flitwick  C) Professor Sprout  D) Professor Kettleburn

14. **(Quidditch)** What position does Harry play on the Gryffindor Quidditch team?
    A) Seeker  B) Keeper  C) Beater  D) Chaser

15. **(Quidditch)** What is the small, golden, winged ball that Seekers chase called?
    A) The Golden Snitch  B) The Quaffle  C) A Bludger  D) A Firebolt

16. **(Places)** What is the name of the triple-decker, purple emergency bus that picks up stranded wizards?
    A) The Knight Bus  B) The Hogwarts Express  C) The Floo Express  D) The Ministry car

17. **(Plot)** Which of these events happens LAST in the book?
    A) Harry receives the Firebolt  B) Aunt Marge is inflated like a balloon  C) Buckbeak loses his appeal  D) Sirius Black is freed from the tower

18. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons does Arthur Weasley win in the Daily Prophet Grand Prize Galleon Draw?
    A) Seven hundred  B) One hundred  C) One thousand  D) Seventy

19. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the Hogsmeade pub famous for its butterbeer?
    A) The Three Broomsticks  B) The Leaky Cauldron  C) The Hog's Head  D) Honeydukes

20. **(Characters)** Who is revealed, in the book's climax, to be Harry's godfather?
    A) Sirius Black  B) Remus Lupin  C) Peter Pettigrew  D) Cornelius Fudge

---

2. A) Hogsmeade
3. A) Dementors
4. A) Professor Lupin
5. A) Inflates her like a balloon
6. A) Expecto Patronum
7. A) Buckbeak
8. A) The Firebolt
9. Thirteen
10. A) Honeydukes
11. A) "I solemnly swear that I am up to no good"
12. Peter Pettigrew
13. A) Professor Snape
14. A) Seeker
15. A) The Golden Snitch
16. A) The Knight Bus
17. D) Sirius Black is freed from the tower
18. A) Seven hundred
19. A) The Three Broomsticks
20. A) Sirius Black
