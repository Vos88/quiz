# Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban — Pub Quiz 1 (Difficulty 3/10)

*32 questions, mostly multiple choice (easy tier = ~90% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Characters)** Who is the dangerous escaped Azkaban prisoner much of the wizarding world believes is hunting Harry?
   A) Sirius Black  B) Peter Pettigrew  C) Lucius Malfoy  D) Igor Karkaroff

2. **(Numbers) (Open)** How old does Harry turn at the very start of the book?

3. **(Places)** What is the name of the street where the Dursleys live?
   A) Magnolia Crescent  B) Privet Drive  C) Diagon Alley  D) Godric's Hollow

4. **(Plot)** What does Harry accidentally do to Aunt Marge after she insults his parents?
   A) Turns her into a toad  B) Sets her hair alight  C) Shrinks her  D) Inflates her like a balloon

5. **(Places)** What is the name of the triple-decker, purple emergency bus that picks up stranded wizards?
   A) The Hogwarts Express  B) The Knight Bus  C) The Floo Express  D) The Ministry car

6. **(Numbers)** How many Galleons does Arthur Weasley win in the Daily Prophet Grand Prize Galleon Draw?
   A) Seven hundred  B) One hundred  C) One thousand  D) Seventy

7. **(Creatures)** What are the hooded, soul-sucking guards stationed around Hogwarts this year?
   A) Boggarts  B) Death Eaters  C) Dementors  D) Inferi

8. **(Characters)** Who is Hogwarts' new Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher?
   A) Professor Snape  B) Professor Lupin  C) Professor Quirrell  D) Professor Moody

9. **(Magic)** What is the incantation of the charm used to drive away dementors?
   A) Expelliarmus  B) Riddikulus  C) Expecto Patronum  D) Alohomora

10. **(Food/Sweets)** What sweet treat does Professor Lupin give Harry and the other students after a dementor boards the train?
    A) Chocolate  B) Cauldron Cakes  C) Ice Mice  D) Sherbet lemons

11. **(Creatures)** What is the name of the half-eagle, half-horse creature Hagrid introduces to his class, which Harry successfully rides?
    A) Norbert  B) Fang  C) Fawkes  D) Buckbeak

12. **(Creatures)** A hippogriff has the body and hind legs of a horse — what kind of creature makes up its front half?
    A) A lion  B) A serpent  C) An eagle  D) A wolf

13. **(Characters)** Which strict Potions Master clearly dislikes Harry and resents the new Defense teacher?
    A) Professor Flitwick  B) Professor Sprout  C) Professor Kettleburn  D) Professor Snape

14. **(Quidditch)** What position does Harry play on the Gryffindor Quidditch team?
    A) Seeker  B) Keeper  C) Beater  D) Chaser

15. **(Quidditch)** What is the small, golden, winged ball that Seekers chase called?
    A) The Quaffle  B) The Golden Snitch  C) A Bludger  D) A Firebolt

16. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the famous sweetshop in Hogsmeade?
    A) Honeydukes  B) Zonko's  C) Florean Fortescue's  D) The Three Broomsticks

17. **(Magic)** Which phrase activates the Marauder's Map?
    A) "Mischief Managed"  B) "Dissendium"  C) "I solemnly swear that I am up to no good"  D) "Alohomora"

18. **(Plot) (Open)** Ron's pet rat, Scabbers, is revealed in the climax to actually be which person?

19. **(Places)** What is the name of the all-wizarding village near Hogwarts that third-years may visit at weekends?
    A) Godric's Hollow  B) Hogsmeade  C) Diagon Alley  D) Little Whinging

20. **(Quidditch)** Who captains the Gryffindor Quidditch team and plays as Keeper?
    A) Oliver Wood  B) Fred Weasley  C) Cedric Diggory  D) Marcus Flint

21. **(Magic)** What charm turns a shape-shifting boggart into something funny, defeating it?
    A) Waddiwasi  B) Riddikulus  C) Expelliarmus  D) Impervius

22. **(Plot)** What broomstick does Harry receive anonymously for Christmas?
    A) The Nimbus Two Thousand  B) The Cleansweep Seven  C) The Comet Two Sixty  D) The Firebolt

23. **(Places)** What train carries students from King's Cross to Hogwarts?
    A) The Knight Bus  B) The Orient Express  C) The Hogwarts Express  D) The Floo Network

24. **(Numbers)** How many houses are students sorted into at Hogwarts?
    A) Three  B) Four  C) Five  D) Six

25. **(Food/Sweets)** What is the name of the Hogsmeade pub famous for its butterbeer?
    A) The Leaky Cauldron  B) The Hog's Head  C) Honeydukes  D) The Three Broomsticks

26. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Hagrid's loyal boarhound dog?

27. **(Quidditch)** What are the heavy black balls that try to knock Quidditch players off their brooms called?
    A) Bludgers  B) Quaffles  C) Snitches  D) Beaters

28. **(Magic)** What magical object, inherited from his father, lets Harry sneak around Hogwarts unseen?
    A) The Marauder's Map  B) A Disillusionment potion  C) A Vanishing Cabinet  D) The Invisibility Cloak

29. **(Numbers)** What is the platform number at King's Cross that the Hogwarts Express departs from?
    A) Twelve and a half  B) Seven and a quarter  C) Nine and three-quarters  D) Ten and three-quarters

30. **(Food/Sweets)** Which classic Honeydukes candy famously comes in every flavor, including unpleasant ones?
    A) Every Flavor Beans  B) Fizzing Whizbees  C) Ice Mice  D) Sugar Quills

31. **(Plot)** Which of these events happens LAST in the book?
    A) Aunt Marge is inflated like a balloon  B) Harry receives the Firebolt  C) Sirius Black is freed from the tower  D) Buckbeak loses his appeal

32. **(Characters)** Who is revealed, in the book's climax, to be Harry's godfather?
    A) Remus Lupin  B) Peter Pettigrew  C) Cornelius Fudge  D) Sirius Black
