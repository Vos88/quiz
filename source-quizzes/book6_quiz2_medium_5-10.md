# Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince — Pub Quiz 2 (Difficulty 5/10)

*28 questions, an even mix of multiple choice and open answer (medium tier = ~50% MC), plus one tiebreaker.*

1. **(Magic)** What spell does Hermione cast on Harry's secondhand Potions book, hoping to reveal hidden enchantments (it shows nothing unusual)?
   A) Priori Incantatem  B) Finite Incantatem  C) Specialis Revelio  D) Revelio

2. **(Characters)** Who is Ron's rival for the Gryffindor Keeper position, later responsible for mishitting a Bludger into Harry's head?
   A) Cormac McLaggen  B) Dean Thomas  C) Ritchie Coote  D) Jimmy Peakes

3. **(Magic) (Open)** What unknown, handwritten spell does Harry use against Draco Malfoy during their bathroom duel, slashing him badly?

4. **(Places)** In which Knockturn Alley shop does Draco Malfoy demand Mr. Borgin repair an object that "has to stay put"?
   A) Flourish and Blotts  B) Borgin and Burkes  C) Dervish and Banges  D) Zonko's

5. **(Characters) (Open)** Name the house-elf, inherited from Sirius Black, who at first refuses to serve Harry.

6. **(Numbers)** According to Dumbledore, how many Horcruxes did Voldemort create in total?
   A) Six  B) Seven  C) Five  D) Four

7. **(Creatures) (Open)** What are the reanimated corpses that guard the underground lake in the Horcrux cave called?

8. **(Quidditch)** What was the final score when Gryffindor beat Ravenclaw to win the Quidditch Cup?
   A) 320–60  B) 450–140  C) 170–60  D) 500–100

9. **(Quidditch) (Open)** Name all three Chasers on Harry's Gryffindor Quidditch team this season.

10. **(Magic)** Which potion does Harry brew to perfection in his first N.E.W.T. Potions class, using the Prince's trick of crushing rather than cutting the sopophorous bean?
    A) Polyjuice Potion  B) Amortentia  C) Veritaserum  D) Draught of Living Death

11. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What chocolate sweets, originally meant for Harry and laced with a love potion, does Ron eat by accident on his birthday?

12. **(Characters) (Open)** Who is revealed, at the book's climax, to be the true inventor of Sectumsempra and the real "Half-Blood Prince"?

13. **(Places) (Open)** Where do Dumbledore and Harry travel to retrieve a Horcrux, finding only a fake locket left behind?

14. **(Numbers) (Open)** How many Galleons did Caractacus Burke pay Merope Gaunt for Slytherin's locket?

15. **(Quidditch)** Who takes over as Seeker for Gryffindor's final match, since Harry is serving detention?
    A) Katie Bell  B) Demelza Robins  C) Ginny Weasley  D) Dean Thomas

16. **(Magic)** What is the name of Snape's invented spell that muffles sound, letting Harry and his friends talk privately?
    A) Silencio  B) Obliviate  C) Langlock  D) Muffliato

17. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Hagrid's giant half-brother, relocated by Dumbledore to a mountain cave?

18. **(Plot)** What is Harry repeatedly tasked with retrieving from Professor Slughorn throughout the book?
    A) A tampered memory about Horcruxes  B) A hidden Horcrux itself  C) A secret potion recipe  D) A list of Death Eaters

19. **(Numbers)** How many O.W.L.s did Ron receive, as revealed early in the book — more than Fred and George's combined?
    A) Five  B) Nine  C) Ten  D) Seven

20. **(Food/Sweets) (Open)** What is Dumbledore's favorite jam flavor, used as the answer to the Prime Minister's security question?

21. **(Places)** At which Diagon Alley shop do Harry, Ron, and Hermione run into Draco and Narcissa Malfoy getting robes fitted?
    A) Twilfitt and Tatting's  B) Madam Malkin's Robes for All Occasions  C) Gladrags Wizardwear  D) Flourish and Blotts

22. **(Creatures) (Open)** What is the name of Voldemort's snake, theorized by Dumbledore to be his final Horcrux?

23. **(Plot)** What does Draco Malfoy repair and use, via a pair of linked magical wardrobes, to smuggle Death Eaters into Hogwarts?
    A) A stolen Portkey  B) The Floo Network  C) A pair of linked Vanishing Cabinets  D) A hidden tunnel from Honeydukes

24. **(Plot)** What does Mr. Weasley's new Ministry department investigate and confiscate, amid a flood of fake protective items?
    A) Illegal broomstick imports  B) Counterfeit defensive spells and protective objects  C) Banned joke-shop products  D) Unregistered Animagi

25. **(Plot) (Open)** Whose name does Snape find written inside the substitute Potions textbook Harry hands over after the Sectumsempra incident?

26. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which senior Ministry official, Head of the Department of Magical Law Enforcement, is revealed to have been murdered, likely personally by Voldemort, early in the book?

27. **(Deaths) (Open)** Which wealthy, elderly witch dies by poisoning two days after showing a young Voldemort her two prized treasures?

28. **(Deaths)** Which savage werewolf is rumored to have killed the Montgomery sisters' five-year-old brother partway through the book?
    A) Fenrir Greyback  B) Remus Lupin  C) Severus Snape  D) Antonin Dolohov
